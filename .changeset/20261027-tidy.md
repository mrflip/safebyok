**Public names change: `useBankVault` is `useListedVault`, a key-value row is named by its `scopepath`, and `Gate.of` takes the vault record alone; `bank.vaults`, `bank.lifecycle` and `vault.stamp` are new; the core's IndexedDB options refuse a field they do not take, and `VaultReadResponse.vault` is `unknown`.** Nothing stored changes. What a host changes:

* **safebyok-react**: `useBankVault(vaultlabel)` is `useListedVault(vaultlabel)`, the same hook: the vault as the bank lists it, `starting`, `ready` or `refused` with its `err`.
* **`StringStore.ckeys()` is `StringStore.scopepaths()`**: a StringStore of your own renames the method; `memoryStore()` and `domStore()` have it. Its `read`, `write` and `erase` take the same text as before, named a scopepath.
* **`IdbRow.ckey` is `IdbRow.scopepath`**, in both inspectors' `rows()`: `inspectIdbDeviceDB` (`safebyok/idb`) and safebyok-indexeddb's `inspectIdbVaultDB`.
* **`Gate.of(vaultRecord, kit)`** takes no Vault object: the gate's scope and `vault_tok` are the record's own, where the first argument repeated them.
* **`vault.stamp`** (new): the Vault object's own fields, its stamp, as one frozen plain object, for a spread, a copy or a log line, in place of `{ ...vault }`, which spreads a class instance.
* **`bank.vaults` and `bank.lifecycle`** (new): the bank's listing and where it stands, read from the current snapshot. Outside React, read these rather than `bank.snapshot.vaults` and `bank.snapshot.lifecycle`; a React component still renders from the snapshot.
* **IndexedDB options are strict**: `browserOpts`, `idbDeviceDB` and `inspectIdbDeviceDB` refuse an option they do not take (`{ dbname: 'acme' }`, misspelt) as 'bad_option', as safebyok-indexeddb's `idbVaultDB` already did, where they ignored it and used 'safebyok'. safebyok-indexeddb takes its default from the core's.
* **`VaultReadResponse.vault` is `unknown`**, as `StoredBox.box` is: an adapter of your own drops its `as VaultReadResponse['vault']`. The base class checks it as before.
* A passphrase handed to a gating's attempt as anything but text is refused "A passphrase is the text the user typed, as a string." again, with its full stop.
