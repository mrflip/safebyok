**Every schema is classic Zod, made in a Validator call, and stored records are strict.** `zod/mini` is gone: importing safebyok loads classic Zod (the `zod` peer, ^4.6.7), which sets Zod's global locale to English as it loads; a host's own `z.config` still wins for its own schemas, and safebyok's refusals say Zod's English whatever it is set to. The exported schemas (`BoxRecord`, `VaultProps`, `GatedVaultProps`, `GatelessVaultProps`, `PassphraseWayinRecord`, `PasskeyWayinRecord`, `WayinRecord`, `Tombstone`, `LeaseRecord`, `DeviceRecord`, `Ciphered`, `BoxSummary`, `Capacity`, `VaultScope`, `Whisper`, `Sidecar`) are classic Zod: `parse` throws a VaultError naming each failing field, never a ZodError (a record 'bad_record', `Whisper` 'bad_whisper', `Sidecar` 'bad_option', `VaultScope` 'bad_scope'), and `safeParse` is Zod's own; `EpochMillis`, `label` and `user_id` stay plain Zod. A stored vault, box, lease or device record, wayin or tombstone holding a field this version does not know now reads as damaged (`damaged_vault`, `damaged_box`, a lease or device record as absent), where the field was dropped; a VaultDB verb handed one refuses it ('bad_record', a box 'invalid_box') where it stored the rest. A refusal of a record read back carries the check's own 'bad_record' as its `cause`, naming each failing field in its message and `meta.fieldnames`, where it carried Zod's error. An unknown option (`hello({ staleMinute })`, `putBox()`'s props, `Bank.make`, a draft) is refused as "There is no field staleMinute: check its spelling, or leave it out", where it read `Unrecognized key`, and a refusal repeats a key only when it is shaped as one of ours (snake_case or camelCase, 32 characters at most): a key shaped like a credential is never repeated. An empty user_id is refused in one sentence. Sizes, brotlied: `mask` from `safebyok/utils` now carries classic Zod (about 25 kB, from 6), as does fastkey's `argon2idWasmDeriver` (35, from 16); `idbDeviceDB` and `browserOpts` grow about 5 kB; `Bank` is unchanged.
