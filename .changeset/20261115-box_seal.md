Empty, as the new `safebyok` is private. What a host of `safebyok` will see: a box is a Thing, sealed and unsealed by `bank.sealBox`, `bank.tombstoneBox` and `bank.unsealBox`. Its whisper is sealed at `whisper_sealed`, `{ key_wrapped, ciphered, codectag, schematag }`, and the seal binds the bank's stamp, the box's Thing fields and those two tags, never its sidecar. A deleted box is a tombstone signed by a sealed empty whisper, so a delete needs the bank unlocked, and `bank.unsealBox` refuses a tombstone made without the bankKey as `damaged_whisper`. A box stored in safebyok-big's shape, or sealed under its ctx, fails safe, since nothing reads older shapes before 1.0: it refuses the box's check as `bad_option`, or its unseal as `damaged_whisper`.
