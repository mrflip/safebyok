Empty: the new `safebyok` is private, and `safebyok-postgres` exports nothing new. What a host of `safebyok` will see: the asks a caller drafts, defaulted fields optional, are drafts by name: `GetAskDraft`, `ListAskDraft`, `PutAskDraft`, `DelAskDraft`, `BankAskDraft` and `GetBoxAskDraft` (were `GetAsk`, `ListAsk`, `PutAsk`, `DelAsk`, `BankAsk` and `GetBoxAsk`); the whole asks a vaultDB takes are `GetAsk`, `ListAsk` and `WriteAsk<RecordT>` (was `GetThingAsk`, `ListThingsAsk`, `WriteThingAsk` and `WriteAskOf`), checked by `checkedGetAsk`, `checkedListAsk` and `checkedWriteAsk`. `AnyAsk` names any of them, or a birth. For a store: `unwrittenWriteOf(raw, { ask, act, world })` takes an `UnwrittenWrite`, and `demandBornAtKept(ask, raw)` no longer takes the Thing stored. `Proving.judgeStrength(device, scope, passphraseCleartext)` takes the device first, as do `PassphraseProving.buildNovel(device, bank, gate, passphraseCleartext)`, `PasskeyProving.buildNovel(device, bank, gate, passkeyCleartext)` and `PassphraseProving.buildStored(device, gate, passphraseCleartext)`.
