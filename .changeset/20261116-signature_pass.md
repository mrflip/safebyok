Empty: the new `safebyok` is private, and `safebyok-postgres` exports nothing new. What a host of `safebyok` will see: the asks a caller drafts, defaulted fields optional, are drafts by name: `GetAskDraft`, `ListAskDraft`, `PutAskDraft`, `DelAskDraft`, `BankAskDraft` and `GetBoxAskDraft`; the whole asks a vaultDB takes are `GetAsk`, `ListAsk` and `WriteAsk<RecordT>` (was `GetThingAsk`, `ListThingsAsk`, `WriteThingAsk` and `WriteAskOf`), checked by `checkedGetAsk`, `checkedListAsk` and `checkedWriteAsk`. `AnyAsk` names any of them, or a birth. `tryBirthBank` answers in the batch answer's shape: `ok: true` with `act: 'born'` where it wrote all three roots, or `act: 'noop'` where a root's put refused and it wrote nothing, and `val` holding each slot's answer under `bank`, `gate` and `box`, each the answer that root's put would give alone, less `world` and `tracked`, which the birth answers once; a refused slot carries its `dbhas` and its `err`. `ok: false` now means only that the step itself failed, and carries no `dbhas`. A birth over another's live bank answers `noop` with every slot `stale` and the root stored as its `dbhas`, where it answered `passed`; one over a live bank stored without its main gate answers `noop`, where it refused `storage_issue`. `Bank.birth` still refuses `gate_made_elsewhere` over another's live bank, and otherwise throws the first refused slot's refusal. For a store: `doBirthBank(ask)` takes the ask alone, `classifyBirthBank(ask, fetched)` and `unwrittenBirthOf(ask, fetched)` take it in place of the puts, `writtenBirthOf(ask, written)` replaces `writtenRootsOf`, and `birthPutsOf` is exported; `unwrittenWriteOf(raw, { ask, act, world })` takes an `UnwrittenWrite`, and `demandBornAtKept(ask, raw)` no longer takes the Thing stored. `Proving.judgeStrength(device, scope, passphraseCleartext)` takes the device first.
