Empty: the new `safebyok` is private at 0.0.0, and `safebyok-big` is untouched. What a caller of the new core will see: **three device options**, `softlockMinutes` (default 10, capped at a twentieth of the lease, none under a minute), `janitorMinutes` (default 10) and `gateStaleMinutes` (default 720), which `GiverConfig` carries too. **`isSoftlocked`** on the world, bank and gate: true while locked, or from `softlockMillis` before the lease ends; `ensureUnlocked` then waits for an `unlock` gating though the bank shows unlocked, and that unlock renews the lease. **The janitor**, a timer that runs while the device is connected and signed in, locks the bank at its first tick past the lease's end, and re-reads the gate, bank and root box from the vaultDB once this tab last read the gate more than `gateStaleMinutes` ago; what it trips on goes to `onBadNews`. **A seal under way** when its lease is evicted or its device ends now finishes; an unseal under way still refuses. **Stored format:** unchanged.
