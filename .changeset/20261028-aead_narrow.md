A new refusal kind, `crypto_failed` (a condition): the browser's WebCrypto failed as a box was sealed -- the boxKey made, wrapped under the vaultKey, or the whisper ciphered -- for no reason this package can act on. A fault of the platform, not of the user or the record; nothing was stored, the vault stays unlocked, and the message says to try again. `Lease.cipheredWith` and the calls over it (`sealProps`, `Box.seal`, `vault.putBox`) refuse it where they threw 'unexpected_error' before. Nothing of WebCrypto's error is kept, not as a `cause` (it was handed key material), but its `name` (`OperationError`, say) ends the message. A host switching on `refusalkind` may now meet it from a box write.

A failure of the device store (a `DeviceDB`) no longer carries the store's own error as the condition's `cause`: 'storage_blocked' and 'storage_unavailable' from `getLease`, `putLease`, `delLease`, `getDeviceRecord`, `raiseDeviceRecord`, `delDeviceRecord` and `delAll` keep neither the throw nor its message, since a device store is handed every lease record and its error may quote one, and the logger hands a condition to the host whole. Only the error's `name` is kept, as the last sentence of the message ("The device store threw QuotaExceededError."), in place of the old "The device store's error is this error's cause." A host that read `err.cause` off one of these sees `undefined`. A `VaultDB` adapter's 'storage_unavailable' still carries its cause, as it holds only ciphertext.

The Lease and Box throwing forms (`Lease.cipheredWith`, `Lease.decipherWith`, `Lease.sealProps`, `Box.seal`, `box.extractWhisper`) now end in one catch that surfaces any throw that is no `VaultError` (a bug) as 'unexpected_error', the throw its cause, so none leaves raw; every refusal passes through unchanged. Inside them each WebCrypto catch holds only WebCrypto calls, their ctx built beforehand, so a vault stamp that fails its check refuses 'bad_record' where it read as 'damaged_lease' (and locked the vault) or as a wrong proof; a 'crypto_failed' from the cipher step throws before the post-wait consistency demand runs.
