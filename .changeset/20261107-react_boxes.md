"safebyok-react": minor

safebyok-react adds the box hooks useBoxes, useBox, useBoxSnapshot and useWhisper; BoxWhisperField, a password field bound to one leaf of a box's whisper through BoxFields, whose value never passes through React; and VaultLockControl and CreateVaultButton, for an account menu to show the lock state, lock, unlock or create a vault from a click.
