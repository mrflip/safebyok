Empty: the new `safebyok` is private, and `safebyok-big` is untouched. What a caller of the new core will see:

- **`vault.tryPutBoxOnce` and `vault.tryDelBoxOnce`** resolve an `Outcome`: the box or tombstone as stored, or the refusal, misuse and a refusal met before the write (`bank_locked`, a missing sidecar) included, as its bad outcome. A `stale_write` answers as the minder's `tryWrite` does, with the revision stored. `putBoxOnce` and `delBoxOnce` throw the refusal instead.
- **A put over a box known only from a list** writes over what the list cached: given a labelpath whose car the minder holds sparse, a patch with no sidecar refuses `bad_record` again, where it fetched the box whole first.
- **`LeaseFormat` is a Thing's `fmt`, the string `'a010501'`**, where it was the number `7102`. A lease is never stored: it lives in memory, and its `fmt` is bound into the wrap of its bankKey, so nothing a host has stored changes.
