**The gate's prompts are the bank's.** `bank.maingate.replacePassphrasePrompt()`, `addPasskeyPrompt()` and `revokePasskeyPrompt()`, each with its `try` form, ask the user through `requestCleartext` and change the bank's main gate. Each opens its gating, `replace_passphrase`, `add_passkey` or `revoke_passkey`, with the cleartexts as before, and leaves this device unlocked, its lease citing the gate row written; a device or session already unlocked stays unlocked as it hears the change. **`bank.delPrompt()` and `bank.tryDelPrompt()` delete the bank**, with its gate, vaults and boxes, once the user types the passphrase into a gating of the new kind `del_bank` (safebyok-react's provider shows `DelBankModal` for it). **`bank.prompt(gatingkind)`** opens any bank gating. **`device.bank` is null once its bank is deleted, or let go for a newer life of it** built elsewhere; the next `ensureBank` brings up the bank listed. **`listing.deliverNotice(notice)`** delivers a watch notice to a listing, which keeps the rows of its own kind: the bank's one watch now delivers bank and gate rows to their listings, so a gate changed on another device reaches this one as it is written. In safebyok-react the gate dialogs name the bank, not the vault, in the password manager's username (`u1 (acme vault)`), and the device-DB inspector shows a lease by its `bank_tok`. **Stored format:** unchanged.
