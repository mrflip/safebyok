safebyok-postgres now runs on the new `safebyok` core: `PostgresVaultDB`, a vaultDB, with `VaultTablesSql`, `VaultFunctionsSql` and `thingsChannelOf`, replaces `PostgresBankDB`, `pgClientOf`, `BankTablesSql`, `BankFunctionsSql` and `bankChannelOf`. Its three tables (`safebyok_bank_things`, `safebyok_gate_things`, `safebyok_box_things`) are new, and it reads nothing the earlier tables hold: each user's bank reads as absent. `client` needs only `query`, so a `pg` Pool goes in as it is. The package ships no `.sql` files. The earlier adapter lives on, private and frozen, as safebyok-postgres-big.
