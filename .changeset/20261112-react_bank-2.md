The Vault's scaffold calls of its bank's lock and gate are gone: isLocked, isUnlocked, lock, ensureUnlocked, unlock, and the replacePassphrasePrompt, addPasskeyPrompt and revokePasskeyPrompt prompts with their try forms. Call them on the bank (bank.isLocked, bank.lock(), bank.ensureUnlocked()) and on its main gate (bank.maingate.replacePassphrasePrompt()). VaultSnapshot loses isLocked; read the bank's snapshot. A thing's alignLock() is named lock(): on a Vault or a Box it locks nothing, and only brings the thing up to its bank's lock, so a host that called vault.lock() to lock now calls bank.lock().
