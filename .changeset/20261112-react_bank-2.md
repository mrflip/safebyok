The Vault's scaffold calls of its bank's lock and gate are gone: isLocked, isUnlocked, lock, ensureUnlocked, unlock, and the replacePassphrasePrompt, addPasskeyPrompt and revokePasskeyPrompt prompts with their try forms. Call them on the bank (bank.isLocked, bank.lock(), bank.ensureUnlocked()) and on its main gate (bank.maingate.replacePassphrasePrompt()). A host that called vault.lock() to lock now calls bank.lock(); vault.lock() and box.lock() no longer compile. VaultSnapshot loses isLocked; read the bank's snapshot.
