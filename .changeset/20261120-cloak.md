Empty: the giver is on no published entry, and safebyok is private. In the new core, `cloak({ ...cleartexts })` adopts a form's cleartexts into a `Cloak`: each is read through its getter (a passkey answer as a fresh copy), the cloak serializes, prints and spreads as `'[cloaked]'`, adopting a passkey answer zeroes the host's PRF bytes, and `cloak.vacate()` zeroes the cloak's bytes and drops its passphrases. In safebyok-react's giver, each form mutation (`useUnlock()` and the rest, and their `…MutationOptions()`) now takes a `Cloak` in place of the cleartexts object, builds the verb's cleartexts from it at each attempt, and vacates it once the mutation settles, so React Query's mutation cache, devtools and hook state hold no cleartext. A host writes `unlock.mutate(cloak({ passphraseClear }))`. The form mutations never retry, whatever a host's mutation defaults say. An `onSettled` in a host's own options for a form mutation replaces the giver's vacate, so it should vacate the cloak itself, and an `onSettled` in its QueryClient's mutation defaults no longer runs for a form mutation. The core's form verbs are now said to vacate their cleartexts, where they were said to spend them; nothing they do changed.
