**Stored format: the vault record nests its gate, and every vault stored before reads as damaged.** The vault props become the vault record, `VaultRecord`: `{ format, applabel, user_id, vaultlabel, vault_tok, registered_at, vault_epoch, gate_epoch, gate }`, where `gate` is `{ passphrase, passkey }` (a `GateRecord`, its passkey null when there is none), and `gate` is null while the vault is novel; `gate_epoch` is 0 exactly when `gate` is null, which the record's check holds. The format stays `safebyok-vault-v6`, and no ctx changes: each wrap binds what it bound before, so a vault written now differs from one written before only in its record's layout. A vault stored before, its `passphrase` and `passkey` flat beside its fields, fails the strict check and reads as damaged (`damaged_vault`), as no shim reads it: `delDamagedVault` clears it, and the user makes the vault anew. A refusal of a malformed record now names its failing field, where the union of a gated and a gateless shape it replaces said only "Invalid input". Convex refuses to deploy a schema its documents do not match, so clear the component's tables before deploying; Postgres keeps its table as it was (each wayin a `jsonb` column, `gate_epoch` a column), so its rows read as before.

**Exports.** New: `VaultRecord`, `GateRecord`, and the shapes sliced from the record, each a check and a type: `VaultSparse` (the record without its gate), `VaultStamp` (its format, scope, `vault_tok` and `registered_at`), `VaultTombstone` (was `Tombstone`), `GateSparse`, `PassphraseWayinSparse` and `PasskeyWayinSparse` (each wayin without its wrap, and the passphrase's without its salt and key deriver); `VaultFormat`, `isGated` (now of a `VaultRecord`, narrowing its `gate`), `BoxStamp`, `LeaseStamp` and the type `LeaseSparse` (was `LeaseView`), and `deviceRecordOf(scope, device_id, highest_vault_epoch)`, which builds a device record from a scope's names alone. Gone: `VaultProps`, `GatedVaultProps`, `GatelessVaultProps` (a `VaultRecord`, and an `ensureVault` draft `VaultRecord & { gate: null }`), `Tombstone`, `VaultPropsExpectation` (now `VaultRecordExpectation`), `Stamp` (now `VaultSparse`, which `vault.stamp` and `Box.make` take), `Stamped` (`demandConsistentWith` takes `Pick<VaultSparse, 'vault_tok'> & Partial<VaultSparse>`, as before in all but name), `LeaseStanding` and `LeaseView`. `VaultScope` is one type, the frozen scope the checks hand out. `vault.stamp` now holds the record's `format` and `registered_at` too. A device record is handed out frozen. A `VaultDB` adapter's `doPutVaultProps` and `doEnsureVault` take the record with its gate nested; `putVaultProps` and the `vault_props_stored` of its refusal keep their names. `safebyok/testing`'s `vaultPropsOf` and `propsOf` are `vaultRecordOf` and `vaultRecordIn`, beside a new `gateOf`. A damaged wayin's refusal now carries the check's own `bad_record` as its cause, never a raw ZodError. In GraphQL, `SafebyokVaultProps` and its input are `SafebyokVaultRecord` and `SafebyokVaultRecordInput`, holding a `gate: SafebyokGate` (`SafebyokGateInput`), its expectation input `SafebyokVaultRecordExpectationInput`, and `SafebyokTombstone` is `SafebyokVaultTombstone`: deploy the server and the browsers together. In Convex, the validators `VaultProps`, `GatelessVaultProps` and `Tombstone` are `VaultRecord`, `NovelVaultRecord` and `VaultTombstone`, beside a new `Gate`, and `VaultPropsExpectation` is `VaultRecordExpectation`.
