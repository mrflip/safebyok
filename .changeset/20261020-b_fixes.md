A strength checker's verdict whose fields throw as they are read is now refused as unexpected_error carrying nothing of the throw, as a throw from the checker itself already was. An unlock or passphrase change outrun by del() (or by the vault props moving on) now erases the lease it just wrote, so no lease outlives the vault it opened.

`JSON.stringify` of a `Wayin` now gives its `summary` (never its wrap, nor a passphrase's salt; a passkey's summary keeps the prf_salt_b64 it is asked with), and of a `Lease` every field but its leaseKey and wrapped vaultKey, where each gave its stored record: a host that logged one, or used `toJSON()` to copy a record, reads `wayin.props` or `lease.props` instead.
