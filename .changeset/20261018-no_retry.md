store() no longer tries again when another device or tab wrote, created or erased the lockbox first: it makes one attempt, and on a refusal catches up with storage once and throws stale_write at once, without waiting -- nothing was written, and the snapshot shows the lockbox as storage now holds it. A host that relied on store() absorbing a concurrent write now sees stale_write and decides whether to store again; a store that loses no longer waits up to about six seconds before saying so. No public option or export is removed: the retry's limit and waits were never public.
