**`getBox` hands back the `Box`; `getWhisper` reads the whisper.** `vault.getBox(boxlabel)` now resolves the sealed `Box` (refusing `absent_box` as before), and what it used to answer, the whisper, is `vault.getWhisper(boxlabel)`, now with a `tryGetWhisper` form. `openBox` and `tryOpenBox` are `decryptWhisper` and `tryDecryptWhisper`, and `useBox` is `useWhisper`: a host calling the old names, or reading a whisper from `getBox`, changes them.

**Calls are plain methods, no longer bound.** Every `x` and `tryX` is a method of the `Vault` class. A call taken out of the vault -- `const { unlock } = vault`, or `onClick={vault.lock}` -- loses its `this`: call it on the vault, or wrap it, `() => vault.tryLock()`. `subscribe` and `getSnapshot` stay bound, for `useSyncExternalStore`. No stored shape changed.

**A broken strength checker is refused like any other call, not thrown.** `ThrownMistake` is gone: a `strengthChecker` that throws or answers no verdict is refused `unexpected_error` or `bad_option`, carrying nothing it heard, by `tryUnlock`, `tryPutWayin`, `ask.attempt` and the rest as a bad `Outcome`, and by their throwing forms as a thrown `VaultError`. `PassphraseModal` meets it with its "Something went wrong" notice and reports the refusal to React, as it does any misuse, instead of throwing it to the app's error boundary.
