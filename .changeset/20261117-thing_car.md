Empty: the new `safebyok` is private. What a caller of a `ThingMinder` will see: the minder caches a `ThingCar` for each scope, never a bare record. A car (`ThingCar<RecordT>`, by kind `BoxCar` and `GateCar`, frozen) holds `newest`, the newest revision heard of, which may be sparse, and `fullest`, the newest whole record seen, so that a host shows `fullest` and writes against `newest`; its `liveness` is `full`, `behind`, `bare`, `gone` or `void`, and `fullest` is null when gone or void and undefined when bare. `at(scoped)` and `get(ask)` return the car; the answers of `tryAt` and `listen` carry it as `car`, beside `val`, which a timely answer still fills with the whole record, the tombstone or null. A car that is `behind` or `bare` is never timely, so the next get fetches the record whole: a box that `list()` lists sparse lands in a `bare` car, or a `behind` one that keeps showing the whole record held before, and a `listen` listener hears it once, untimely (the wontfix row for the timely-then-untimely notice is gone). A tombstone leaves no whole record, and neither does a later life heard sparse. A box deleted elsewhere and listed by `list()` as a sparse tombstone lands `gone`, and is timely: `get` and `tryAt` answer it `gotgone` from the cache, where before it was invalidated and the next get fetched it whole. A get of a box that storage does not hold answers its shell under the bank's `rootBox`, whether or not the root box's car is cached. A box minder caches its bank's live root box `bare`, and caches nothing for the root box before the bank's birth. `isWholeRecordAt(scoped)` is removed: read `at(scoped)?.liveness`. A host's own `QueryObserver` on a minder's key sees cars, and its `staleTime` default is now a function. `ThingCar`, `BoxCar`, `GateCar`, `Tombstone`, `DeliverableThing`, `carLivenessVals`, `carOf` and `isVaultDBNewer` are exported from `safebyok`. `carOf` refuses `bad_option` for anything but a Thing's record, sparse or whole, or the void sentinel; `isVaultDBNewer` reads only a car's `liveness`.
