Empty, as the new `safebyok` is private. What a host of `safebyok` will see: `Gate` and `Bank` stand on a shared `BaseThing`, and show every Thing field as a read-only prop. A bank is born in one `vaultDB.tryBirthBank` call that stores the bank, its main gate and its root box, the root box's first revision sealed under the new lease; `bank.put({ cited, record })` writes the bank's next revision. A gate's wayins always carry `passkey0`, null for none; the gate's stamp is `{ userid, banklabel, labelpath, lifeseq, born_at }`, and the lease's record is fmt 7102, naming its gate by `gatelabelpath`, `gate_lifeseq` and `gate_revtok`. Gates, wayins and leases stored before fail safe, since nothing reads older shapes before 1.0: a gate without `passkey0` and a lease of fmt 7101 fail their checks, and a wayin no longer unwraps the bankKey. `lease.isEnabled` and `lease.demandEnabled()` follow the device too: a sweep refuses a seal or unseal, even one under way, with the device's own `disabled` refusal, which every disabled answer of a bank now shares.
