Empty: safebyok-react-lean is private, and nothing published changes. What a host of the React layer will see: the parts' entry exports **four field hooks**, each the behaviour of a part without its markup, for a host that brings its own elements. **`usePassphraseField(opts)`** answers `inputProps` and `toggleProps` to spread onto an uncontrolled `<input>` and a `<button>`, with `isRevealed` and `isCapsLockOn`; **`useWhisperField(opts)`** does the same for a whisper's field, with the attributes that keep password managers away. **`useWhisperText(labelpath, field, opts)`** answers `textRef`, `isRevealed`, `isRevealing` and `toggleProps`: Show writes the field into the element `textRef` is given, never into React's state, and Hide, a lock or another box or field empties it. **`useWhisperMask(labelpath, field, opts)`** answers `maskRef`, the element a mask is written into. Destructure what `useWhisperText` and `useWhisperMask` answer: React's lint takes a read of an object holding a ref for a read of the ref. `PassphraseInput`, `WhisperInput`, `WhisperText` and `WhisperMask` are now markup over those hooks, and render as before. **`WhisperMask` no longer goes blank for a moment while locked**: the stored mask shows from the moment the bank locks, and an unlock keeps it showing until the live one lands. **`WhisperInput` takes `isPending`**, as `PassphraseInput` does. **`useNewPassphrase()`** answers **`verdict`**, the strength checker's verdict on the new passphrase unworded (`{ strong: true }` or `{ strong: false, reason }`, null while the field is empty and before the first verdict), and **`match`** (`untyped`, `differs` or `matches`), for words of a host's own beside `verdictText` and `matchText`. Types exported beside them: `PassphraseFieldOpts`, `PassphraseField`, `PassphraseFieldInputProps`, `WhisperFieldOpts`, `WhisperField`, `WhisperFieldInputProps`, `WhisperTextOpts`, `WhisperTextResult`, `WhisperTextToggleProps`, `WhisperMaskOpts`, `WhisperMaskResult`, `RevealToggleOpts`, `RevealToggleProps`, and from the kit `PassphraseMatch` and `PassphraseVerdict`. `WhisperTextProps` and `WhisperMaskProps` are type aliases now, not interfaces. **Stored format:** unchanged.
