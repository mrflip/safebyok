Empty: the new `safebyok` is private, and `safebyok-big` is untouched. What a caller of the new core will see: **a `Vault` writes a bank's boxes.** `Vault.make(bank)` makes the vault of a bank's boxes with a `ThingMinder` of its own (call `vault.minder.hello()` to start it), and it is on the front door, `safebyok`, not on `safebyok/core`. `vault.putBoxOnce(cited, patch, secrets)` seals the next revision over `cited`, a box or its labelpath (then over the newest revision the minder has, getting it when nothing is cached), writes it once through the minder, and resolves the box as stored, already in its car and its parent's listing; it throws the write's refusal, `stale_write` included. `vault.delBoxOnce(labelpath)` writes the tombstone over the newest revision, and the box's car is `gone`. **The bank's box verbs are gone:** `bank.tryGetBox`, `bank.tryListBoxes`, `bank.tryPutBox` and `bank.tryDelBox`, with `GetBoxAskDraft`, `GetBoxAnswer`, the `otherThanBox` thrower and its phrase; get and list a box through the minder. **`bank.sealBox` and `bank.tombstoneBox` resolve the ask that writes the sealed box** (a `SealedBoxAsk`, newly exported: a put of a live box, a del of a tombstone), where they resolved the box itself; the box is its `record`, and it cites the donor sparse, without its whisper. `bank.vaultDB` is a new getter. **Stored format:** unchanged.
