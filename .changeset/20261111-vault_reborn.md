**The refusal kind `vault_mismatch` is renamed `vault_reborn`**, and the thrower `vaultMismatch` exported from `safebyok/storage` is `vaultReborn`. It is still a condition: a live vault stands under another `vault_tok` than the call named, and the app reads the vault again by its scope. A host that names `vault_mismatch` renames it.
