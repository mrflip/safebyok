**`passphraseStrengthTest` is required, and `browserOpts()` brings a good one.** The core no longer offers a length-only test: `passphraseLengthTest` is gone from `safebyok`, and `openVault` refuses to open without a `passphraseStrengthTest` (`bad_option`), as it does without a `keyDeriver` or `deviceDB`. `browserOpts()` from `safebyok/browser` now supplies `zxcvbnPassphraseTest` beside the key deriver and device store; pass your own after the spread to replace it. `checkStrengthTest`, exported beside `checkValidators`, is the same check, for a wrapper that takes the options apart. **What a host will see:** one that spreads `browserOpts()` gets zxcvbn's test where it had the 12-character floor, so a common password of any length (`password1234`) is now refused at create, add and change; one that passes its own `keyDeriver` and `deviceDB` without `browserOpts()` must now pass a `passphraseStrengthTest` too, or `openVault` refuses with `bad_option`.

**A strength test hears the vault's scope.** It is called as `test(passphrase, scope)`, `scope` the vault's `{ applabel, user_id, vaultlabel }`, and `zxcvbnPassphraseTest` hands those names, whole and in parts (`ladyfern` and `zinnia` of `q7.ladyfern@zinnia.example`), to zxcvbn as the user's own words, so a passphrase built from them is refused. A one-argument test of a host's own still works.

**`zxcvbnPassphraseTest` loads its dictionary on demand, and answers through a promise.** zxcvbn and its dictionary (about 214 kB minified and brotlied) load by dynamic `import()` at the test's first call, as a chunk of their own in a bundled app: `browserOpts()` itself costs a page about 0.7 kB more, and a page that never chooses a passphrase never fetches the rest. A load that fails rejects that call, and the next tries again. `MinPassphraseChars` (12), its length floor, moves to `safebyok/browser` beside `MinZxcvbnScore`. `@zxcvbn-ts/core` and `@zxcvbn-ts/language-common` stay ordinary dependencies, since `browserOpts()` imports them.

**`VaultProvider` holds `passphraseStrengthTest` like any other option.** It checks it at render, throwing a missing one or one that is no function to the error boundary as `bad_option`, and the vault keeps it for its life: a test with a new identity reopens the vault, as a new `logger` or `clock` does, and is warned of once. Define it once, outside the component, or memoize it. **What a host will see:** an arrow written inline as `passphraseStrengthTest` now reopens the vault at every render, ending any dialog, where before it was taken up quietly.

**The dialog's advice defaults to the strength test.** With no `passphraseAdvice`, the passphrase dialog says the test's reason beside the field as the user chooses a weak passphrase, and nothing once it is strong enough. A host's own `passphraseAdvice` still wins.

**`withUnlockedVault` resolves null for an action the provider's own close cut short**, where it used to reject with `vault_closed`: an action, or its run again after an unlock, still under way when the provider unmounts, signs out, switches users or reopens. A `vault_closed` from a handle closed by anyone else still rejects.
