Empty: the new `safebyok` is private and the giver is not yet exported. What a caller of `ThingMinder` will see: `tryWrite(ask)` writes a put or del ask the bank made (`Bank.makePutAsk`, `Bank.makeDelAsk`) through the vaultDB's `tryWriteThing`, and resolves the vaultDB's `WriteThingAnswer` as it came, a refusal such as `stale_write` included, never throwing. Before it resolves, the write's own record is in the minder's cache: the record written (or, under `passed`, found), or a refusal's `dbhas`; so a listener has been told of its car by the time a caller's `await` returns, and after a `stale_write` the car's newest is the record the vaultDB reported. The bank, main gate and tracked Things the answer reports are cached only as the vaultDB's watch tells of them. Nothing of the ask is cached before the answer. A born box joins its parent's listing; a deleted box's car goes `gone`, and its tombstone stays listed. Before `hello`, after `goodbye`, or for an ask outside the minder's bank, it answers the refusal (`disabled`, `bad_record`) without asking the vaultDB. The minder-not-minding message now names writing as well as reading.
