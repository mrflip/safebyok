Empty: the new `safebyok` is private and the giver is not yet exported. What a caller of `ThingMinder` will see: `tryWrite(ask)` writes a put or del ask the bank made (`Bank.makePutAsk`, `Bank.makeDelAsk`) through the vaultDB's `tryWriteThing`, and resolves the vaultDB's `WriteThingAnswer` as it came, a refusal such as `stale_write` included, never throwing. Before it resolves, every record the answer carries is in the minder's cache: the record written (or, under `passed`, found), a refusal's `dbhas`, the bank and main gate of its `world`, and each Thing its `tracked` reports stored; so a listener has been told of each car by the time a caller's `await` returns, and after a `stale_write` the car's newest is the record the vaultDB reported. Nothing of the ask is cached before the answer. A born box joins its parent's listing; a deleted box's car goes `gone`, and its tombstone stays listed. Before `hello`, after `goodbye`, or for an ask outside the minder's bank, it answers the refusal (`disabled`, `bad_record`) without asking the vaultDB. The minder-not-minding message now names writing as well as reading.
