Empty: the new `safebyok` is private. What a caller will see: a Thing's record guards are statics on a class, and each answers for its class's kind. `isTombstone`, `isGone`, `isVoidSentinel`, `isWholeRecord`, `isSparse`, `cmpThings`, `haveSameScope` and `sparseOf` are no longer exported as functions: call `Thing.isTombstone(rec)`, `Thing.isGone(rec)`, `Thing.isVoidSentinel(rec)`, `Thing.isWhole(rec)`, `Thing.isSparse(rec)`, `Thing.cmp(ante, post)`, `Thing.haveSameScope(ante, post)` and `Thing.sparseOf(rec)` for any kind, or the guards on `Bank`, `Gate` or `BaseBox` for that kind alone (`Gate.isTombstone(rec)` is false of a box's tombstone, and narrows `rec` to a gate's). `Thing.isLive(rec)` is new. A guard reads its kind from the class it is called on, so pass one as `(rec) => Gate.isTombstone(rec)`, never detached. `safebyok`, `safebyok/core` and `safebyok/storage` export `BaseThing`, which holds the guards and the Thing fields, and `Thing`, the base of `Bank`, `Gate` and `BaseBox` (the old `BaseThing`); `safebyok/core` exports `BaseBox` in place of `CoreBox`, which is abstract and is still passed as the class itself to a lease's `seal` and `unseal`. `AnyThingRecord`, every field of every kind of Thing in one type, is new. `ShellOf<RecordT>` now pins the void revision (revseq -1, the void revtok and lifeseq), and `VoidSentinel` is `ShellOf<ThingRecord>`: a live record or a tombstone no longer type-checks as a shell. `safebyok` takes `type-fest` as a dependency. Nothing stored changes.
