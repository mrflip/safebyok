**A new entry, `safebyok/storage`, holds what a storage adapter is written against**: `VaultDB` and `DeviceDB` with their answer and `do*` types, `MemoryVaultDB` and `MemoryDeviceDB`, every storage shape and its checker (scopes, queries, records, rows, tombstones, sparses, options, the gate verbs' params, the lease shapes) with the helpers that read a row, and the throwers an adapter raises (`storageUnavailable`, `unexpectedError`, `staleWrite`, `camped`, `vaultFull` and the rest) beside `VaultError`, `isVaultError`, `refusalKindVals`, `outcomeOf` and their types. The front door (`safebyok`) is unchanged. **`safebyok/testing` gains `describeVaultDBBattery(label, opts)`**, the cases every VaultDB must pass: an adapter hands it `makeVaultDB` and a `_cheat_rowsOf` backdoor to its raw rows (`VaultDBRowCheats`), and optionally `makeTwin` (a second store over the same rows), `makeCappedVaultDB`, `_cheat_failNextCall`, and `watches: false`. It also exports the scopes and rows the battery is built from (`bankScope`, `vaultScope`, `boxscope`, `vaultScopeOf`, `boxscopeOf`, `passphraseWayin`, `passkeyWayin`, `vaultAt`, `boxAt`, `vaultTombstoneOf`, `boxTombstoneOf`). A gate verb refused for a registered_at that disagrees says so in the same words. **Stored format:** unchanged.
