The strength checker is held to its contract in one place. Vault.make's schema takes strengthChecker (a function, defaulting to the one that refuses every passphrase) and refuses one that is not, in its own words, as bad_option; parseStrengthChecker is gone. A checker may carry an idempotent load, which every hello() runs before reading storage: one that fails fails the hello, unexpected_error with the load's error as the cause, and the next hello tries again. zxcvbnStrengthChecker carries one that fetches its dictionary, and no longer answers weak when it will not load. vault.strengthChecker is the host's checker guarded (a GuardedStrengthChecker): a blank passphrase is weak unheard, a checker that answers no verdict is refused bad_option, one that throws unexpected_error carrying nothing. safebyok-react's dialog asks it for its advice, so a broken checker now fails the dialog, reporting the vault's refusal, where it said nothing. hello() and ensureLease() refuse anything but a bag of their options with Zod's own detailed message, the event hint beside it; every options bag's refusal now leads with what Zod found, naming the field.
