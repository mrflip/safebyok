**A vault does one call's work at a time.** Every call's work against the vault -- a hello's read, an unlock, a change to the ways in, a box read or written, `lock()`, `del()`, `close()` and the expiry -- runs as one turn, in the order called, so a `lock()` lands after the turn in flight: a `getWhisper()` already reading hands its whisper back, an `unlock()` already proving writes its lease, and the lock then locks and erases it. `hello()` reads through `VaultDB.getVault` beside this device's lease and mark, in one round, and takes nothing up when any of them, or the strength checker's `load`, fails: the vault stays `opening` after a first hello refused so. No call reads storage on its own: `unlock()`, `putWayin()` and `delWayin()` prove against the vault as the last hello within `staleMinutes` read it (call `hello({ staleMinutes: 0 })` first to prove against storage as it stands), and a write storage refuses as stale refuses at once, `putBox`'s with `meta.box_stored`, with no catch-up read: the next hello reads afresh, whatever its `staleMinutes`. A `putBox()` lists the box it wrote, and `delBox()` unlists it, reading nothing. Within `staleMinutes` of `askPassphraseAt`, `ensureLease()` asks for a proof (`ask.why` `lease_ending`) though the vault still shows `unlocked`. A ring of the storage watch is a hello that reads; its failure warns once, as `{ call: 'refresh' }`. A lease a read drops or discards is erased after the read, and a device store that fails that is logged, not thrown. No stored shape changes: every vault and lease written before reads as it did. In safebyok-react nothing changes but what the vault does.
