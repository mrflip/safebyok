Two words for what storage holds in the open, used the same way everywhere in the docs, doc blocks and READMEs. A field is *visible* when it is unencrypted and authenticated, bound into its record's encryption context, so one altered in storage makes the record refuse to open: a lockbox's label, revision, epoch, times and sidecar, and each wayin's header. It is *unencrypted* when nothing more is promised, as of a backend that holds "ciphertext and unencrypted fields". These replace "public fields", "public face" and "server-readable". Documentation only; nothing stored or exported changes, and no host needs to do anything.
