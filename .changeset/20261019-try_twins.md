**Every vault call in two forms.** `tryHello`, `tryUnlock`, `tryLock`, `tryChangePassphrase`, `tryAddPassphrase`, `tryAddPasskey`, `tryDelWayin`, `tryPutBox`, `tryGetBox`, `tryDelBox`, `tryDel` resolve to an `Outcome` and never reject; `tryClose` answers one at once. `hello`, `unlock` and the rest resolve its `val` or throw its `err`, as before. New `getWhisper(boxlabel)`, the whisper or its refusal thrown. **A throw that is no refusal is `unexpected_error`** (a new kind, misuse): a bug in safebyok, or in a function the app handed it -- a passphrase test, a key deriver, a passkey maker that throws something other than a `VaultError` -- reaches the caller as a VaultError of that kind, what was thrown its `cause`, and never logged (a host's function may have been handed a passphrase); a host that caught a passphrase test's own error from `unlock()` now finds it as `err.cause`. The `VaultDB` base says a throw in its own code that way too, where it said `storage_unavailable`; an adapter's own throw is `storage_unavailable` as before. **A `do*` hook that answers with an `Outcome` still wrapped** -- a VaultDB handing its call on to another and forgetting to unwrap -- is refused as `bad_option`, where it read as a damaged record, or for `doPutVaultProps` and `doDelVault` as landed. **`valOf` and `unexpectedError` are exported**, and `outcomeOf` takes what a throw becomes as a second argument. **`hello` ignores an event** handed in place of its options, so `addEventListener('focus', vault.hello)` works. In `safebyok-react`, `VaultProvider`, `PassphraseModal` and `PasskeyUnlockButton` call the `try` forms. No stored shape changed.
