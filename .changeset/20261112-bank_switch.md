**`vault_locked` is renamed `bank_locked`**, as the bank is what locks: a host matching `err.kind === 'vault_locked'` now matches `'bank_locked'`, refused where it was before, its message saying the bank is locked and nothing was read or written. **`BankAnnouncement`, `BankAnnounce` and `BankPanic` are renamed `DeviceAnnouncement`, `DeviceAnnounce` and `DevicePanic`**, the device being what announces and panics; their shapes are unchanged. **`Device.make` takes a `bankDB`** where it took a `vaultDB`: a `BankDB` from a storage adapter, branded and answering each of its verbs, the refusal `bad_option` otherwise. **The bank holds the lock.** `bank.ensureUnlocked()`, `bank.unlock()` and `bank.lock()` replace the vault's: one lease per bank on this device, cited by its main gate, sealing every vault's boxes; a vault's `isLocked` reads the bank's, and `vault.lock()` only tells its listeners and boxes that the lock moved. `bank.autolock()` peeks the bank's one cached lease. The vault's `replacePassphrasePrompt`, `addPasskeyPrompt`, `revokePasskeyPrompt`, `gate`, `fullRecord`, `ensureUnlocked`, `unlock` and `vaultListing.buildVault` are gone; the gate's prompts come back on the bank's gate in the next release. **`bank.ensureVault()` puts a vault where none is listed**, needing the bank unlocked (`bank_locked` otherwise); one already stored is answered as it is. **`device.ensureBank()` builds the bank** through a `build_bank` gating where none is stored, and asks storage once at startup. **A gating is a bank's:** its kinds are `build_bank` and `unlock_bank`, its `bank` replaces its `vault`, its snapshot is the bank's scope, and `VaultForGatingkind` is `BankForGatingkind`. **`lease_revoke_failed` names the `bank`** where it named the `vault`, and the default `onLifecycle` vacates that bank. `bank.maingate` is the bank's `Gate`, now kept in step with its row; its JSON leaves out its wayins, and its snapshot shows each wayin's header. The types `Gate`, `GateListing`, `BankListing`, `GateSnapshot` and `BankGatingkind` are exported. **Stored format:** the models store through the BankDB's rows, a bank row, its gate row and vault rows with no gate; nothing stored through a VaultDB is read, so a host starts with no bank, and its user builds one.
