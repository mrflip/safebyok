**Renamed exports; each behaves as before.** `DeviceDB`'s `incrementDeviceRecord`, `tryIncrementDeviceRecord` and the hook `doIncrementDeviceRecord` are `advanceDeviceRecord`, `tryAdvanceDeviceRecord` and `doAdvanceDeviceRecord`: a host's own `DeviceDB` subclass renames its `doIncrementDeviceRecord` override, a `deviceDB` a host hands the bank must answer `tryAdvanceDeviceRecord`, and the exported type `DeviceVerb` names `'advanceDeviceRecord'` in place of `'incrementDeviceRecord'`. **Stored format:** unchanged.

**The models' bank keeps a device record and can clear a cached vault.** `Bank.hello()` now advances this device's record of the bank's scope and takes the `device_id` it holds, so every session on a device shares the first `device_id` stored; until a hello, `bank.device_id` is the option's (or a fresh one). `Bank.clearCachedVault(bag)` and `tryClearCachedVault(bag)` close the vault and erase its lease and cached copy, sound or damaged, leaving the device record. The bank's `deviceDB` must now answer `advanceDeviceRecord` and `clearCachedVault` too. A new, slimmer `DeviceDB` for the models, with `memoryDeviceDB` and an `idbDeviceDB` over its own IndexedDB database (`<dbName>:devicedb`), lives in `src/devicedb/` and is not yet exported. **New export:** `Scopepath.withinBankScopepathPrefix(scope)`, the prefix every vault row of a bank's scope begins with (`'vault^acme^u1^'`). **Stored format:** the new DeviceDB stores a device record, format 8001, under `bank^<applabel>^<user_id>`; the old `DeviceDB` and what it stores are unchanged.
