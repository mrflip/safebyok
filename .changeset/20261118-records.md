Empty: the new `safebyok` is private. What a caller will see: a record's type is `Foo` plus `Record`, and a bare `Foo` is left for a class. `Thing` is now `ThingRecord`, the union of the kinds' records, and `BankThing`, `GateThing` and `BoxThing` are `BankRecord`, `GateRecord` and `BoxRecord` (the old `BankRecord` alias of `BankThing` is that one type). The fields every kind has, the old `ThingRecord`, are `Sparse<ThingRecord>`, and `ThingChecks.thingRecord` is `ThingChecks.sparse`. A box has no forms of its own: the old `BoxRecord` (a live box) is `LiveOf<BoxRecord>`, `BoxTombstone` is `TombstoneOf<BoxRecord>`, and `BoxShell` is `ShellOf<BoxRecord>`, a new generic beside `LiveOf` and `TombstoneOf`, which now mark a record by intersection: `LiveOf<GateRecord>` is a gate with `am: 'a'`, where it was any gate, and `TombstoneOf<GateRecord>` a gate with `am: 'x'`, where it was `never`. `safebyok`, `safebyok/core` and `safebyok/storage` export the new names and no alias stays under an old one. `safebyok-react`'s tests read the new names. Every kind of Thing now has the one `fmt`, `ThingFormat` (100501), in place of `BankThingFormat`, `GateThingFormat`, `BoxThingFormat` and `ThingFormats`: the kind is in the labelpath alone, and a stored Thing of the old fmts (3101, 4101, 5103) fails the record check and reads as damaged. A box tombstone carries every field of a live box, its `sidecar` always `{}`: one stored before this, without a sidecar, fails the record check and reads as damaged (no migration, before 1.0). A revival after a tombstone or the shell still needs the sidecar given in `publicProps`, as it did. `safebyok-postgres`'s `safebyok_box_things` table makes `sidecar` NOT NULL for a tombstone too, in step: a table made before this refuses a box tombstone with a `sidecar`, and is made again (the postgres store is not exported yet).
