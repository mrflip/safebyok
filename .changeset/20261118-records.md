Empty: the new `safebyok` is private. What a caller will see: a record's type is `Foo` plus `Record`, and a bare `Foo` is left for a class. `Thing` is now `ThingRecord`, the union of the kinds' records, and `BankThing`, `GateThing` and `BoxThing` are `BankRecord`, `GateRecord` and `BoxRecord` (the old `BankRecord` alias of `BankThing` is that one type). The fields every kind has, the old `ThingRecord`, are `Sparse<ThingRecord>`, and `ThingChecks.thingRecord` is `ThingChecks.sparse`. A box has no forms of its own: the old `BoxRecord` (a live box) is `LiveOf<BoxRecord>`, `BoxTombstone` is `TombstoneOf<BoxRecord>`, and `BoxShell` is `ShellOf<BoxRecord>`, a new generic beside `LiveOf` and `TombstoneOf`, which now mark a record by intersection (`LiveOf<GateRecord>` is a gate with `am: 'a'`, where it was `never`). `safebyok`, `safebyok/core` and `safebyok/storage` export the new names and no alias stays under an old one. `safebyok-react` and `safebyok-postgres` read the new names.
