Empty: the new `safebyok` is private, and `safebyok-big` is untouched. What a caller of the new core will see: **a Bank's prompts join the gating already open on it**, where they refused `already_asking`: `launchUnlockBankPrompt` joins a gating of any kind on its bank (each ends with the bank unlocked), and each gate-change prompt joins one of its own kind; every joined prompt resumes when the gating lands, and rejects `dismissed` when it is dismissed. A prompt that cannot join, such as a passphrase replacement while an unlock is asked, or any prompt while a birth is asked, still refuses `already_asking`. **`bank.ensureUnlocked()`** resolves at once on an unlocked bank and otherwise prompts. **Re-entry points:** `bank.attemptUnlock`, `attemptReplacePassphrase`, `attemptAddPasskey` and `attemptRevokePasskey` take the cleartexts a form collected and resolve an `Outcome` instead of throwing: each answers the bank's open gating of its kind, resuming every prompt waiting on it, and with none open makes its change directly, spending a passkey answer whatever the outcome. **`device.lock()`** locks the device's bank (for now, the last `Bank` made on the device). **Stored format:** unchanged.
