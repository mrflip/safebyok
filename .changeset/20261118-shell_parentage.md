Empty: the new `safebyok` is private. What a caller will see: `shellOf(raw, parent?)` takes any partial record. A root's shell is decided by its labelpath alone, a bare modelkind, and reads no parent. Below a root, the shell's labelpath and lifepath come from the new `labelpathWithParentage` and `lifepathWithParentage`: given whole, or a label (and the shell's lifeseq) under the parent's. A parent of another user or bank, or one the shell's labelpath or lifepath does not hang from, refuses `bad_record`, where a parent's user and bank went unread before. A shell's refusals now say what the failing field check says: the phrases `ShellUnscoped`, `ShellNotChild`, `ShellOfOtherLife` and `ShellLifeless` are gone, and the types `ShellScoped` and `ShellParent` are no longer exported. `ShellLifeseq` is renamed `VoidLifeseq`, and `ShellLifepath` `RootVoidLifepath`. `ThingChecks` is exported whole, with `modelkind`, `anyLifeseq` and `anyLifepath` among its checks. `carOf` is unchanged: a `fullest` that is not an earlier whole record of `newest`'s life still refuses `bad_record`. In `safebyok-big`, `demandConsistent`'s refusal is optional, and refuses `bad_record` where none is given. A revtok must now be a UUID, as `crypto.randomUUID` mints and the void revtok is: a stored record with any other revtok fails its check and reads as damaged. `isTombstone` now checks that it was given a Thing's scope and citation marked deleted, not only `am: 'x'`. A lifeseq or lifepath that may be void refuses with what a lifeseq or lifepath is, where it said only "Invalid input".
