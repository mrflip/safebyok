Empty: the new `safebyok` is private, and `safebyok-big` is untouched. What a caller of the new core will see: **`vault.withWhisper(labelpath, fn)`** calls `fn` with the box at `labelpath` unsealed (its whisper, sidecar and record fields) and resolves what `fn` resolves. On a locked bank it first asks the user to unlock it, as `bank.ensureUnlocked()` does, and rejects as that does (`dismissed`, say). It unseals the box's newest revision as the vault's minder gets it, and refuses `absent_box` where no box is stored, or it was deleted. It keeps nothing of the unsealed box, and rethrows what `fn` throws as it came. **`vault.putBoxOnce(labelpath, patch, secrets)`** now gets the box whole when its minder holds it only sparse (known from a list, say), so it keeps the stored sidecar where it refused `bad_record` for want of one; a sparse box passed as `cited` still needs the sidecar in `patch`. **`bank.launchUnlockBankPrompt()` and `bank.ensureUnlocked()`**, finding a passphrase or passkey change open on the bank, wait for it to end, however it ends, and then resolve if the bank is unlocked or ask the user to unlock it: before, they joined that change and rejected with its refusal (`dismissed`, say), the bank left locked. An open unlock is still joined, and a gate change still refuses `already_asking` beside a gating of another kind. **`bankStageVals` and `bankEndCauseVals`**, exported from `safebyok/core`, list the values of `BankStage` and `BankEndCause`. **`device.getSnapshot()`'s `gating`** is now `{ gatingkind, gatingseq, isAttempting }`: its `lastRefusal` is gone, and so is the `gating.lastRefusal` getter. A dialog shows the refusal its own `attempt…` call resolves with. **Stored format:** unchanged.
