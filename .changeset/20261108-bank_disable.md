A bank swept or vacated is spent from the call on: `bank.isEnabled` turns false for good, its snapshot reads `refused` with a `disabled` err, and `hello`, `ensureVault` and `vaultAt` refuse `disabled`; make another bank with `Bank.make`. A disabled bank calls none of the host's `onPanic`, `onBadNews` or `requestCleartext`, falling back to the core's defaults, with one exception: `bank.vacate()` disables the bank first, then erases the bank's leases on this device, and that erase still tells the host's own `onPanic` of a lease it cannot erase and `onBadNews` of a refused listing. `bank.vacate()` now answers a promise, which settles once that erase is done, and rejects only a story it cannot take. A vault's own lease erase during a bank's vacate falls to `DefaultOnPanic`, which reports it to the platform, as the bank's erase covers that row too.

**A revoked identity is a panic, not bad news.** Where the vaultDB refuses `identity_revoked`, the bank's `onPanic` hears it, no longer its `onBadNews`, and the caller still gets the refusal. `DefaultOnPanic` vacates the bank on it, the bank's snapshot then reading `refused` with that refusal as its `err`, and still reports it to the platform. A host's own `onPanic` decides for itself: call `DefaultOnPanic(panic)` from it to keep the vacate. `onPanic` now hears anything the host must act on for safety, where it heard only a cached lease that outlived its erase. A box listing's revocation names its vault in the `Panic`, so `DefaultOnPanic` vacates that vault too. `bank.vacate({ cause })` takes the refusal that called for it, optional, which the snapshot then shows, and a `hello` the vacate crosses refuses with it; a repeat vacate settles with the first one's erase, a vacate after a sweep still erasing. The new exported type `VacateStory` is what `bank.vacate` may be told. The new exported type `BankPanic` is how a vault or a listing panics through its bank. `bank.panic` and `vault.panic` check what they are told, and reject a malformed panic `bad_option`.
