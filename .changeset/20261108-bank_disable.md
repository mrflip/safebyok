A bank swept or vacated is spent from the call on: `bank.isEnabled` turns false for good, its snapshot reads `refused` with a `disabled` err, and `hello`, `ensureVault` and `vaultAt` refuse `disabled`; make another bank with `Bank.make`. A disabled bank asks the user nothing and calls neither the host's `onPanic` nor its `onBadNews`: a panic's refusal goes to the platform, and bad news to the bank's logger. The one exception: `bank.vacate()` disables the bank first, then erases the bank's leases on this device, and that erase still tells the host's own `onPanic` of a lease it cannot erase and `onBadNews` of a refused listing. `bank.vacate()` now answers a promise, which settles once each erase has been tried. A vault's own lease erase during a bank's vacate is reported to the platform, as the bank's erase covers that row too.

**A revoked identity is a panic, not bad news.** Where the vaultDB refuses `identity_revoked`, the bank's `onPanic` hears it, no longer its `onBadNews`, and the caller still gets the refusal. `DefaultOnPanic` vacates the bank on it, the bank's snapshot then reading `refused` with that refusal as its `err`, and still reports it to the platform; on any other panic it vacates the vault named, never both. A host's own `onPanic` decides for itself: call `DefaultOnPanic(panic)` from it to keep the vacate. `onPanic` now hears anything the host must act on for safety, where it heard only a cached lease that outlived its erase. A revocation names no vault in the `Panic`, as it concerns the user. `bank.vacate({ cause })` takes the refusal that called for it, optional, which the snapshot then shows, and a repeat vacate settles with the first, a vacate after a sweep still erasing. The new exported type `VacateStory` is what `bank.vacate` may be told. The new exported type `BankPanic` is how a vault or a listing panics through its bank. **`bank.panic` is no longer public**: each listing and vault is handed the bank's panic as it is made, so the `Vault` constructor takes it fourth. A listing of vaults or boxes answered after a sweep or a vacate refuses `disabled`, and panics nobody.
