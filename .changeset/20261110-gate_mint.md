The vault side now mints a gate verb's revision. `VaultDB.replacePassphrase`, `addPasskey` and `revokePasskey` (and their `try` forms) take `(record, { prior })`: the whole next `VaultRecord`, and the revision it replaces. A storage adapter checks that record and mints nothing. `replacePassphraseParams`, `passkeyAddParams` and `revokePasskeyParams` are gone, for `passkeyAddRecord`, `passkeyRevokeRecord` and `gateVerbOpts`, and the GraphQL mutations take `record` and `opts` arguments. A storage answer that does not match what the device sent is refused as `storage_issue`. `BoxWorld` no longer has `demandUnlocked`, and `vault.demandUnlocked()` on a tombstoned, swept or vacated vault refuses as `vault_locked`, where it said gone or disabled. **`putVault` takes the `PutOpts` that `putBox` takes**, `ifPresent: 'repl'` included and the default, so `putVault(record, { prior })` over a live vault at `prior` replaces it, where it refused as `camped`; over a live vault at another revision it answers `stale_write`. A put replaces a live row only within its own life: `putVault` or `putBox` at a live row's revision under another `vault_tok` or `box_tok` refuses as `camped`, writing nothing. **Storage adapters:** `isVaultPutAllowed` and `isBoxPutAllowed` take the record being written, `(ante, record, opts)`; the IndexedDB, Convex and Postgres adapters pass it. `putVaultOpts` and `PutVaultOpts` are gone for `putOpts` and `PutOpts`, and GraphQL's `safebyokPutVault` takes `SafebyokPutOptsInput` (`SafebyokPutVaultOptsInput` and `SafebyokPutVaultIfPresent` are gone). Each gate verb checks the stored vault, then puts through `putVault` with `ifAbsent: 'fail'` and `ifPresent: 'repl'`; a gate verb or a delete refuses an `ifAbsent` or `ifPresent` offered in its options as `bad_option`. **Options sent as null take their default** (`putOpts`, `ifAbsentOpts`, a box's put, create and fetch options), so a GraphQL client sending `ifPresent: null` gets `'repl'` where it was refused; a `Checkers` builder gets `orDefault(check, fallback)` to do the same. `putOptsGiven` checks a put's options as parsed, every one given, which is what a `doPut*` receives and what the Convex component validates. A box put carrying both whisper and sidecar over a sparse box no longer fetches the row first: `box.ensurePuttable` takes the patch, and a box behind storage answers `stale_write`.
