The vault side now mints a gate verb's revision. `VaultDB.replacePassphrase`, `addPasskey` and `revokePasskey` (and their `try` forms) take `(record, { prior })`: the whole next `VaultRecord`, and the revision it replaces. A storage adapter checks that record and mints nothing. `replacePassphraseParams`, `passkeyAddParams` and `revokePasskeyParams` are gone, for `passkeyAddRecord`, `passkeyRevokeRecord` and `gateVerbOpts`, and the GraphQL mutations take `record` and `opts` arguments. A storage answer that does not match what the device sent is refused as `storage_issue`. `BoxWorld` no longer has `demandUnlocked`, and `vault.demandUnlocked()` on a tombstoned vault says locked rather than gone. A box put carrying both whisper and sidecar no longer fetches the row first: `box.ensurePuttable` takes the patch, a sparse box may take such a put, and a box behind storage (or stored but not listed) answers `stale_write`.
