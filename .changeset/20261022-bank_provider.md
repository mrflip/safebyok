safebyok-react's `VaultProvider` is replaced by `BankProvider`, which makes the app's bank for the signed-in user from the same props, less `vaultlabel`: move `vaultlabel` to the hooks, `useVault('main')` and `useVaultSnapshot('main')`. `useVault(vaultlabel)` now hands down the bank's entry for that vault (`status` `starting`, `timely` or `refused`, its `vault` and `err`), asking the bank for it only while the bank holds none, where `useVault()` handed down the Vault object: read `useVault('main')?.vault`. `useOpenError`, `openError`, `retryOpen` and the provider's `delDamagedVault` are gone: render the entry's `err`, ask again with `useBank()` (new) and `bank.ensureVault`, and delete a damaged vault with `bank.delDamagedVault` (new on the core's Bank). A vault deleted, or found deleted or made anew, now stays refused (`vault_gone`, `vault_remade`) until the app asks again, rather than coming back as a new, novel vault on its own; the dialog's "Start over" still asks for the new one (`PassphraseModal`'s new `onStartedOver`). A vault a later read finds behind stays in the entry, locked, its `trouble` `vault_behind`; `bank.clearCachedVault` through `useBank()` is the way past it. Fixed in the core: a bank `subscribe` listener that called `bank.ensureVault` as it heard an entry go `starting` recursed until the stack ran out; it now joins the birth under way. A birth let go in the task that asked for it now reads nothing. `bank.tryDelDamagedVault` refused `stale_write` lets the entry go, so the next ask births the vault made anew, and `bank.clearCachedVault` lets the entry go only once this device's record and lease are erased. Nothing stored changes shape: a host with stored vaults, device records and leases sees them read as before.
