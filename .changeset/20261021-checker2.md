A broken strength checker is thrown, not refused: a strengthChecker that throws or answers no verdict is thrown by the call that asked it, even a try form (tryUnlock, tryPutWayin, ask.attempt), as unexpected_error or bad_option carrying nothing it heard, for the app's error handler. vault.strengthChecker and the GuardedStrengthChecker type are gone: ask vault.checkPassphrase(typed), which binds the vault's scope, refuses a non-string as bad_option, and resolves the verdict; ProofVault picks checkPassphrase. hello() runs the checker's load beside its read of storage rather than before it: a failed load still fails the hello, unexpected_error with the load's error as cause, and takes up no lease, but the read may have landed (the state may show absent or locked rather than opening). A hello the storage watch runs that fails is logged as that hello's own refusal, once until a hello succeeds, never as a storage read failure. safebyok-react: PassphraseModal's advice defaults to vault.checkPassphrase (VaultProvider no longer supplies it); advice that throws, and an attempt that throws, go to the app's error boundary above the dialog's own; a host passphraseAdvice's VaultError is no longer passed through, reported instead as a fixed unexpected_error.
