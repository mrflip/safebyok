Empty: safebyok-react-lean is private, and nothing published changes. What a host of the React layer will see: **each part takes `className` and passes every other attribute through**, and marks its state in `data-` attributes for a stylesheet to select. `PassphraseInput` and `WhisperInput` hand both to their `<input>`, which carries `data-revealed` while the text shows and `data-invalid` while `aria-invalid` is true; `SubmitButton` carries `data-pending` while the form is pending; `WhisperText` hands them to its outer `<span>`, which carries `data-revealed` and `data-pending`, and `WhisperMask` to its `<code>`. On `WhisperText` and `WhisperMask`, `className` replaces the part's own class (`whisper-text`, `whisper-mask`) rather than adding to it. **The words are overridable in one place**: `<KitPhrasesProvider phrases={{ unlockButton: 'Déverrouiller' }}>` puts a host's words in place of the English ones for every kit form and part inside it, a word left out keeping the one the provider above gives; `PartsPhrasesProvider` does the same for the parts alone, and `useKitPhrases()` and `usePartsPhrases()` read them. A misspelt word, or one that is no string, is refused with `bad_record`. `refusalTextOf(err, phrases)` takes the words to answer in, the English ones when left out. `KitPhrases` no longer holds the four messages of a passkey ceremony's refusals, which a refusal carries as its message and `refusalTextOf` shows as they are: they are not overridable yet. **The kit's forms show a visible label for each field**, in place of an `aria-label`; the placeholder stays, and a test that finds a field by its label text still finds it. `useNewPassphrase()`'s `field` and `confirmField` each carry an `id`, for a host's `<label htmlFor>`. Types exported beside them: `KitPhrasesProviderProps`, `RefusalPhrases`, `PartsPhrasesProviderProps`. **Stored format:** unchanged.
