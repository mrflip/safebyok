The schema in `safebyok-graphql/server` is typed: one operation for each front verb of a vaultDB, and `safebyokWatch`. Each operation answers what the verb's `try*` form answers. A refusal is the answer's `err` field, never a GraphQL error: its refusalkind, its message, and a meta holding only capacity, fieldnames and call. Records, options and answers are typed fields. A sidecar, a damaged row and `err.meta` are `SafebyokJson`, and times are `SafebyokEpochMillis`. `safebyokTidyGone` and `safebyokHeard` are gone. `safebyokWatch` ends with an `identity_revoked` notice once its session is signed out, or signed in as another user. A refusal's cause no longer reaches the server's logs through the resolvers. `GraphQLVaultDB` speaks it: a class implementing the core's `VaultDBVerbs` over your urql Client, each answer checked by the core's answer checks. A GraphQL error, a client and server out of step, is `unexpected_error` with the error its cause; a failed fetch stays `storage_unavailable`. Its watch subscribes again a second after a stream ends, except after `identity_revoked`. Breaking: `GraphQLVaultDB` takes `{ client }` alone, refusing `maxRecordBytes` as `bad_option`: a record's size is the server's store to cap. `VaultOperations` holds the new documents.
