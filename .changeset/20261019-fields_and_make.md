**`Vault.make` touches nothing; the first `hello()` reads.** A vault is made in state `opening` and reads no storage, no device store, and starts no timer or watch until a call asks: the first `hello()` -- the host's, or the one any other call but `lock()` and `close()` starts -- reads storage, starts the storage watch and settles. A host that read `state` straight after `Vault.make` without calling anything sees `opening` until it does; the logger no longer warns of a first hello refused, since the refusal reaches the hello's caller. **The vault's fields are first-class**: `applabel`, `user_id`, `vaultlabel`, `vault_id`, `registered_at`, `unlock_epoch`, `vault_key_epoch`, `wayins`, `state`, `boxes`, `capacity`, `askPassphraseAt`, `trouble`, `syncing` and `snapshot` are read-only getters on the vault, live, and `leaseMillis` is a getter too. **`VaultSnapshot` is reshaped and frozen**: the same fields in one frozen object; `scope` is gone (read `applabel`, `user_id` and `vaultlabel`), and `vault_id`, `registered_at`, `unlock_epoch`, `vault_key_epoch` and `capacity` are new. **`trouble` is bad news only**: the latest `bad_news` refusal or damaged box, or what the new `onBadNews` handler threw, cleared by a read that finds nothing amiss; a condition such as `storage_unavailable` reaches only its caller, and `syncing: false`. **`hello(opts)`** takes `staleMinutes` (default from `Vault.make`'s new `staleMinutes` option, `StaleMinutes`, one minute; `0` forces a read) and resolves the vault itself. **`onBadNews`**, a new `Vault.make` option (`BadNewsHandler`), is told each piece of bad news once, before the call that met it settles, never awaited; a box a listing newly shows damaged is told as `damaged_box` with the new `meta.box_listed`. In `safebyok-react`, `VaultProvider` passes `staleMinutes` and `onBadNews` through, says the vault's first hello itself, and hands down `openError` from that hello's refusal rather than the snapshot's `trouble`. No stored shape changed. A `lock()`, `close()`, `del()`, unlock or expiry landing while a `hello()` takes up this device's lease now wins: the hello takes nothing up.
