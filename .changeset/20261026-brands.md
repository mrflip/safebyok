**Names for the common primitives, and brands for what only safebyok makes.** New exported types, each a plain alias, so a host's own strings and numbers pass as before: `Applabel`, `Vaultlabel`, `Boxlabel`, `UserId`, `VaultTok`, `RevisionTok`, and `EpochMillis` (now a type, a number, beside the check of that name). New branded types, which only a parse makes: `PassphraseSalt`, `PrfSalt`, `WhisperCiphered`, `BoxKeyWrapped`, `VaultKeyWayinWrapped`, `VaultKeyLeaseWrapped` and `PassphraseCleartext`. And three facets, generic types for later shapes to use: `Lifecycled`, `Refused` and `ConsistencyWith`. Exports whose types became branded: `PassphraseWayinRecord` (`salt_b64`, `vault_key_wayin_wrapped`), `PasskeyWayinRecord` (`prf_salt_b64`, `vault_key_wayin_wrapped`), `BoxRecord` (`box_key_wrapped`, `whisper_ciphered`), `LeaseRecord` (`vault_key_lease_wrapped`), so every type built on them (`WayinRecord`, `VaultProps`, `GatedVaultProps`, `PasskeyWayinSummary`, `BoxCiphered`, `PasskeyWayin`'s `prf_salt_b64`). Reading those fields works as before: a branded salt is a string, a branded wrap a `Ciphered`. Writing one by hand no longer typechecks, as a test fixture or an adapter's hand-built record might: parse the record with its schema, whose output is branded, or type a record not yet checked as Zod's `input` of its schema (`z.input<typeof BoxRecord>`), which is not branded. A custom `KeyDeriver` still takes the passphrase as a plain string. Nothing stored changes, and nothing at runtime, but two sentences: an empty `vault_tok` or `revision_tok` is refused as "A token is minted by safebyok, so it can't be empty", where Zod said "Too small", and a passphrase that is not text is refused as before, its sentence now without a full stop. `FarFuture`, `FarPast`, `ForeverMillis`, `MinuteMillis` and `EpochMillis` are exported as before.
