A Bank births each vault: `Bank.make({ applabel, vaultDB, ...browserOpts() })` once, then `bank.ensureVault({ userId, vaultlabel })` hands out the vault's Vault object, born of one read of storage and this device's store. `Vault.make`, `close()`, the `opening` and `absent` states and `syncing` are gone: a vault is `novel` (stored, with no passphrase yet), `locked` or `unlocked`, and the snapshot's `freshness` is `timely`, `aged` or `refused`. `bank.closeVault`, `closeVaults` and `reopenVault` let a Vault object go; a let-go, deleted or remade vault refuses every call `vault_gone` (or `vault_remade`), and `bank.ensureVault` hands out the next. The device mark and `rolled_back` give way to the device record (the highest `vault_epoch` seen per scope, never lowered) and `vault_behind`, cleared with `bank.clearCachedVault`; a DeviceDB of your own implements `getDeviceRecord`, `raiseDeviceRecord` and `delDeviceRecord` in place of the mark verbs. `created_elsewhere` is `gate_made_elsewhere`, and a first unlock that loses the race no longer tries its passphrase on the winner's vault. `del()` locks only once storage deleted the vault. A host with records written before sees each device's marks swept unread, and its first birth starts a new device record at the vault's current `vault_epoch`; leases stored before are taken up as ever when they fit. A call waiting on the passphrase prompt when its Vault object is let go or deleted refuses `vault_gone`, where it refused `vault_locked`. safebyok-react's `VaultProvider` keeps its props, built on a Bank; once its own vault is deleted (as the dialog's "Start over" does) or found deleted or made anew, it asks for the vault again on its own and hands down the one that stands, so `openError` never reads `vault_gone`; sign-outs, a new user and unmounting refuse waiting calls `vault_gone`. Opening a vault now writes (`ensureVault`), so a safebyok-postgres role needs `INSERT` and `UPDATE` on `safebyok_vaults` and `DELETE` on `safebyok_vault_tombstones` beside `SELECT`.
