Empty: the new `safebyok` is private, and `safebyok-postgres` exports nothing new. What a host of `safebyok` will see: a `track` carries the root box, at `box`, beside the bank and the main gate, and `tracked` answers for all three. `VaultDB` no longer builds asks: `Bank` does, with `makeGetAsk`, `makeListAsk`, `makePutAsk` and `makeDelAsk`, each filling in the bank's scope, the modelkind, the default flags and the bank's `track` (the revisions this Bank object holds of itself, its main gate and its root box, which may be stale); `getAskOf` and the other builders stay in `safebyok/storage`. `Device.make` takes `onBadNews`, which hears each refusal no caller waits on, by default as a warning on the device's logger (`DefaultOnBadNews`, `tellBadNewsTo`, `BadNews` and `BadNewsHandler`, as in safebyok-big), and `bank.tellBadNews` passes news to it. `ThingMinder.make({ modelkind }, { vaultDB, tsq, queryCacheOpts? }, bank)` reads the bank's `scope`, `rootBox`, `makeGetAsk` and `tellBadNews`; `minders` is gone, and `QueryCacheOpts` holds `staleTime` (default an hour) and `gcTime` alone, every minder setting `networkMode: 'always'` and `retry: false`. Every minder holds its own watch of the bank and caches only the Things of its kind; no minder caches a notice's `world`, and a change to a root an ask tracked comes as that root's own notice. A box minder caches the bank's root box at `hello`, and builds the shell of a void box from it. `get` and `tryAt` take one whole ask, the bank's get ask with `staleTime` and `ifStale`, and no options; `minder.makeGetAsk({ labelpath, staleTime?, ifStale? })` builds one. `list()` caches the Things one label under the minder's root sparse, and stale unless held whole. `listen(listener)` calls `listener(answer)` with the answer `tryAt` would give for that scope, `vacant` once its entry is removed. What a listener throws goes to `onBadNews` as `unexpected_error`, and so does the watch's trouble, as it is. A get answered older than the record cached when it began refuses `stale_read`, a new refusalkind in family `condition`, and the cached record stays, no longer timely. A get under way when `goodbye` is called refuses `disabled`. `shellOf(scoped, parent?)` makes the shell of any scope, replacing `voidSentinelOf`, and refuses a record that is not a shell. The shell's lifeseq is `'-000000001'`, ten characters like every other. `seqOfLifeseq` is now `lifenumOfLifeseq`, and `lifeseqOf` is `lifeseqOfLifenum`. `hello` called twice opens one watch, and `goodbye` also clears the minder's query defaults. A cache key names the modelkind once. `pins` is now `ties`.
