Empty: the new `safebyok` is private. What a host of `safebyok` will see: `ThingMinder.make({ modelkind }, { vaultDB, tsq, queryCacheOpts?, minders? }, bank)` takes its bank third, as a friend, reading only its `scope` and `rootBox`; `onBadNews` and the ended-bank check are gone. `QueryCacheOpts` holds `staleTime` (default an hour) and `gcTime` alone: every minder sets `networkMode: 'always'` and `retry: false`, and refuses either from a host. The bank's minder holds the bank's only watch, and hands each gate and box it hears of, and the main gate of each world, to the minders given as `minders`; no other minder watches. `hello` called twice opens one watch, and `goodbye` also clears the minder's query defaults. `list()` lists the Things one label under the minder's root, such as the boxes under the root box, and caches each sparse, and stale unless held whole. `listen(listener)` hears TanStack Query's cache events for the minder's own entries. `VoidSentinel` is now a type: `voidSentinelOf(scope, parentLifepath)` makes the shell of the Thing at a scope, which a get of a void scope returns and a birth cites; a box under the root box gets its own shell, in the root box's life. A cache key names the modelkind once (`['safebyok', userid, banklabel, ...labels]`). `pins` is now `ties`.
