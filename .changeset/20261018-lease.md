What a device keeps to unlock again without a proof is now its lease. Exports: DeviceUnlock is Lease, UnlockExpectation is LeaseExpectation, ForcePassphraseReentryHours is LeaseHours and MaxReentryHours is MaxLeaseHours; openVault's forcePassphraseReentryHours option (and VaultProvider's prop) is leaseHours, and a vault's reentryWindowMillis is leaseMillis; a DeviceDB's readUnlock, writeUnlock and eraseUnlock are readLease, writeLease and eraseLease, so a DeviceDB of your own renames its three verbs; Ckeys.unlock and Ckeys.isUnlock are Ckeys.lease and Ckeys.isLease; safebyok/testing's unlockOf is leaseOf. Stored: the record's format is safebyok-lease-v1, its local_unlock_key is lease_key and its vault_key_local_wrapped is vault_key_lease_wrapped, the new format and field name both bound into the lease's encryption; the IndexedDB device store files it under lease^ rather than unlock^. There is no migration: a device's lease from before this release is not read (idbDeviceDB deletes it unread at its next look; a DeviceDB of your own that hands it back sees the vault erase it, with one warning to the logger), so each device asks for the passphrase or passkey once more after the upgrade. The device mark is untouched, and no IndexedDB database or object store changes.
