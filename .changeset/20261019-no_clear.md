A storeBox() over a lockbox the snapshot lists as damaged and naming no revision (a record that is not JSON, say) is now refused as damaged_lockbox, writing nothing, where it retired the record first and then wrote: the gap between those two calls let another device's store, landing between them, be retired unseen. Retire such a lockbox with retireBox() first, then store; a damaged lockbox that names its revision is still written over in one write, carrying nothing forward. A store is now always one write to storage, and it no longer warns through the logger with call: 'storeBox' (only retireBox() warns of a retirement that removed another revision). Nothing stored changes.
