**Stored format and public surface: a vault holds one passphrase and at most one passkey.** The vault props are format `safebyok-vault-v5`, with `passphrase` and `passkey` (or null) in place of the `wayins` list; way-in ids and labels are gone. **Every vault already stored reads `damaged_vault`**: there is no migration before 1.0, so `delDamagedVault` clears it and the user makes the vault again. Convex hosts clear the component's tables before deploying (Convex refuses a schema its documents do not match); Postgres hosts drop `safebyok_boxes` and `safebyok_vaults` and run `VaultTablesSql` again (`passphrase jsonb NOT NULL`, `passkey jsonb`); a GraphQL server deploys its new schema with its clients. The snapshot's `wayins` is replaced by `passphrase` and `passkey` summaries (`PassphraseWayinSummary`, `PasskeyWayinSummary`), on the vault as well. `changePassphrase(passphrase, newPassphrase)`, `addPasskey(passphrase, makePasskey)` and `delWayin(passphrase)` each take the standing passphrase alone as proof (a passkey's answer is refused `bad_option`), so a forgotten passphrase can no longer be reset with the passkey, which still opens the vault. `addPasskey` resolves true, or false when dismissed, and a second passkey replaces the first; `delWayin` removes the passkey. `addPassphrase`, `MaxUnlocks`, `unlock()`'s and `ask.attempt()`'s `{ wayinId }`, the `wayinlabel` validator, and the refusal kinds `last_wayin` and `too_many_wayins` are removed. A passkey's answer on a vault with no passkey refuses `absent_wayin`; one from another passkey, `wrong_passkey`. In safebyok-react, `askPasskey` takes the snapshot's one `passkey` summary, and the provider's `openError` reports a `rolled_back` a later read met, rather than staying at opening with none.
