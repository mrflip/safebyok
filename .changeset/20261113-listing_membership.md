**A deleted vault leaves its bank's list, and lets go of its box listing.** Once a vault's tombstone arrives, here or from another device, the vault leaves `bank.vaultListing`'s list (`bank.vaultAt` still returns the Vault), and the box listing of its live life ends its watch and is let go, its boxes dropping their exposed whispers. The Vault object still stands for its scope: `bank.ensureVault`, or a new life arriving from another device, makes it present again, with a new box listing (call `vault.beginWatch()` for it, as `useVault` does). A Box held from the deleted life answers nothing, as before. **`listing.damaged_rows` is gone**, from every listing: a row that fails its checks is told to `onBadNews` as `damaged_row`, and the thing at its scope leaves the list, as before; a listing's listeners now hear only when its list (its snapshot) changes. `listing.delist` is gone too. **A device's listeners hear of its bank's end only once everything below shows it**: inside the listener, the bank's snapshot is locked and each box's whisper let go. **A bankDB `watch` whose stop function throws** no longer makes `bank.lock()` or `device.sweep()` throw: what it throws reaches `onBadNews` as `unexpected_error` (the logger's warning once the device is disabled), and the lock or sweep finishes. Stored format: unchanged.
