**A deleted vault is disabled, and has no box listing.** Once a vault's tombstone arrives, here or from another device, every call on that Vault (`get`, `del`, `beginWatch`, `getBox`, `putBox`, `delBox`) refuses `vault_gone`, and so does reading `vault.boxListing`; `hydrate` and `ensureTimely` refuse it when they have to fetch, and answer from the tombstone in hand while it is recent. Before, the vault stayed enabled and `vault.boxListing` handed out a fresh listing whose `hello()` watched the deleted scope. The Vault object still stands for its scope: `bank.ensureVault`, or a new life arriving from another device, makes it present and enabled again, with a new box listing (call `vault.beginWatch()` for it, as `useVault` does). A Box held from the deleted life answers nothing, as before. **`listing.damaged_rows` is gone**, from every listing: a row that fails its checks is told to `onBadNews` as `damaged_row`, and the thing at its scope leaves the list, as before; a listing's listeners now hear only when its list (its snapshot) changes. `listing.delist` is gone too. **A device's listeners hear of its bank's end only once everything below shows it**: inside the listener, the bank's snapshot is locked and each box's whisper let go. **A bankDB `watch` whose stop function throws** no longer makes `bank.lock()` or `device.sweep()` throw: what it throws reaches `onBadNews` as `unexpected_error` (the logger's warning once the device is disabled), and the lock or sweep finishes. Stored format: unchanged.
