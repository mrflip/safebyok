safebyok, private: a lease and a wayin answer a Thing's questions. `Lease` gains `label` (its gate's label), `labelpath` (`lease/<label>`), `scope` (its bank's scope and that labelpath), `am` (always `'a'`) and `written_at` (its `proved_at`). `Wayin` gains `labelpath` (`wayin/main` or `wayin/passkey0`), `am` (always `'a'`) and `written_at` (its `born_at`). These are getters alone: nothing stored, checked, stamped or serialized changes, and a check of a Thing's scope still refuses these labelpaths.
