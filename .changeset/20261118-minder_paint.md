Empty: the new `safebyok` is private. What a caller of a `ThingMinder` will see: `isVaultDBNewer` is renamed `dbHasNewer`, and `carOf` takes a second argument, `fullest`, the whole record of the life where `newest` is sparse, making a `behind` car; a `fullest` that is not an earlier whole record of that life refuses `bad_record`. `bestCarOf(prior, delivered)`, the minder's structural sharing, merges two cars of a scope, and `cmpThings` orders two records of one scope. `isStaleAt(scoped, opts)` takes a scope (an ask or a record passes) and the get options, refusing `bad_record` for a scope of another modelkind. A get's `staleTime` accepts `Infinity` and `'static'` (`TsqStaleTime`, exported), and the minder's own accepts `Infinity`. Neither makes a `behind` or `bare` car timely: a car is timely only where the vaultDB has nothing newer and TanStack Query counts it fresh, so an invalidated car, or one whose last get was refused, is stale under `Infinity` and timely under `'static'`, and `get` and `isStaleAt` agree in every case. A finite `staleTime` or `gcTime` past `MaxTimerMillis` (24.5 days; `MaxTimerHours`, 588, beside it, both exported) refuses `bad_record`, saying to give `Infinity`: a longer timer would fire at once. A get's, `isStaleAt`'s and `tryAt`'s options may be given as undefined, for no opinion, where they refused before. `isTombstone` is now true of the void sentinel too, and the new `isGone` is true of a deleted Thing's tombstone alone; `safebyok/storage` exports both. A gone car whose newest is the void sentinel refuses as a broken car. `isWholeRecord` is true of the void sentinel, a whole tombstone. `isGoneLifeseq` is renamed `isTombstoneLifeseq`: it is true of every odd life, the shell's among them. `checkedGetAsk` and `checkedListAsk` are replaced by `AskChecks`, whose `getAsk` and `listAsk` refuse `bad_record`.
