"safebyok-indexeddb": minor

safebyok-indexeddb now runs on the new `safebyok` core, its peer dependency in place of safebyok-big: `IdbVaultDB`, a vaultDB, replaces `IdbBankDB` and `idbBankDB`. It keeps its Things in a database of another name, so it reads nothing the earlier release wrote: on each browser, a user's bank reads as absent, and the old database stays until you delete it. The earlier store lives on, private and frozen, as safebyok-indexeddb-big.
