"safebyok": minor

A first unlock that creates the vault while another device deletes it now unlocks nothing and leaves no lease: a read that finds the vault deleted or replaced while the create is under way stands it down, and the unlock refuses vault_locked (unlock again to create it anew). A read that began before this device's own change to the vault's ways in landed no longer puts the older vault props back over the change's, which locked the device at its next read.
