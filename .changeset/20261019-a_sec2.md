**Public surface: the snapshot and `vault.wayins` no longer carry a passphrase's salt or any wrapped vaultKey.** `VaultSnapshot.props` and `vault.props` are gone, and `vault.wayins` is the snapshot's own list of `WayinSummary`s (it was `Wayin` models): what an offline guess at a passphrase starts from no longer reaches React state, devtools or an error reporter. A host that read the stored record there reads it from its own `VaultDB` (`getVaultProps`), and `Wayin.listOf(vault, vaultProps)` makes the models of it.

A first unlock that creates the vault while another device deletes it now unlocks nothing and leaves no lease: a read that finds the vault deleted or replaced while the create is under way stands it down, and the unlock refuses vault_locked (unlock again to create it anew). A read that began before this device's own change to the vault's ways in landed no longer puts the older vault props back over the change's, which locked the device at its next read. A locked vault's getBox, getWhisper, useBox and delBox refuse absent_box from the box listing in hand, reading storage no more: a box another device stored since the last read is found once staleMinutes have passed, or at once after hello({ staleMinutes: 0 }).

`Lease.grant` checks the vault props it is handed (their `vault_id` and `unlock_epoch`) and `askPassphraseAt` against what a lease record holds, and throws, as misuse, where it used to grant a lease that never opened (a deadline that is no number, say).

In safebyok-react, what `PassphraseModal` and `PasskeyUnlockButton` report to React's `onCaughtError` names the component it came from: a VaultError whose cause was left out says so in its message, and an error that was not the vault's own says where to look (the functions the app handed the dialog, or, for the passkey button, the passkey prompt).

A vault that refuses `rolled_back` now forgets the vault props it held, its state `opening`, so the restore recipe -- erase the device mark (`deviceDB.delMark`), then `hello()` again -- works on the very handle that refused, where it left that handle refusing changes to the ways in as `stale_write`. `RefusalMeta.vault_props_stored` is removed: nothing filled it, and filled it would have carried every wrapped vaultKey and salt.
