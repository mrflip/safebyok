A vault is made, then says hello. openVault(opts) is gone, with no alias: Vault.make(opts) makes the handle at once, synchronously, throwing misuse (bad_label, bad_option, insecure_context) before storage is touched, and starts vault.hello() itself, which reads the vault props and this device's lease and settles the snapshot from opening into absent, locked or unlocked. Where a host wrote `const vault = await openVault(opts)`, it writes `const vault = Vault.make(opts)`, and `await vault.hello()` where it wants to know the vault has settled or why it could not; every call but lock(), lockSoon() and close() waits for hello first, so a call made before storage answered simply waits. OpenVaultOpts is MakeVaultOpts, and Vault is now a value as well as the handle's type. A hello refused no longer leaves nothing behind: the handle stands, its storage watch live, and the snapshot's new field trouble (a VaultError, or null) says why, the state still opening; the vault's logger warns of it ({ call: 'hello' }, where the logger's story said 'openVault'; Vault.make's own notices say 'make'), and calling hello() again, or any other call, tries again. Close the handle to let it go. Once settled, a later hello() reads nothing anew, until a read of storage fails (trouble then says why, cleared by the next read that succeeds), storage the vault cannot trust refuses it (identity_revoked, rolled_back, newer_format), or the vault is retired: its next call then reads storage and the lease afresh, so after such a refusal the next call meets it again rather than vault_locked. A lock() or lockSoon() made while a hello is taking up the lease wins over it. Vault.make refuses an option it does not take as bad_option, naming it, and naming the new name of a retired one (forcePassphraseReentryHours is leaseHours): it used to ignore it, so a retired lease setting quietly gave the seven-day default. React: VaultProvider makes the vault with Vault.make and hands the handle down at once, its snapshot opening until hello settles, where vault used to stay null until the open succeeded; openError is the snapshot's trouble while the vault is still opening, when it is a condition; retryOpen() and retireDamagedVault() have the same handle say hello again rather than open a new one; and a prop Vault.make does not take is thrown to the error boundary as bad_option, where the provider used to drop it. safebyok/testing gains settledVault(opts), Vault.make then hello.
