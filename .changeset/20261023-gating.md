**No vault call takes a passphrase or a passkey's answer any more: every one goes through a gating.** A call that needs the user raises a gating, a discriminated union on `gatingkind` (`initialize_gate`, `unlock_vault`, `change_passphrase`, `put_passkey`, `del_passkey`, `del_vault`), handed to `Bank.make`'s new `requestCleartext` (it replaces `getProof`); the gating's attempt runs the whole change. What a host sees:

* `getProof`, `Ask`, `AskWhyVals`, `ProofAnswerVals`, `'carry_on'` and `lease_ending` are gone; `requestCleartext(gating)` resolves when its dialog closes, and a gating still open then is declined (the call refuses `vault_locked`). `GatingKindVals`, `CorrectableKindVals` and the gating types are exported.
* `vault.unlock()` / `tryUnlock()` are gone. `vault.ensureLease()` is renamed `ensureUnlocked()` (`tryEnsureUnlocked()`); a `renewMinutes` as long as the lease or longer refuses `bad_option`. Before asking the host, its gating takes up a lease another tab granted when it fits, and no longer erases one that does not.
* `vault.putWayin(slotlabel)` (`'passphrase'` or `'passkey'`; on a `novel` vault, the first passphrase), `vault.delWayin(slotlabel)` (`'passphrase'` refuses the new `passphrase_required`) and `vault.del()` (now proven by the passphrase; a `novel` vault refuses `absent_vault`) each raise their gating and resolve once it settles; `putWayin` resolves the Vault, not a way in's summary.
* An attempt's `wrong_passphrase`, `wrong_passkey`, `weak_passphrase` and the new `same_passphrase` leave the gating open; anything else settles it, and every waiting call hears it. A second attempt while one runs refuses the new `attempt_in_flight`.
* A `lock()` now stops a change to the ways in under way: it attaches no lease, the attempt refusing `gating_landed`, though a gate it already stored stays stored.
* `safebyok-react`: `PassphraseModal` and `PasskeyUnlockButton` take a `gating` (an `unlock_vault` or `initialize_gate` one) in place of an `ask`; `BankProvider` takes `requestCleartext` in place of `getProof`, and its own dialog declines the other kinds for now. `allowStartOver` offers nothing: deleting a vault needs its passphrase.

Nothing stored changes shape: vaults and leases written before this release read and open as before.
