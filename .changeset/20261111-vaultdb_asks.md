A vaultDB answer, and a vaultDB refusal's meta, carry `ask` in place of `req_params`: a `LoggableAsk`, the ask's op, address, policies and row revisions, never a record's payload (`loggableAsk` checks it; `ReqParams`, `reqParams`, `requestParams` and `reqParamsOf` are gone, and `crossedReqParams` is `crossedAsk`). `listVaults` and `listBoxes` take asks, `{ op: 'list', applabel, user_id }` and `{ op: 'list', ...vaultScope, vault_tok }`. An adapter's `do*` calls return `VaultStored`, `BoxStored`, `VaultsStored` or `BoxesStored` (were `VaultClassified` and `BoxClassified`), made by `classifyStoredVault`, `classifyStoredBox`, `classifyStoredVaults` and `classifyStoredBoxes` (were `classifyVault` and `classifyBox`); `doListVaults` and `doListBoxes` classify what they fetch, and `BoxesFetched` is `{ world, boxes }`. `boxWorldOf` and `boxVaultActOf` are no longer exported. GraphQL: the answer field `req_params` is `ask`, of type `SafebyokAsk`. Convex: the component's `listVaults`, `getVault`, `listBoxes` and `getBox` take `{ ask }`, and `getBox` and `listBoxes` return `world` where they returned `vault`; a host redeploys its Convex functions with this version. `tidyGone` takes an ask too, `{ op: 'tidy_gone', applabel, user_id }` (`boxTidy` checks it, `BoxTidyAsk` its type), and so does the Convex component's `tidyGone`, as `{ ask }`. A refusal's `meta.call` is `meta.oplabel`, naming the verb that refused, on the wire too (GraphQL's `SafebyokErr.meta`), and the logger's story names its operation as `oplabel` where it said `call`. `revokePasskey` over a vault with no passkey wayin answers the act `passed`, with the row as stored, where it answered `revoked`. An answer's `act` is one of a closed set: `goodActVals` for a good answer, `badActVals` for a refused one, and a refusal the classifier never saw (a bad ask, storage out of reach, a revoked identity) carries the act `unclassified_storing` where it carried its refusalkind, which stays on its `err`. A thing no longer has `receive`: its listing decides what a record received does, and the thing takes it by `receiveRecord` or `markRefreshed`. A listing's `build(record)` and `receiveRow(record)` are one synchronous `deliverRecord(record)`, which throws where `build` rejected. A listing's and a thing's `fetch` and `tryFetch` are gone: `get` and `tryGet` honour `maxMillisSinceRefresh`, and with 0 always ask storage (`box.get({ maxMillisSinceRefresh: 0 })` is the old `box.fetch()`). `bank.tryProbe` takes `maxMillisAtGetBack`, 0 by default, for the get after its put. A watch notice's `act` is `gotlive` or `gotgone`, the acts of a get, where it was `got` or `gone` (`Notice`, and on the GraphQL wire `SafebyokNotice.act`). A refused `void` or a refusal with a null `dbhas` (an `ahead` over nothing stored) where a live thing is listed is told to `onBadNews` as `record_conflict`, beside the refusal thrown.
