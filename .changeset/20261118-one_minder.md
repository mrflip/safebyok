Empty: the new `safebyok` is private. What a caller of a `ThingMinder` will see: one minder minds every Thing in its bank, of every kind, through one watch of the bank, in a TanStack QueryClient it makes and shares with nothing else. `ThingMinder.make(opts, bank)` loses its modelkind argument, and `MinderOpts` loses `tsq`: a host can no longer hand the minder its own client, put an observer on a minder's query key, or invalidate a car, and the refusal `QueryClientExpected` is gone. The `ties`, `modelkind` and `keyPrefix` members are gone, and so are `MinderGetAsk` and `ThingTies`. `get`, `tryAt`, `at` and `isStaleAt` take a scope of any kind in the bank, where a scope of another kind refused `bad_record` (`ModelkindMismatch`, gone); the minder builds each get ask with the bank's `makeGetAsk`, so a caller no longer supplies the ask, its `ifAbsent` or its `track`. `list(labelpath)` lists the children of the Thing at `labelpath`, where `list()` listed under the minder's own kind. `goodbye` empties the cache, and a later `hello` starts minding again with the options the minder was made with. `listen` hears every car, and no longer an `invalidate`. `at`, `get` and `tryAt` type their car by the labelpath's kind where it is known when compiled (`ScopeAt`, `ThingAt`, `CarAt`, and `BankCar` beside `BoxCar` and `GateCar`, all exported), and as any Thing's otherwise. `MinderAskOpts` and `MinderGetAskOpts` are renamed `MinderTryAtOpts` and `MinderGetOpts`, since they qualify `tryAt` and `get`, not an ask.
