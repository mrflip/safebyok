Empty: the new `safebyok` and `safebyok-react-lean` are private at 0.0.0, and `safebyok-big` is untouched. What a caller of the new core will see: **`device.connect()` and `device.disconnect()`** replace every `sweep`. `Device.make` starts nothing; `connect()` starts the VaultMinder's watch of the vaultDB, which runs while the device is both connected and signed in, and has the vault TSQ hear the VaultMinder and refetch the bank's items and listings. `disconnect()` locks the bank, has the vault TSQ stop hearing and stops the watch; it never throws, disables nothing (the device's verbs work, and `connect()` resumes it), and leaves a caller of `ensureUnlocked` waiting for a later unlock. Both are idempotent. **`device.disable()`** ends the device for good: it locks the bank, rejects every caller of `ensureUnlocked` with a `disabled` refusal, and disconnects; every verb on the device's bank, gate, vault or world then refuses `disabled`, a later `connect()` does nothing, and the snapshot shows the stage **`'disabled'`, renamed from `'swept'`** (a host's `case 'swept':` becomes `case 'disabled':`). It never throws, and a second call does nothing. **Gone:** `sweep` on `Device`, `World`, `Bank`, `Gate`, `Lease`, `Vault`, `VaultMinder` and `VaultTSQ`, the `bank_swept` phrase and its thrower; `HostedVault` is `{ connect, disconnect }`. **The vault TSQ** keeps an item or a listing fresh for good only while it hears a watching VaultMinder; otherwise its staleTime is 0. **A watch that throws** at sign-in or at `connect()` goes to `onBadNews`, and the bank comes up, where sign-in failed. **The giver's provider** connects its device on mount and disconnects it on unmount. **Stored format:** unchanged.
