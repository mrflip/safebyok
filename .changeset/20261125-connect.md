Empty: the new `safebyok` and `safebyok-react-lean` are private at 0.0.0, and `safebyok-big` is untouched. What a caller of the new core will see: **`device.connect()` and `device.disconnect()`** replace every `sweep`. `Device.make` starts nothing; `connect()` starts the VaultMinder's watch of the vaultDB, which runs while the device is both connected and signed in, and has the vault TSQ hear the VaultMinder and refetch the bank's items and listings. `disconnect()` locks the bank, has the vault TSQ stop hearing and stops the watch; it never throws, disables nothing (the device's verbs work, and `connect()` resumes it), and leaves a caller of `ensureUnlocked` waiting for a later unlock. Both are idempotent. **Gone:** `sweep` on `Device`, `World`, `Bank`, `Gate`, `Lease`, `Vault`, `VaultMinder` and `VaultTSQ`, the `bank_swept` phrase and its thrower; `HostedVault` is `{ connect, disconnect }`. Nothing now reaches the `'swept'` stage, which stays in `bankStageVals` until the ends are built. **The vault TSQ** keeps an item or a listing fresh for good only while it hears a watching VaultMinder; otherwise its staleTime is 0. **A watch that throws** at sign-in or at `connect()` goes to `onBadNews`, and the bank comes up, where sign-in failed. **The giver's provider** connects its device on mount and disconnects it on unmount. **Stored format:** unchanged.
