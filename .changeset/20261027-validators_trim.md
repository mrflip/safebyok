**The bag naming a vault is strict, and the `Validators` namespace holds only the checks a host or a storage adapter calls.** The first bag of `bank.ensureVault`, `reopenVault`, `clearCachedVault`, `delDamagedVault` (and their `try` forms), `closeVault`, `vaultOf` and `bankVaultOf` holds the `vaultlabel`, and the bank's own `applabel` and `user_id` if you like, and nothing else: any other field refuses `bad_scope` before storage is touched, counted and never named, where it was left out. A `vaultDB` or `deviceDB` put there by mistake is now refused, where the vault was silently born in the bank's own stores; put a vault's own stores in the second bag, `bank.ensureVault({ vaultlabel: 'work' }, { vaultDB: workVaultDB })`. A Vault object, its snapshot, a `BankVault` or a vault record is no longer taken as the bag: name the vault by its label, `bank.closeVault({ vaultlabel: vault.vaultlabel })`. `bank.demandConsistentWith(thing)` still takes anything carrying a scope. This supersedes what other notes in this release say of a field the first bag does not know. `Validators` keeps what is stored or crosses the `VaultDB` and `DeviceDB` seams, the scopes and scopepaths, each token and ciphered field, the options and objects a host hands in and what their callbacks return, and every validator exported by name; the pieces the package builds those from leave it (`BaseShape`, `MadeUnderShape`, `MadeShape`, `sealBoxlabel`, `LeaseGrant`, `VisibleShape`, `CipheredShape`, `LeaseStampPicked`, `VaultStampPicked`, `VaultStampButFormat`, `whisperBag` (use `Sidecar`, the same check refusing `bad_option` where it refused `bad_whisper`), `passphraseCleartext`, `ListedBox`, `StoredRevision`, `ReadAnswer`, `ListingAnswer`, `StoredVaultTok`, `vaultMadeUnderShape`, and `Bank.make`'s per-option checks `maxWhisperBytes`, `maxBoxes`, `declaredBytes`, `declaredBoxes`, `leaseHoursAsMillis`, `staleMinutesAsMillis`, `renewMinutesAsMillis`: check the options whole with `makeBankOpts`). **Stored format:** unchanged.
