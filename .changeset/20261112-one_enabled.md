**The device's lifecycle is the only one.** A bank, gate, vault or box, and each listing, loses its own `sweep()` and `vacate()` and its own enabled flag: `device.sweep()` and `device.vacate()` end everything under the device, and a thing answers while its device does, its bank is the device's, and it is the one listed at its scope; a thing that answers no more refuses `disabled`. `bank.lock()` locks the bank as before, and each lock and unlock now tells a listener once, the bank and its vaults changing together. **A Vault object stands for its scope through its lives**: a vault deleted, or deleted and made again, on any device is the same `Vault` with a new `vault_tok`, `bank.vaultAt(scope)` answers it, and its box listing is made again under the new life; a `Box` or box listing kept from the life that ended refuses `disabled`. A main gate likewise stands through its lives. A Bank is still one life of the bank: its tombstone, or a row of the bank built again elsewhere, leaves `device.bank` null until the next `ensureBank`. **`device.tellBadNews(badnews)` and `device.announce(announcement)`** tell the host's `onBadNews` and `onLifecycle`, as the core's models do. The types `BankFriend`, `DevicePanic` and `DeviceAnnounce` are no longer exported. The default `onLifecycle` vacates the device, not the bank, when a lock's revoke of the cached lease is refused. A put's `expected` row no longer needs a `vault_tok`. **Stored format:** unchanged.
