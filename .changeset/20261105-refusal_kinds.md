The refusal kinds a VaultError carries changed. Sixteen kinds no core call refused with are gone: gate_made_elsewhere, passkey_unavailable, bad_whisper, whisper_too_large, invalid_box, damaged_vault, damaged_wayin, crypto_failed, vault_behind, not_permitted, storage_blocked, gate_moved, no_whisper, passphrase_required, vault_remade and corrupt_record. damaged_record is renamed damaged_row (a row read from storage that fails its checks). The damagekind a listing answers of a row it set aside in damaged_records says damaged_row where it said damaged_record; format_mismatch is unchanged. storage_error is now storage_unavailable; a delete of another life of the vault refuses vault_mismatch (was stale_write), as a live vault under another vault_tok does; unknown_key_deriver is newer_format; invalid_input is bad_option; corrupt_record, a record storage answered under another scope or another vault_tok, and a put answered with nothing are record_conflict. A record handed in that fails its check refuses bad_option, except the record a vaultDB verb is handed, which safebyok prepares and so refuses unexpected_error, its options staying bad_option; one read back refuses damaged_row, and bad_record stays only where one check serves both. Bank.make requires a requestCleartext, with no default, and refuses bad_option without one; a gating attempt holding no proof refuses bad_option; guards that only a bug in safebyok can trip refuse unexpected_error. A host that matched on a removed or moved kind matches on its new one.
