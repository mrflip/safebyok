The refusal kinds a VaultError carries changed. Fifteen kinds no core call refused with are gone: gate_made_elsewhere, passkey_unavailable, bad_whisper, whisper_too_large, invalid_box, damaged_vault, damaged_wayin, crypto_failed, vault_behind, not_permitted, storage_blocked, gate_moved, no_whisper, passphrase_required and corrupt_record. damaged_record is renamed damaged_row (a row read from storage that fails its checks). storage_error is now storage_unavailable; vault_mismatch is vault_remade, as is a delete of another life of the vault (was stale_write); unknown_key_deriver is newer_format; invalid_input is bad_option; corrupt_record, a record storage answered under another scope or another vault_tok, and a put answered with nothing are record_conflict. A record handed in that fails its check refuses bad_option, one read back damaged_row, and bad_record stays only where one check serves both. ensureUnlocked on a bank made without requestCleartext, and a gating attempt holding no proof, refuse bad_option; guards that only a bug in safebyok can trip refuse unexpected_error. A host that matched on a removed or moved kind matches on its new one.
