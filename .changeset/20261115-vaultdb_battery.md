Empty: the new `safebyok` is private, and `safebyok-postgres` exports nothing new. What a host of `safebyok` will see: every vaultDB answer and notice carries `world`, the bank's record spread flat with its main gate as `maingate`, or null. An ask may carry `track: { bank: { revseq, lifepath }, gate: { revseq, lifepath } }`, and its answer then carries `tracked`, which gives for each of the two `'same'` where it matches the one stored, or else the record as stored; an ask with no `track` gets no `tracked`. A store made with `doNotifyTracked` on, its default, also tells the bank's watches of each record in `tracked` that is not `'same'`. What `tracked` holds for a missing or damaged record is unspecified. A box is the root box, at `box`, or one label under it. `safebyok/testing` exports `describeVaultDBBattery`, the cases every vaultDB must pass. `safebyok-postgres` holds a `PostgresVaultDB` that passes them, with its own tables (`safebyok_bank_things`, `safebyok_gate_things`, `safebyok_box_things`) and write functions; it is not exported yet.
