**More types on the front door**: `safebyok` now exports the types its exported signatures name, `AnyGating`, `PassphraseCleartext`, `PassphraseVerdict`, `KeyDeriver`, `StrengthChecker`, `VaultLogger`, `DeviceDB`, `VaultDB`, `RefusalKind` and `RefusalFamily`, so a host can name what it hands `Bank.make` and what a refusal carries without reaching into the package. **Refusals:** a VaultDB's `listBoxes`, `getBox` and `watch` refuse `bad_scope`, where they refused `bad_option`, for a query failing its check, as its other scope verbs do. **Stored format:** unchanged.
