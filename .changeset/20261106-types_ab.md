**The device store on the front door**: `safebyok` exports the `DeviceDB` class, the base a device store extends, and `MemoryDeviceDB`, the one that keeps its rows in memory; `Bank.make`'s `deviceDB` takes either. **More types on the front door**: `safebyok` now exports the types its exported signatures name, `AnyGating`, `PassphraseCleartext`, `PassphraseVerdict`, `KeyDeriver`, `StrengthChecker`, `VaultLogger`, `VaultDB`, `RefusalKind` and `RefusalFamily`, so a host can name what it hands `Bank.make` and what a refusal carries without reaching into the package. `BankOpts`, `KeyDeriver`, `VaultLogger` and the snapshots (`BankSnapshot`, `VaultSnapshot`, `BoxSnapshot`, `GatingSnapshot`) are interfaces with a sentence on each field; `KeyDeriver`'s `deriveKeyBytes` and `load` are readonly function fields, where they were methods, which a class implementing it still satisfies. **Refusals:** a VaultDB's `listBoxes`, `getBox` and `watch` refuse `bad_scope`, where they refused `bad_option`, for a query failing its check, as its other scope verbs do; a box handed to a listing's `build` that fails its check refuses `bad_option`, where it refused `bad_record`, as one handed to `put` already did; a `VaultError` that a key deriver's or strength checker's `load()` throws reaches `bank.hello()`'s refusal as it came, where it was wrapped as the cause of an `unexpected_error`. **Stored format:** unchanged.
