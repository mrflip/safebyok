`GatingModal` answers an `add_passkey` gating, asking for the passphrase and then the browser's passkey prompt, and a `revoke_passkey` gating, asking for the passphrase; so `vault.addPasskeyPrompt()` and `vault.revokePasskeyPrompt()` show the provider's own dialog where they were declined unasked. For a vault holding a passkey, in a browser offering WebAuthn, the unlock dialog offers "Use a passkey" first. New exports: `AddPasskeyModal`, `RevokePasskeyModal`, `ReplacePassphraseModal`, `DelVaultModal` and their props types; `PasskeyUnlockButton`, `offersPasskey` and `PasskeyUnlockButtonProps`; and the WebAuthn helpers `createPasskey`, `askPasskey`, `attemptOnce`, `passkeysAvailable`, with `CreatePasskeyOpts` and `AskPasskeyOpts`. `GatingModal`, `PassphraseModal` and `AddPasskeyModal` take `passkeyRpId`, for passkeys made under a relying party other than the page's domain. The helpers refuse `wrong_passkey`, in words for the user, where no passkey answer can be had (no WebAuthn, no PRF, a browser that failed to ask), `bad_option` for a relying party the page cannot claim, `absent_wayin` for a vault holding no passkey, and `bad_record` for a stored credential id that is not base64url; a dismissed prompt resolves null.
