**Stored format: a cached lease reads as absent, so the user unlocks once more on each device.** A lease is now the bank's, not a vault's: the deviceDB keeps one lease row per bank scope (`user_id`, `banklabel`), at `fmt` 7003, citing the gate it was granted under by `gatelabel`, `gate_tok` and `gate_revision_tok`, and a lease citing an earlier revision of its gate is forgotten. An `IdbDeviceDB` keeps its leases in a database of layout `device-v002-001-001` now, named `safebyok-<dbName>:device-v002-001-001`; an older one is wiped on open. **Exported names:** a `LeaseQuery` is a `BankScope` and a `bank_tok`, where it was a vault scope and a `vault_tok`; a `LeaseScopepath` is `[user_id, banklabel]`; a `DeviceDB` subclass's `doPeek` is handed a `BankScope`. A `StrengthChecker` is handed the bank's scope, `{ user_id, banklabel }`, where it was the vault's. A `WayinHeader` is the header of a gate row's wayin, its `wayinlabel` included. Every wrap names its bank's stamp first: a wayin's ctx binds the bank's stamp and the gate's, and a box's binds the bank's, the vault's and the box's.
