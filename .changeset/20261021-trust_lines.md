**Stored formats: `vault_key_epoch` is gone, and a box's sidecar is a JSON value.** The vault props are format `safebyok-vault-v6`, each box `safebyok-box-v8` and each device mark `safebyok-mark-v5`, none holding a `vault_key_epoch`, and neither a way in nor a box binds one into its ctx. A box holds its sidecar as `sidecar`, the JSON value itself (any JSON value, `null` among them), in place of `sidecar_json`, the text the vault serialized; its ctx binds the sidecar's JSON with keys sorted, worked out at each seal and open, so storage may keep it as JSON of its own (Postgres `jsonb`) and reorder its keys. A stored sidecar nested too deep to check lists its box as damaged, calmly. There is no migration before 1.0: a vault stored before this release reads as `damaged_vault` (never `newer_format`), which `delDamagedVault` clears so the user makes the vault again; a box stored before it lists as damaged; an older device mark reads as none. The snapshot's and the vault's `vault_key_epoch` are removed, `BoxSummary` and `SeenVault` lose the field, and a `BoxExpectation` is `{ vault_id, revision_tok }`: a VaultDB of your own compares only those in a box write, and the contract's line 3.8 is gone. Postgres hosts drop `safebyok_boxes` and `safebyok_vaults` and run `VaultTablesSql` again (no `vault_key_epoch` columns; a box's sidecar is the nullable `jsonb` column `sidecar`, a SQL null for a sidecar of JSON null, and a sidecar `jsonb` cannot hold -- a string holding `\u0000` -- fails the write with Postgres's own error as its cause); Convex hosts clear the component's tables before deploying (a box's `sidecar` is `v.any()`); a GraphQL server deploys its new schema (no `vault_key_epoch` on any type or input; a box's `sidecar` is the new nullable scalar `SafebyokSidecar`) with its clients. **The host's clock is checked once, at `Vault.make`**: one reading must be epoch milliseconds from 1981 to 2255, or make throws `bad_option`; every later reading is taken as it comes, rounded down to whole milliseconds. A later bad reading no longer drops the vaultKey or throws `bad_option`, and a clock that later throws fails the call that read it as `unexpected_error`. `vault.clock` is removed. **The vaultKey lives in one place, the `Lease`, and `Box`, `Wayin` and `Lease` take the vault they are of.** While unlocked, the vault holds an opened `Lease`, which keeps the vaultKey's non-extractable handle in a private field, and wraps and unwraps boxKeys under it (`lease.wrapBoxKey(boxKey, ctx)`, `lease.unwrapBoxKey(boxKeyWrapped, ctx)`); locking drops it. `Lease.grant(vault, standing, vaultKeyPair)` resolves a lease already opened, its deadline the vault's `leaseMillis` from `vault.now()`; `Lease.make(vault, record)` makes one not opened, and `lease.tryOpen(standing)` resolves a new, opened Lease (it reads the time from the vault). A box seals and opens under the lease: `Box.seal(vault, lease, boxlabel, whisper, opts)`, `box.tryOpen(lease)` and `box.open(lease)`, which refuse a lease not opened or ended, and a box (or `over`) of another vault than the lease's, as `vault_locked`, and a `lease` that is no Lease as `bad_option`. A box has its own `vault_id`: the lease's when sealed, and, from `Box.make(vault, record, vaultId = vault.vault_id)`, the vault props it was read under (`absent_vault` when there are none). Ways in are `Wayin.make(vault, identity, record)` and `Wayin.seal(vault, identity, proof, deviceId, vaultKey)`, `identity` the vault props they are one of (new type `VaultIdentity`: their `vault_id` and `registered_at`). Each takes a slice of the vault, new types `BoxVault`, `WayinVault` and `LeaseVault` (and `LeaseStanding`), in place of `BoxVaultProps`, `WayinVaultProps` and `LeaseVaultProps`, which are removed, with `box.vaultProps`, `wayin.vaultProps` and a passphrase way in's `keyDeriver`. **New: `vault.now()`**, the vault's clock read now, in whole epoch milliseconds. **`Box`, `Wayin` and `Lease` hold their stored record once, and show their visible fields once.** Each keeps its validated record, frozen through, in a private field, handed out as `record` (in place of `props`, removed); its visible fields -- what a host decides with -- are its own fields, read from the record, and a spread, `Object.keys`, a log line and `JSON.stringify` (`toJSON()`) show each once, and nothing else. A box shows `boxlabel`, `revision_tok`, `sidecar`, `registered_at`, `written_at` and `vault_id`; its ciphertext, `format`, `whisper_codec` and `whisper_schema_version` are in `box.record` alone, and `JSON.stringify(box)` is no longer its record (hand storage `box.record`). A way in shows `wayinkind`, `device_id` and `registered_at`, and a passkey's `credential_id` and `prf_salt_b64`; its wrap, and a passphrase's `salt_b64` and `key_deriver_label`, are in `wayin.record` alone. A lease shows its scope, `vault_id`, `unlock_epoch` and `ask_passphrase_at`; its `lease_key`, its wrap and its `format` are in `lease.record` alone. `box.summary` and `wayin.summary` are removed: `wayin.toJSON()` is the summary. **`BoxSummary` gains `vault_id`**, so a listed box is a box's visible fields and `damaged: false`; a VaultDB's `listBoxes` lists `ListedSummary`s (a `BoxSummary` but for `vault_id`, which the vault adds). New types `BoxView` and `LeaseView`, a box's and a lease's visible fields. `vault.decryptWhisper(record)` checks a record once, before it looks for a key, and refuses one handed to a vault not made yet as `absent_vault`. **A vault's boxlabel `validators` gate writes alone**: `getBox()`, `getWhisper()`, `useWhisper()` and `delBox()` hold a boxlabel to the floor only, so a name the host's check refuses with nothing under it is now `absent_box`, where it was `bad_label`; `putBox()` still refuses it as `bad_label`. `delBox()` reads no box before it deletes: a box another device deleted first is `absent_box`, as storage answers it, and a delete into a vault deleted elsewhere and not made anew is now `stale_write`, as its doc block said, where it was `absent_box`.
