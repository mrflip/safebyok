What a host of `safebyok` will see: a `ThingMinder` sets a default `queryFn` on its key, so a host's own `QueryObserver` on a minder's `queryKey` fetches through the vaultDB with the bank's get ask, `track` included, and needs no `queryFn` of its own (after `goodbye`, an observer that kept it is refused `disabled`, and one made after has none). An observer's `select` and `placeholderData` now share structure as TanStack's default does wherever the two values are not revisions of one Thing at one labelpath: a selected revtok follows each new revision, and a placeholder follows the box the observer moves to. `at(scoped)` and `isWholeRecordAt(scoped)` refuse a scope in another user's bank, another bank, or of another modelkind, as `bad_option`. A box that `list()` caches sparse is stale before `list` returns, as before; a `listen` listener may hear it timely, then untimely, in that one step (`notes/wontfix.md`). `isVoidSentinel(record)` is exported from `safebyok` and `safebyok/storage`. `gcTime` counts from when no TanStack observer uses an entry: a minder's listener keeps nothing cached.
