safebyok-react's BankProvider now answers every gating through GatingModal, a new export: a dialog per gatingkind, so vault.putWayin('passphrase'), putWayin('passkey'), delWayin('passkey') and del() are asked of the user (the passphrase, and for a passkey the browser's prompt) rather than declined unasked. A refusal the core leaves the gating open for (CorrectableKindVals, same_passphrase among them) shows in place and the dialog asks again. DialogAnswer gains 'deleted'. In safebyok, vault.checkPassphrase is gone: call vault.checkStrength or tryCheckStrength, whose weak_passphrase carries the checker's reason as its message. A declined gating still refuses vault_locked, its message now worded for what was asked ('The passphrase is unchanged.', 'Nothing was deleted.'). Nothing stored changes: a host with vaults written before sees them as before.

Refusal messages a user may see now name the passphrase and passkey together as a "vault unlocker" (gate_moved, stale_write, damaged_wayin, damaged_vault and others); their kinds are unchanged.
