---
"safebyok": minor
---

A Box's stamp (applabel, user_id, vaultlabel, vault_tok, vault_epoch, gate_epoch) is no longer shown by a spread, a key list, JSON.stringify or box.toJSON(): they show the box's visible fields alone (boxlabel, revision_tok, sidecar, registered_at, written_at), and BoxView narrows to match. The stamp still reads as box.vault_tok and the like. A host that read the stamp off a spread or a box's JSON reads it off the box itself. Stored boxes are unchanged: every one already written reads and opens as before.

**The vault's lease is the Vault object's own**: no getter, field or JSON of a Vault object yields it, and `BoxVault` and `LeaseVault` no longer name `lease`. `Box.seal(vault, ...)` and `box.extractWhisper()` seal and open under it inside the package; a Box from one copy of safebyok cannot seal or open under a Vault object of another copy (it refuses `vault_locked`). What an app reads of the lease sits in one block of getters: `vault.askPassphraseAt`, when it ends and the vault locks of itself (for an autolock, or a "locks in 10 minutes" line), and `vault.leaseMillis`, how long one lasts.

`lock()`'s, `putBox()`'s and `delBox()`'s docs now name the waits a lock interrupts (`getProof`; for a `putBox()`, also `VaultDB.getBox`, `box.extractWhisper()` and `Box.seal`). The write itself, once sent, lands or refuses as storage decides.
