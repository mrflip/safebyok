`DeviceDB`'s lease verbs take and answer a `KeptLeaseRow`: a `LeaseRecord` as before, or a lease row of the new vault models (`fmt` 7001, `ModelsLeaseFormat`, carrying its vault's `applabel`, `user_id` and `vaultlabel`), so one deviceDB keeps both while the models are built. On the get side, `getLease` and `tryGetLease` answer a `KeptLeaseRow`: a host that reads the answer as a `LeaseRecord` narrows it with `leaseRecord.parse`. On the put side, `putLease` and `tryPutLease` take any `KeptLeaseRow` and resolve the row as handed, typed as handed, so a call with a `LeaseRecord` still resolves a `LeaseRecord`; the `doPutLease` an adapter writes is handed a `KeptLeaseRow` and stores it as it is, as before (an override typed for `LeaseRecord` still compiles). No stored row changes, and a row that is neither still reads as absent and is erased. `keptLeaseRow`, `KeptLeaseRow` and `ModelsLeaseFormat` are exported. Nothing else ships: the models' vaults are born unlocked, unlock by proof or by the lease the device keeps, and lock, but nothing under src/models/ is exported yet.
