The Bank's options and lifecycle move to a new **`Device`**. **`Device.make(opts)`** replaces `Bank.make`: it does no I/O, takes no `device_tok` (the device asks `deviceDB.ensureCachedDevice` for its row), and requires `gates: ['main']`, which must be exactly that for now. **`await device.ensureBank()`** replaces `bank.hello()` and resolves to the `Bank`; `device.bank` is null until then and after a sweep or vacate. On a ready device, `device.ensureBank()` returns the bank it has and does not list the vaults again, where a second `bank.hello()` forced a reset. On the device now: `vacate`, `sweep`, `isEnabled`, `autolockMinutes`, `openGating`, and `subscribe`/`getSnapshot` with a `DeviceSnapshot` (`stage`, `err`, `openGating`, `bank`). The `Bank` keeps `ensureVault`, `vaultAt`, `lock`, `autolock`, `tryProbe` and `device_tok`, and its `BankSnapshot` loses `stage`, `err` and `openGating`. Renamed exports: `BankOpts` is `DeviceOpts`, `BankEvent` is `DeviceEvent` (its `bank` is `device`), `BankStage` is `DeviceStage`, and `BadNews.bank` is `device`; `DefaultOnBadNews`, `DefaultOnLifecycle` and `tellBadNewsTo` are exported, and `deviceOpts` from `safebyok/storage`. A host sees fewer paints: a bring-up paints only `ready`, with no `made` stage shown locked first, and a vacate paints `disabled` once. **React:** `BankProvider` makes the device and fills in `gates`, and no longer waits on the deviceDB before making it; `useDeviceTok` is gone. `useBank()` answers null until the bank is up. New hooks: `useDevice()`, whose `vacate()` signs out whether or not the bank is up, and `useDeviceSnapshot()`. A refused `ensureCachedDevice` no longer reaches the error boundary: it reaches `onLifecycle` as a refused `helloed`, logged at warn by default, and the device stays `made`.
