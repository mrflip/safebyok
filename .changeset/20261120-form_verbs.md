Empty: the new `safebyok` is private, and `safebyok-big` is untouched. What a caller of the new core will see: **the Bank's gating machinery is gone** -- the `Gating` class, `Device.make`'s `requestCleartext` (now refused as an unknown option), `guardRequestCleartext`, `device.openGating`, `DeviceSnapshot.gating` and `OpenGatingSnapshot`, the `launch…Prompt` verbs, and the `tryAttempt…`/`attempt…` re-entry points. **In their place, a form verb per change, each a pair:** `Bank.birth(device, cleartexts)`/`Bank.tryBirth`, `bank.unlock`/`tryUnlock`, `bank.replacePassphrase`/`tryReplacePassphrase`, `bank.addPasskey`/`tryAddPasskey` and `bank.revokePasskey`/`tryRevokePasskey`: the plain form throws its refusal, the `try` form resolves an `Outcome`. Each spends a passkey answer whatever the outcome, and a refusal changes nothing and never unlocks. A passphrase replacement or a passkey's revocation is proved by the passphrase or the passkey (`{ passphraseClear }` or `{ passkeyClear }`, beside `nextPassphraseClear` for a replacement), and every gate change leaves the bank unlocked under a lease citing the gate it wrote; adding a passkey takes `{ passphraseClear, nextPasskeyClear }`. Revoking where the gate has no passkey unlocks by the proof and writes nothing. **`device.judgeStrength(passphrase)`** is the strength checker's verdict, for a live meter. **`bank.ensureUnlocked()`** no longer asks anyone: on a locked bank it sets the snapshot's new **`unlockWanted`** and waits, every caller sharing one wait, which any unlock or gate change resolves, **`bank.dismissUnlock()`** rejects `dismissed`, and a sweep rejects with the bank's end cause. `vault.withWhisper` waits the same way. **Stored format:** unchanged.
