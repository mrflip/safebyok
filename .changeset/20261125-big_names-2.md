safebyok-react is now the React binding of the new `safebyok` core, its peer dependency in place of safebyok-big: `reactGiver(config)` and its hooks, made once at module scope, replace `BankProvider` and the `useBank`, `useVault`, `useBox` and `useWhisper` hooks; form fields are on `safebyok-react/parts`, and ready-made forms on `safebyok-react/kit`. Nothing of the earlier interface carries over. The earlier binding lives on, private and frozen, as safebyok-react-big.
