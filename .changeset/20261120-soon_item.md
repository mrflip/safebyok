Empty: the new `safebyok` is private at 0.0.0, the giver is on no entry of `safebyok-react`, and `safebyok-big` is untouched. What a caller of the new core will see: **the `ThingMinder` caches records, not cars.** Each Thing has an item, its whole record, tombstone or shell, and, only while the item is behind it, a soon: a sparse record newer than the item. **`minder.get`, `tryGet` and `at`** answer the item's record itself (typed `ThingAt<L> | Tombstone<ThingAt<L>>`), never a `{ newest, fullest, liveness, agerank }` car; a list's sparse record no longer shows in `at()`. **`minder.soonAt(scoped)`** is new: the sparse record newer than the item, or undefined; a write over the Thing cites `soonAt(scoped) ?? at(scoped)`, the soonest record, as `vault.putBoxOnce` and `delBoxOnce` do given a labelpath. **`tryAt`'s answer** carries `record` (the item's, however old) where it carried `car`; it answers `untimely` beside a newer sparse record, as before, under every staleTime. **`listen`'s `ThingEvent`** carries the item's record as `state.data`, its revision fields from that record, and no `agerank`; when the item falls behind a newer sparse record it tells `action: { type: 'invalidate' }`, once, with `state.isInvalidated` true, and none where a refused get already left it invalidated. **`listen` tells every event of the cache but its observers'**: a query `added`, each `updated`, and `removed`, which keeps the last `state`, `data` included. **TanStack's action is passed whole**, as a frozen copy typed from TanStack's own (`success` with its `data` and `manual`, `error` with its refusal, `fetch`, `invalidate` and the rest), and is undefined for `added` and `removed`. **Every event carries an `eventkind`**, `'item'`, `'soon'` or `'list'`, to narrow on: **`ThingMinder.isThingEvent` is gone**. A soon's events are a `SoonEvent` (its sparse record as `state.data`, `removed` once the item catches up). Every event carries the query's **`queryHash`** beside its `queryKey`, and never the query itself. **A newer sparse record lands in the soon, and then the item is invalidated**: a listener on the soon's `success` finds `soonAt` answering the soon and `isBehindAt` true, and the item's `invalidate` follows at once, though the item's own `state.isInvalidated` may read false in that listener. **A `get` fetches an item behind**, as from inside that listener, unless the caller gives a `staleTime` of its own; an item marked invalidated it fetches whatever the staleTime. `SoonEvent`, `RecordEventFields`, `MinderEventkind`, `MinderActionOf`, `MinderActionTyped` and `TSQQueryAction` are exported. **Behind is a comparison:** an item is behind while a soon newer than it is cached, or a soon with no item, which **`minder.isBehindAt(scoped)`**, new, answers; so a read made inside the `success` of a whole record that catches up the soon answers `gotlive`. **A whole record, tombstone or shell older than the item** that comes by a notice or a write's answer is dropped: the item keeps its record and its time, and `listen` tells nothing. One at the item's revision lands, makes the item fresh as of then, and tells `success`. A get's late, older answer still marks the newer item fresh (notes/wontfix.md). **A staleTime of `'static'` is refused**, for a call and for `queryCacheOpts`: `Infinity` is the longest. **Query keys** put the kind of entry after the bank: `[..., 'item', ...labels, '!']`, `[..., 'soon', ...labels, '~']`, `[..., 'list', ...labels, '#', 'kids']`. `ThingMinder.queryKeyOf` is renamed **`itemQueryKeyOf`**, `minder.queryKey` **`itemQueryKey`**, `minder.listingQueryKey` **`listQueryKey`**, and `minder.soonQueryKey` is new. **Gone:** `descendantsQueryKey` (no one prefix covers a Thing's descendants of every kind; it returns per kind when nested boxes need it), `ThingCar`, `BankCar`, `BoxCar`, `GateCar`, `CarAt`, `CarFreshness`, `carLivenessVals`, `carOf` and `dbHasNewer`; callers use `ThingAt` and the record types. **`newerThingOf(prior, next)`** is new: the newer of two records of one Thing by life then revision, `prior` on a tie. **The giver** (unpublished): `useBoxRecord`'s `data` is the box's record, or null where none is stored or it was deleted, as before; a `select` of the host's is handed the whole record, tombstone or shell, where it was handed the car, and so is `boxRecordQueryOptions`' `data`. `boxCarQueryOptions` is `boxRecordQueryOptions`, `boxCarQueryKey` `boxRecordQueryKey`, and a giver's bank's `getBoxCar` and `listenBoxCars` are `getBoxRecord` and `listenBoxRecords`. The host's entry is stale only while the bank's minder has the item behind (its staleTime is a function, 0 then and `Infinity` otherwise), so a host fetch or `setQueryData` that lands while the item is behind leaves it stale. The bridge invalidates it when the minder caches a newer soon, and when the item falls behind, and marks it stale without refetching when a get of the item is refused; a soon's invalidation leaves a refetch under way running. A giver's bank's `listenBoxRecords` hears every event of a box's item and its soon, `GiverEvent` carries their `eventkind`, its `type` may be `'added'` and its `action` any of TanStack's, and the bridge acts only on an update; `useBoxRecord` reads a tombstone or the shell by `Thing.isTombstone`. `GiverEvent` is no longer generic and carries the box's `userid`, `banklabel` and `labelpath`, with `state.data` typed `unknown`, and the bank has **`isBoxRecordBehind(labelpath)`**. **`MinderEventFormat`** is new, and `listen` takes it as its `version` in place of `ThingFormat`. **`minder.isStaleAt(scoped, opts)`** answers true when the item is not timely for a `get` with these `opts`: a `staleTime` given wins over the item's being behind, and an invalidated item is stale whatever the staleTime, so inside a listener on a soon's `success` it answers false under `staleTime: Infinity`, where it answered true. **`vault.forceTimelyUnlocked(labelpath, { staleTime? })`** is new: it gets the box while it waits for the bank to be unlocked, and once both have settled rejects with the `unlock` gating's refusal, else the get's refusal as it came, else as `withWhisper` refuses a box it cannot unseal. **`vault.withWhisper`** unseals the box's item, never its soon: it hands `fn` the box as stored at a moment during the call, which may be behind a revision of the same life heard of since, where it refused the sparse record; it refuses `stale_read` (new, with its own phrase, "This device has heard of a newer revision of the box but has not fetched it yet.") where the newest revision heard of is a sparse record of a newer life, or only a sparse record of the box is cached; and `absent_box` for a deleted or never-stored box. Where its get is refused: with nothing at all cached for the box it rejects with the get's own refusal, such as `storage_unavailable`; with a record of the box cached it unseals the item, or judges the cache as above. **Stored format:** unchanged.
