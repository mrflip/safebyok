Every call that needs the vault unlocked (putBox, getBox, getWhisper, useBox, delBox) now goes through the new vault.ensureLease() and tryEnsureLease(): when the vault is locked, or not yet made, it hands an Ask to Vault.make's new getProof option and waits for its answer, one prompt at a time. With no getProof the call refuses vault_locked, as before. New exports: Ask, AskWhyVals, ProofAnswerVals, and the types AskWhy, GetProof and ProofAnswer. A lock(), close() or del() while a prompt is up refuses every call waiting on it vault_locked and settles ask.lockLanded; an attempt under way then takes nothing up. putBox now checks its whisper, label and sidecar before asking for anything. In safebyok-react, VaultProvider is the vault's getProof unless given one (its new getProof prop; null for none): its PassphraseModal shows for any call that needs the vault, only while the user's gesture is in hand. useWithUnlockedVault and the WithUnlockedVault type are removed: call the vault directly, and a dismissed dialog refuses the call vault_locked rather than resolving null. PassphraseModal and PasskeyUnlockButton take an ask in place of a vault, and onDone hears 'unlocked' or 'declined' in place of a boolean; storage out of reach now closes the dialog, as does starting over, after which the next call asks to choose a passphrase. A call under way when the provider closes its vault now rejects rather than resolving null.
