**onLifecycle replaces onPanic.** `onPanic`, `PanicHandler`, `Panic` and `DefaultOnPanic` are gone from safebyok and from BankProvider's props; `onLifecycle`, `LifecycleHandler`, `BankEvent` (and `BankAnnouncement`, `BankAnnounce`, `EraseSite`) and `DefaultOnLifecycle` take their place. `onLifecycle` hears a `BankEvent` by `kind`: `lease_unerased` for every cached lease that outlived its erase (`where`: `lock`, `tombstone`, `vacate` or `outdated`); `vacated`, with its `cause` and the `unerased` count, once a vacate's erase settles; and `helloed`, with the outcome of each `bank.hello()` and `vault.hello()`. A revoked identity now vacates the bank whatever your handler does. A host with its own `onPanic` renames it `onLifecycle` and reads the event's `kind`: a revocation arrives as `vacated` with `cause` `identity_revoked`, a lease left behind as `lease_unerased` with `refusal`. A bank row a vacate cannot forget now reaches `onBadNews`, and an outdated row's refused erase reaches `onLifecycle` rather than only the logger. `vault.hello()` refuses a foreign throw as `unexpected_error`, as `bank.hello()` does. `onBadNews` and `DefaultOnBadNews` are unchanged. **safebyok-react:** BankProvider and `useVault` no longer tell `onBadNews` of a refused hello (`onLifecycle` hears it as `helloed`), and `useVault` answers `{ stage: 'spent', vault: null, cause }` once the bank is swept or vacated, where it said `waiting` for good; `VaultStage` gains `'spent'`.
