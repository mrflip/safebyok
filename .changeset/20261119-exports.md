Empty: the new `safebyok` is private, and nothing published reaches what changed. What a host will find on each entry of the new core:

- **`safebyok`** exports everything `safebyok/core` and `safebyok/storage` export, the same values, beside the `ThingMinder`, `carOf`, `dbHasNewer` and the minder's shapes, `DefaultStrengthChecker` and `guardStrengthChecker`, and from safebyok-big `correctablekindVals`, `Modelkind`, `MinuteMillis`, `ForeverMillis`, `PasskeySaltSize`, `PassphraseSaltSize`, `MaxLabelChars`, `MaxUserIdChars`, `Patterns`, `UserID`, `toB64`, `fromB64`, `mask` with its `MaskOpts`, `mash` and `WhisperFieldAttributes`. It no longer withholds `Lease`, `Gating`, `Gate`, `Wayin`, `BaseBox` or `guardRequestCleartext`.
- **`safebyok/core`** keeps the device, bank, gate, gating, wayin and lease without the minder or TanStack Query. It now exports `Lease` as a class, and every type its classes' signatures name: the vaultDB's answers and asks, `StrengthChecker`, `GuardedStrengthChecker`, `GuardedKeyDeriver`, `ProvingOpts`, `ProvingParts`, `QueryCacheOpts`, `ThingFieldname`, `ThingOptions`, `WriteAskFlags`; and from safebyok-big `VaultError`, `isVaultError`, `IsVaultError`, `refusalkindVals` and the refusal types with `Capacity`, `DefaultLogger`, `setDefaultLogger`, `KeyDeriver`, `KeyDeriverLabel`, `VaultLogger`, `Outcome`, `GoodOutcome`, `BadOutcome`, the wayin headers and records, `Wayinkind`, `PassphraseSalt`, `PrfSalt`, `CipherCtxRecord`, `PrfAnswerSize`, `PasskeyAnswer`, `PassphraseCleartext`, `PassphraseVerdict`, `Sidecar`, `Whisper`, and the acts and ask options a vaultDB's answer names.
- **`safebyok/storage`** gains what an adapter reached into safebyok-big for: `Awaitable`, `GotAct`, `PutAct`, `DelAct`, `WriteAct`, `GoodAct`, `RefusedAct`, `IfAbsent`, `IfPresent`, `PutIfAbsent`, `StoragePolicy` and `storagePolicyVals`; the throwers `storageIssue`, `storageUnavailable`, `storageFailed`, `unexpectedError`, `identityRevoked`, `sanitizeForeignError` and the phrase `fetchFailed`; `VaultError`, `isVaultError` and the refusal types with `Capacity`; the checker makers `Checker`, `Checkers` and `objCheck`, with `ObjCheckOpts`, `Refusing`, `Story` and `ConsistencyRefusal`; and `LiveOf`, `ShellOf`, `TombstoneOf`, `ThingFieldname`, `ThingOptions`, `WriteAskFlags`.
- **`safebyok/browser`** is new: what `safebyok-big/browser` exports (the Argon2id derivers, the IndexedDB device database, the zxcvbn strength checker, `browserOpts`), except that its `StrengthChecker` is `safebyok`'s. It is on no other entry.

The proving classes (`Proving`, `PassphraseProving`, `PasskeyProving`, and `NOVEL`) remain on no entry as values; `safebyok/core` exports their types, `NOVEL`'s as `Novel`. Nothing stored changes.
