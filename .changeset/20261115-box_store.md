Empty, as the new `safebyok` is private. What a host of `safebyok/storage` will see: the vaultDB keeps the bank, its gates and its boxes as Things, each kind by its labelpath (`bank`; the main gate at `gate` and further gates at `gate/<label>`; the root box at `box` and boxes below it), and a lifepath has no leading `/` and one lifeseq per segment of its labelpath. A write ask is a `put` or a `del` and names its `modelkind`; `tryListThings`, `tryBirthBank` and the ask builders `getAsk`, `listAsk`, `putAsk` and `delAsk` are new. Stored gate Things at `gate/main` with a `/`-led lifepath now fail safe, since nothing reads older shapes before 1.0: the main gate at `gate` reads as absent, and the old row at `gate/main` refuses as `damaged_row`.
