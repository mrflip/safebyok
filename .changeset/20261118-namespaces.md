Empty: the new `safebyok` is private. What a caller will see: each check comes through a bundle, and a helper through the object of its type's name. `ThingChecks` keeps the kind-generic checks and carries `byKind` (today's `KindChecks`), `ofLabelpath` (`kindChecksOf`), `ThingFormats` and `thingkindVals`; `BankChecks`, `GateChecks` and `BoxChecks` hold each kind's `record`, `cited`, `stored`, `policies` and `labelpath`, with a gate's `wayins` and `storedWayins` and a box's `sealable`, `live` (`boxRecord`) and `liveOrShell` (`boxRecordOrShell`). `bankThing`, `gateThing`, `boxThing`, `bankCited`, `gateCited`, `boxCited`, `boxSealable`, `boxRecord`, `boxRecordOrShell`, `thingScope`, `thingCited`, `thingRecord`, `storedCited`, `storedWayins`, `sealed` and `wayins` are no longer exported on their own. `AskChecks` takes in `loggableAsk`, and `thingWatchScope`, `watchArgs` and `trackShape` are no longer exported on their own. `StampChecks` holds `bankScope`, `bankStamp`, `gateStamp`, `stampedGate`, `boxStamp`, `leaseStamp`, `leaseRecord` and `LeaseFormat`; `MinderChecks` holds `queryCacheOpts`, `tsqStaleTime`, `minderFriend`, `minderListener`, `minderScope`, `minderLabelpath`, `minderTryAtOpts` and `minderGetOpts`; `GateishChecks` holds `gateRevisionedStamp`, `provingDevice` and `strengthChecker`; `BankishChecks` holds `deviceRecord`. `DefaultQueryCacheOpts`, `MaxTimerMillis` and `gatingkindVals` are carried by their bundles and still exported on their own. `adapterVaultDBOpts` is private to `adapter-vault-db.ts`; `AdapterVaultDBOpts` is exported as before. `Lifeseq` has `ofLifenum`, `lifenumOf`, `isTombstone`, `ofLifepath` and `leadingOf` (`lifeseqOfLifenum`, `lifenumOfLifeseq`, `isTombstoneLifeseq`, `lifeseqOfLifepath`, `leadingLifeseqsOf`); `Labelpath` has `kindOf` (`modelkindOf`), `isOfKind` (`isLabelpathOfKind`), `labelOf`, `parentOf`, `MainGate` (`MainGateLabelpath`) and `RootBox` (`RootBoxLabelpath`); `Shell` has `of` (`shellOf`), `Cited` (`ShellCited`), `childCited` (`childShellCited`), `VoidLifeseq`, `RootVoidLifepath`, `labelpathWithParentage` and `lifepathWithParentage`. `safebyok/storage` exports `Lifeseq` and `Labelpath` as values, and `Classifier` in place of the named list of `thing-classifier` (`Classifier.worldOf`, `Classifier.classifyGetThing` and the rest, with its types). No alias stays under an old name. `safebyok-react` and `safebyok-postgres` read the new names.
