What a host of the React layer will see: **`putBoxMutationOptions(boxNamed, { whisperOf, sidecarOf })`** on the bank giver, the mutation twin of `putBoxAction`, for a host that writes its form as a TanStack mutation: `useMutation(Knock.putBoxMutationOptions('jokes', opts))`, then `put.mutate(form)`.
