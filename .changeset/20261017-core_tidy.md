**More lockboxes fit under a backend's vault limit.** `openVault` now plans 1 KiB, not 2 KiB, for each lockbox's fields beside its whisper and public bag when it checks that `maxLockboxes` lockboxes fit the adapter's `maxVaultBytes`. On Convex's 8 MiB, the default `maxWhisperBytes` admits 102 lockboxes (was 101), and a `maxWhisperBytes` of 2048 admits 1638 (was 1365). Nothing stored changes; a `maxLockboxes` that opened before still opens.
