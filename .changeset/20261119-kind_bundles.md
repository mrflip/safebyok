Empty: the new `safebyok` is private, and nothing published reaches what changed. Each kind's bundle now carries its stamp and the asks that are its alone, beside what it carried: `BankChecks` has `record`, `cited`, `stored`, `policies`, `labelpath`, `stamp` (the bank's stamp) and `birthAsk` (a bank's birth ask, which leaves its three records unchecked); `GateChecks` has the same five, `wayins`, `storedWayins`, `stamp` (a gate's stamp) and `stampedGate`; `BoxChecks` has the same five, `sealable`, `live`, `liveOrShell` and `stamp` (a box's stamp). Each carried check is the same check `StampChecks` and `AskChecks` still hold. The three bundles come from the new module `shapes/shapes-thingkinds.ts`, on the same entry points as before (core, storage, front door). `ThingChecks.byModelkind` and `ThingChecks.ofLabelpath` move to the new bundle `ThingkindChecks` (`ThingkindChecks.byModelkind`, `ThingkindChecks.ofLabelpath`), and give the fuller bundles. No check changes what it accepts; nothing stored changes.
