safebyok (the new core, private): a Bank is made whole. `Bank.make(record, { device, vaultDB, gate })` takes its main gate made, and refuses one made for another bank's stamp (`record_conflict`, naming the fields) or something other than a Gate; the record is the bank's Thing with its root box at `rootBox`, which must be live: a shell or a tombstone of the root box refuses (`bad_record`), so there is no unborn Bank. `BankMakeRecord` no longer carries `maingate`. `Bank.makeRecord(stamp)` takes a bank stamp and returns the first record of the bank's Thing alone; `BankDna` is gone. A birth makes one gate, unlocks it once, seals the root box under it and hands it to the Bank it stores; where the vaultDB's answer is broken (its bank's revtok or its root box; the answer's gate is not read), that gate is swept and no bank is made. A device's `hello` makes the gate from the stored gate record, then the bank with it, locked. `Phrases.GateExpected` is new. Nothing stored changes, nor what a lease or wayin binds. Nothing published changes.
