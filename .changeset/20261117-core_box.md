Empty, as the new `safebyok` is private. What a host of `safebyok` will see:

- A third party naming a bank and gate's stamps names them `bankStamp` and `gateStamp` (`PassphraseProving.buildNovel(device, bankStamp, gateStamp, cleartext)`, `wayinCtxOf(bankStamp, gateStamp, header)`).
- `safebyok/core` exports `CoreBox`, a class of statics that seals a box's whisper and makes its revisions. A lease takes the sealer itself and refuses `bad_option` for a sealer missing a member; `sealerkindVals`, `Sealerkind`, `SealerKinds`, `PriorOf`, `RecordOf` and `CleartextsOf` are gone.
- A seal takes the sealable it is made from, the props in the clear and the secret props: `lease.seal(sealer, donorProps, publicProps, secretProps)` and `bank.sealBox(donorProps, publicProps, secretProps)`, for example `bank.sealBox(box, { sidecar }, { whisper })`. The sealable (`Sealable<BoxRecord>`) may be a whole box, sealed or unsealed, a sparse one, a tombstone or the shell: only its Thing fields and sidecar are read. The sidecar comes from `publicProps` or else the sealable, and a seal with neither refuses `bad_option`. `written_at` may be given; it is signed, and a new life is born at it.
- `publicProps` of `{ am: 'x' }` seals the tombstone, as `bank.tombstoneBox(donorProps, { written_at })` does. A tombstone is written at the `written_at` given, or now, and it is signed. Secret props given with `{ am: 'x' }` refuse `bad_option`, for any sealer, and so does a sidecar given for a box's tombstone: neither is ignored. The tombstone of a tombstone or of the shell refuses `bad_option`.
- `lease.unseal(sealer, record)` and `bank.unsealBox(box)` answer the record with its whisper in place of the sealed one (`BoxUnsealed`), where the lease answered the cleartexts alone.
- `BoxSparse` and `boxSparse` are gone: `Sealable` / `boxSealable` is what a seal is made from, and `boxStamp` / `BoxStamp` is the stamp a box's seal binds. `safebyok/core` and `safebyok` export `Sealable` and `BoxPublicProps`; `safebyok/core` also exports `withoutSecrets` and the sealer's record types.
- `bank.rootBox`, and the `rootBox` of `BankMakeRecord`, is the root box whole (`BoxRecord`), or its shell before the birth (`BoxShell`); a root box given as its Thing fields alone refuses `bad_option` at `Bank.make`. `safebyok/storage` exports `boxRecord` and `boxRecordOrShell`, which check it.
- The gate revision a lease is granted for is a `GateRevisionedStamp` (`proving.buildLease(bank, gate)`, `bank.unlockByProving(proved, gate)`), exported from `safebyok/core` in place of the lease's `GateCited` / `gateCited` (was `CitedGate` / `citedGate`). `gateCited` in `safebyok/storage` is the cited record of a gate write, as before.
- `lease.unseal` and `bank.unsealBox` build their answer from the record as the sealer checks it, never from the object given.
- A lease's stamp field `gatelabelpath` is `gate_labelpath`. A lease record written before refuses `bad_option` at `Lease.make`, its gate labelpath at a field no lease has; and since the lease's ctx binds the new name, a bankKey wrapped under the old one would not unwrap (`damaged_lease`). A box's seal binds the same bytes as before, and every box already stored still unseals.
