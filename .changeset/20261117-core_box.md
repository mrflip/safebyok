Empty, as the new `safebyok` is private. What a host of `safebyok` will see: a third party naming a bank and gate's stamps names them `bankStamp` and `gateStamp` (`PassphraseProving.buildNovel(device, bankStamp, gateStamp, cleartext)`, `wayinCtxOf(bankStamp, gateStamp, header)`). `safebyok/core` exports `CoreBox`, a class of statics that seals a box's whisper and makes its revisions: `CoreBox.nextRevisionOf(prior)` and `CoreBox.tombstoneOf(prior)`. A lease takes the sealer itself, `lease.seal(sealer, nextRevision, cleartexts)` and `lease.unseal(sealer, record)`, and refuses `bad_option` for a sealer missing a member; `sealerkindVals`, `Sealerkind`, `SealerKinds`, `PriorOf`, `RecordOf` and `CleartextsOf` are gone. Nothing stored changes: every ctx binds the same bytes.
