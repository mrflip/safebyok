The body of each Lease and Box try pair lives in the throwing method -- `Lease.cipheredWith`, `Lease.decipherWith`, `Lease.sealProps`, `Box.seal` and `box.extractWhisper` -- and the `try` form is derived from it, so a throw that is no `VaultError` (a bug) in a throwing method's own lines leaves it as it was thrown, where it used to arrive wrapped as 'unexpected_error'. `Box.seal`, `box.extractWhisper` and `Lease.sealProps` still reach the step below them through its `try` form, so a bug in that step still arrives wrapped; the `try` forms still resolve 'unexpected_error' for every bug, and every refusal kind and its order are unchanged. The core's `KeptLease.read` (not exported) is `KeptLease.tryRead`, as it resolves an Outcome.
