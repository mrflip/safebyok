The body of each Lease and Box try pair lives in the throwing method -- `Lease.cipheredWith`, `Lease.decipherWith`, `Lease.sealProps`, `Box.seal` and `box.extractWhisper` -- and the `try` form is derived from it, so a throw that is no `VaultError` (a bug) leaves the throwing form as it was thrown, where it used to arrive wrapped as 'unexpected_error'; the `try` forms still resolve 'unexpected_error' for it, and every refusal kind and its order are unchanged. The core's `KeptLease.read` (not exported) is `KeptLease.tryRead`, as it resolves an Outcome.
