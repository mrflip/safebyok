Empty: the new `safebyok` and `safebyok-idb-lean` are private at 0.0.0, and `safebyok-postgres` does not export `PostgresVaultDB`, whose `doStats` is the one change in a published package. What a caller of the new core will see: **`vault.stats()`** answers the bank's storage stats, `{ box: { count, live_count, size }, gate: { count, live_count, size }, max: { count, size } }`, and **`vault.tryStats()`** answers them as an Outcome; neither needs the bank unlocked. `count` is every Thing stored, tombstones included, `live_count` the live ones and `size` their bytes. `max` is the store's limits, never `Infinity`: a store with none reports `StoreLimitCap`, 19,006,492,568. A store's limits are at least two boxes and `StoreLimitFloor` (5,000) bytes: an adapter that reports less refuses `storage_issue`, and IdbVaultDB refuses `storage_blocked` at its first call where the browser offers the page under `StoreLimitFloor` bytes. Nothing refuses a write for being over a limit. A vaultDB answers them with **`tryStats(ask)`**, and a concrete adapter implements `doStats`; `StatsAsk`, `StatsAnswer`, `StatsActual`, `VaultStats`, `ThingkindStats`, `StoreLimits`, `VaultStatsChecks`, `StoreLimitCap`, `StoreLimitFloor`, `StoreLimitLeastBoxes`, `storeLimitsOf`, `vaultStatsOf`, `rowWeightOf` and `FiledRaw` are exported. **Stored format:** unchanged.
