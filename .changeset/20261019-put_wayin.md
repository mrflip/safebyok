**Public surface: `putWayin` and `delWayin` are the two calls that change a vault's ways in, and the strength checker is optional.** `vault.putWayin(passphrase, newProof)` replaces `changePassphrase` and `addPasskey`: a new passphrase as text replaces the passphrase; a passkey's answer (`PasskeyAnswer`, from safebyok-react's `createPasskey`, made before the call) puts the vault's passkey, replacing any. The call no longer takes a function that makes the passkey, so a passphrase refused after the passkey was made leaves that passkey on its authenticator, a way into nothing. `putWayin` and `delWayin(passphrase)` each resolve the vault, unlocked (`tryPutWayin`, `tryDelWayin` the same as an `Outcome`); a blank new passphrase is refused `weak_passphrase`. `Vault.make`'s `passphraseStrengthTest` is renamed `strengthChecker` (type `StrengthChecker`) and is optional: left out, every new passphrase is refused as too weak and the vault logs once, at error level, that a checker must be chosen (the new CUSTOMIZATION.md). `checkStrengthTest` is `parseStrengthChecker`, handing back that default for none. A checker that throws is refused `unexpected_error` carrying nothing of what it threw (a condition it threw used to pass through). `safebyok/browser`'s `zxcvbnPassphraseTest` is `zxcvbnStrengthChecker`, and answers weak, saying to try again, when its dictionary fails to load, where it rejected `storage_unavailable`. `Wayin.sealPassphrase`, `sealFirstPassphrase` and `sealPasskey` are one `Wayin.seal`, resolving the way in and a session handle on the vaultKey. `safebyok/testing`'s `anyPassphraseTest` is `anyStrengthChecker`.
