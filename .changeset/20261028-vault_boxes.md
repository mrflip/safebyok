A vault's box calls read as one method each, with no behaviour a host relies on changed. Two edges move: a getBox, getWhisper, putBox keeping its whisper, or delBox on a locked vault whose listing does not show the box now answers at once (null, 'absent_box' or 'no_whisper') while another kind of gating (a putWayin, delWayin or del) is pending, where it waited that gating out first; and when the Vault object goes gone while getBox, getWhisper (and so useWhisper), putBox or delBox waits on storage, the call refuses 'vault_gone' without the logger's 'moved on' line. The vault's `log` and `warn` for storage it cannot trust (a user signed out, an older copy of the vault) now name the call that met it in their story, `{ call: 'getWhisper' }` or `{ call: 'refresh' }` for a read the vault made of itself, where they said `{ call: 'untrustedStorage' }`; the bank's warning at a refused birth keeps `untrustedStorage`. A host filtering its logs on that tag sees the vault's under the calls' names. `useWhisper` on a Vault object gone refuses 'vault_gone' before it checks the callback, as every other call does; a throw from the callback still rejects as it came. Storage ringing while the vault reads now has it read once more after that read, every ring meanwhile sharing the one read, where a ring joined the read under way: a change made elsewhere just after a read began now shows, at the cost of a second `getVault` when rings come during a read. A read that lists a box otherwise than this Vault object's own putBox or delBox left it while the read was under way is ignored, so a box this Vault object just deleted, or wrote over, no longer reappears in its snapshot as the older read listed it. A `hello` or `refresh` asked for while a read is under way still shares it. **Stored format:** unchanged.
