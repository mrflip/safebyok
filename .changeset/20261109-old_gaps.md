**Vaults have default caps, a lost create race refuses, and blocked storage has its own refusal.** A vault holds 100 live boxes and a box record 5,600 bytes unless the vaultDB is made with `maxBoxes` or `maxRecordBytes`; `IdbVaultDB` takes `maxBoxes`. A `buildVault` that loses to a vault made elsewhere refuses `gate_made_elsewhere` instead of answering the winner's vault locked. A browser withholding IndexedDB refuses `storage_blocked` where it refused `storage_unavailable`. `DefaultLogger` and `setDefaultLogger` are exported. `bank.probe()` trials the whole storage path at startup.
