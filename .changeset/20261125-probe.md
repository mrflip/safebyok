Empty: the new `safebyok` is private at 0.0.0, and `safebyok-big` is private too; big's `RefusalMeta` gains an optional `stepkind`, which nothing in big fills. What a caller of the new core will see: **`vault.probe(asked?)` and `vault.tryProbe(asked?)`**, a self-test of storage and keys on an unlocked bank: it puts a box (by default at `box/safebyok_probe`), gets it back from storage, unseals it and checks its whisper, and deletes it, resolving a `ProbeReport` of each step's milliseconds and `whisperMatched`, with no whisper in it. A locked bank refuses `bank_locked` before anything is written, and never asks for an unlock. A step's refusal is rethrown as a refusal of the same kind whose `meta.stepkind` names the step (`put`, `get`, `unseal`, `del`), the step's refusal its cause. Exported beside it: `ProbeChecks`, `ProbeLabelpath`, `probeStepkindVals`, and the types `ProbeAsked`, `ProbeReport`, `ProbeStep`, `ProbeStepkind`. Unlike safebyok-big's `bank.tryProbe`, it takes no vaultlabel or freshness options, always asks storage for the get, deletes its box, and its steps carry no rows. **Stored format:** unchanged.
