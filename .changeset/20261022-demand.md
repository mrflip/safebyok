**A call no longer catches up with storage: it demands that the vault still stands as it was, and refuses when it does not.** A Vault object carries a `stamp` (`{ applabel, user_id, vaultlabel, vault_tok, vault_epoch, gate_epoch }`, the exported `Stamp`), and `vault.demandConsistentWith(thing, { leaseBound? })` (with `tryDemandConsistentWith`) refuses, synchronously, a box, lease or stamp the vault has moved on from: **`vault_gone`** (deleted), **`vault_remade`** (deleted and made anew elsewhere), **`gate_moved`** (its passphrase or passkey changed), or `vault_locked` (no lease in hand, when lease-bound). Each call makes that demand at its own steps, so a call racing a deletion, a remake or a change of the ways in now refuses with one of the three new kinds where it refused `vault_locked`: an `unlock()` proven against a vault deleted, made anew or re-gated meanwhile, and a `getBox()`, `getWhisper()`, `useWhisper()` or `putBox()` whose vault was deleted or made anew while storage read the box. **A stale write reads nothing after it**: `putBox()`, `delBox()`, `putWayin()`, `delWayin()` and `del()` refuse `stale_write` as storage gave it, the snapshot unchanged until the next `hello()`; a `putBox()` into a vault deleted or made anew elsewhere is `stale_write` (it was `vault_locked` once a catch-up relocked the device), and its message now says "another call, here or on another device". A `putBox()` called again reads the box afresh, so it lands without a hello. A landed `putBox()` or `delBox()` sets the listing from what storage stored, reading nothing; `putBox()` judges `vault_full` from the listing in hand, never reading first. **`vault.refresh()`** (and `tryRefresh()`) reads now; a refresh or hello asked for while a read is under way shares it, whatever its `staleMinutes`, and so does the storage watch's ring. **Only the first hello takes up this device's lease**; a later read drops the lease in hand, from memory only, when this device's store no longer holds it (a lock in another tab), the ways in changed, or the vault was deleted or made anew, and `ensureLease()` takes up another tab's lease before it prompts. A read older than the vault props in hand (one served before this tab's own write) is ignored, never refused `rolled_back`; `rolled_back` is met by a Vault object made anew. **`lock()` locks before it returns**: the lease dropped, the prompt landed and the snapshot `locked`, then the erase it awaits. The expiry drops the lease from memory and erases nothing. A cancel landing while an attempt writes its lease leaves that lease in this device's store, and the next `ensureLease()` takes it up. **Vaults stored before** read as they did: nothing stored changes shape.
