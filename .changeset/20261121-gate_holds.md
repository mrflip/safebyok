safebyok (the new core, private): the `Gate` superintends a bank's gating. The lease, the awaited `unlock` gating (`ensureUnlocked`, `dismissUnlock`, `isUnlockWanted`), the form verbs `unlock`, `replacePassphrase`, `addPasskey` and `revokePasskey` with their makers and try twins, `lock`, `sweep`, `seal` and `unseal` are on the gate: call `bank.gate.unlock(…)`, which resolves the gate. `bank.gate` replaces `bank.maingate`, and is the same object across a gate change, its props updated. `Bank` keeps pass-throughs for `lock`, `ensureUnlocked`, `dismissUnlock`, `isLocked`, `isUnlocked` and the birth (`Gate.birthGating` is the birth's maker), and gains `trySealBox`, `tryUnsealBox`, `sealBoxEnsuringUnlock` and `unsealBoxEnsuringUnlock`. `Gate.make` takes `{ device, vaultDB, bankStamp }`; a lease is granted to a gate (`LeaseGate` replaces `LeaseBank`), and binds the same bytes. A Thing models one life, and carries `subscribe`. The gate's put ask carries no `track`, which nothing read. `BirthOpts` and `GateBank` are gone. A birth makes its Bank once the bank is stored.
