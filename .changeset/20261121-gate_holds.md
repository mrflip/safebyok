safebyok (the new core, private): the `Gate` superintends a bank's gating. The lease, the awaited `unlock` gating (`ensureUnlocked`, `dismissUnlock`, `isUnlockWanted`), the form verbs `unlock`, `replacePassphrase`, `addPasskey` and `revokePasskey` with their makers and try twins, `lock`, `sweep`, `seal` and `unseal` are on the gate: call `bank.gate.unlock(…)`, which resolves the gate. `bank.gate` replaces `bank.maingate`, and is the same object across a gate change, its props updated. `Bank` keeps pass-throughs for `lock`, `ensureUnlocked`, `dismissUnlock`, `isLocked`, `isUnlocked` and the birth (`Gate.birthGating` is the birth's maker), and has four seal verbs: `sealBox` and `unsealBox`, passing through to `gate.seal` and `gate.unseal` for a box of the bank's own, and their Outcome twins `trySealBox` and `tryUnsealBox`. `bank.sealBox` resolves the sealed box, no longer an ask that writes it: the vault builds its put and del asks itself. `bank.tombstoneBox` is gone: `bank.sealBox(box, { am: 'x' })` seals the tombstone, and `vault.delBoxOnce` writes it. `SealedBoxAsk` is gone. `Gate.make` takes `{ device, vaultDB, bankStamp }`; a lease is granted to a gate (`LeaseGate` replaces `LeaseBank`), and binds the same bytes. A Thing models one life, and carries `subscribe`. The gate's put ask carries no `track`, which nothing read. `BirthOpts` and `GateBank` are gone. A birth makes its Bank once the bank is stored. Nothing stored changes. safebyok-react-lean, private, makes its gate gatings on `bank.gate`. Nothing published changes.
