**Deleting a vault asks nobody.** `vault.del(vault)` deletes an unlocked vault with no prompt, and refuses `vault_locked` on a locked one, as a box put does; nothing locks first, so a refused delete leaves the vault as it was. `vault.delVaultPrompt()` and `tryDelVaultPrompt()` are gone, and with them the `del_vault` gatingkind, its entries in `CleartextsForGatingkind` and `VaultForGatingkind`, and its `requestCleartext` call: a host's dialog no longer sees that kind. A vault already deleted refuses `vault.del` as `vault_locked`. **`vault.hello()` is `vault.beginWatch()`**, and `tryHello()` is `tryBeginWatch()`; the `helloed` lifecycle event keeps its name. **React:** `DelVaultModal` is a plain confirm the host mounts: its props are `vault`, which must be unlocked, and `onDone(deleted)`, called with true once the vault is deleted and with false on Cancel or Escape; it has no passphrase field, and a refusal shows in the dialog. `BankProvider` no longer renders it for a gating. **Stored format:** unchanged.
