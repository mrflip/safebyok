**Names are the package's own to check, `bad_scope` replaces `bad_label`, and classic Zod 4.6.7 is the peer.** `Bank.make`'s `validators` option is gone, with `safebyok/validators`, `VaultValidators`, `NameValidator`, `DefaultValidators`, `AnyTextValidators`, `NameFloor`, `NameFloorBytes` and `checkValidators`; `BankProvider` refuses a `validators` prop as `Bank.make` refuses any option it does not take (`bad_option`). An applabel, vaultlabel and boxlabel are each a `label` (lowercase words of letters and digits joined by single underscores, 2 to 40 characters), and a user id a `user_id` (1 to 100 ASCII letters, digits and `-_.~:@+|%`, never dots alone, where `~` and `%` are new and `..` is now refused); percent-encode (RFC 3986) a name outside them. `Label` and `UserId` are exported as `label` and `user_id`, beside `LabelPattern`, `MaxLabelChars`, `MaxUserIdChars` and `UserIdPattern`. Every check that used to say `bad_label` says `bad_scope`, and its message names each failing field with what is wrong (`boxlabel: A label is ...`), up to eight, quoting no name; `meta.fieldnames` lists the same fields, for a form to mark. Every `VaultDB` verb and every record read back holds names to the same checks, where it held them to the 1 KiB floor: a vault, box, lease or device record stored under a name outside them reads as damaged, and a box stored under such a name (by a host whose validators admitted it) is no longer listed or reachable; `vault.del()` or `delDamagedVault` still removes it. `zod` is a peer at `^4.6.7` in `safebyok` and `safebyok-indexeddb`, and the core now bundles classic Zod beside `zod/mini` (about 15 kB more, brotlied, on `Bank`).
