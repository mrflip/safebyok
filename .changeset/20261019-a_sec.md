An ask's answer now ends its attempts: an attempt still running when the user cancels (or after a second submit, once the first unlocked) unlocks nothing, so a lock() after a Cancel stays locked; ask.attempt after the answer refuses vault_locked at once. A hello's lease take-up takes nothing up when the vault's ways in change (a new unlock_epoch) or the vault is deleted and made anew on another device while it unwraps the lease; more generally, a read that finds the vault's ways in changed, or the vault deleted or remade, takes that up at once, so no take-up or unlock finishing meanwhile leaves the vault unlocked under the old ones. A key deriver or passphraseStrengthTest that throws now refuses the call unexpected_error, what it threw the cause, even when it threw a VaultError of misuse (bad_option, say); a condition it raises, such as storage_unavailable, still comes through as it was. In safebyok-react, what PassphraseModal and PasskeyUnlockButton hand React's onCaughtError carries nothing the user typed: a VaultError arrives without its cause, and a throw that is no VaultError (a passphraseAdvice's, say) as a fixed unexpected_error. The dialog's trouble notice now closes as a lock lands and answers unlocked when the vault unlocks under it. VaultProvider moves to the next user's onBadNews and getProof only after it has let the last user's vault go.
