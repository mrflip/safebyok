Empty, as the new `safebyok` is private. What a host of `safebyok` will see: a bank is made with a VaultDB beside its device (`Device.make` requires `vaultDB` among its opts, and `Bank.make` takes `{ device, vaultDB }` and a record holding its `rootBox`), and stores its boxes through it with `bank.tryListBoxes()`, `bank.tryGetBox({ labelpath, ifAbsent })` (where `ifAbsent: 'shell'` gives the shell of a box not stored), `bank.tryPutBox({ cited, record })` and `bank.tryDelBox({ cited, record })`, none of which throws on a refusal. The bank's stamp is `{ userid, banklabel, lifeseq, born_at }`, with no `bank_tok`, and the lease's record is fmt 7101. Wayins and leases stored before fail safe, since nothing reads older shapes before 1.0: a wayin no longer unwraps the bankKey, and refuses as damaged, and an older lease fails its check. A box under another life of the root box refuses `record_conflict` at `bank.sealBox`, `bank.tombstoneBox` and `bank.unsealBox`.
