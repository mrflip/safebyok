**Renamed:** `isVaultCondition(err)` is `isCondition(err)`, in `safebyok` and as `safebyok-react` uses it; it answers as before.

**`staleMinutes` defaults to 5** (was 1): with neither `Vault.make` nor the call saying otherwise, a `hello()` within five minutes of the vault's last read of storage reads nothing anew. `StaleMinutes` is 5. A host that wants the old pace passes `staleMinutes: 1`.
