|  | drafting | **G494** A key pair per way in (HPKE), so that rotation does not sign every other device out — NOT YET, the Coach moved it to TODO. This is open question 1 of the rotation design. A remaining envelope can't be re-wrapped around a new vaultKey without its own key (Argon2id over the passphrase, or HKDF over the passkey's answer), and the vault keeps neither. So the design keeps only the ways in proven during rotation and removes the rest, and every other device's remembered unlock lapses. The alternative gives each way in a key pair: its private half wrapped under that way in's key, its public half in the envelope. Rotation then wraps the new vaultKey to each public key, and nothing has to be re-proven. That is HPKE (RFC 9180), or ECDH plus HKDF plus AES-GCM over P-256 or X25519 in WebCrypto. It is a new construction in a design VOCABULARY calls "everything is symmetric", so it needs the Coach's yes; it can be added later as a new envelope field. It is also what would let a device lockout (G495) leave the other devices signed in. The Coach ruled in the big\_feats doc: move it to a TODO section and land the best for now.
