A cross-tab answer needs a shared signal, such as a BroadcastChannel or a deviceDB generation; it was out of scope, and is noted in `notes/security.md`. Separately, a losing unlock's undoing erase is unconditional: if a second unlock on the same handle, begun after the lock, wrote its record in between, that is erased too, and the device asks for the passphrase at its next open (the safe direction). Doing better needs a compare-and-erase, which the `DeviceDB` interface lacks.
