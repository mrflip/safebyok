# Interface

The seams of Box, Vault, Gate, Lease, Proving, Gating, Bank and Device as they are meant to be: the design of `notes/design-proof.md`, laid out atoms first, with entrypoints under Public. A bank has one gate, one lease and one lock: its vaults and boxes are locked when it is. `lock` is the bank's, and `sweep` and `vacate` the device's; each is a no-op where there is nothing to do. The device caches only leases and the device row, not bank, gate, vault or box records, and no refresh timer runs. useWhisper is postponed, not removed. The goal: when lock is called, nothing crosses it.

Removed from the interface, and so absent from every table: every `cleartexts` parameter (`ensureVault(bag, cleartexts)`, `ensureUnlocked(cleartexts)`, the gate verbs'), `takeUpLease`, `takeUpCachedLease`, `vaultListing.close`, `makeStaged` and the staged record, `UnlockAtBirth`, `BirthLockSeq`, `closeSeqs`, the lease mutex, the unlock join and the ensuring map, `hydrateAll`, `attemptPasskey` (an `attempt({ passkeyClear })`), `ensureFullAndTimely` and `buildCachedLease`.

* 💪 sound (always true): all records are valid and mutually consistent: bank, gate, vault, box, listing, lease, wayins, gatings
* ⏳  timely: refresh age was within limit at some point between call and first seam; parents are also 😎 timely
* 🎫 received: listing has memoized instance, sparse, full, or tombstoned
* 🗂️ listing: factory and index of storables; has their subscriptions, delivers bankDB responses; to delist is to remove from its list
* ❗️ whole: record is either full or a tombstone
* 😎 whole and timely return value: the full record as stored in BankDB at end of transaction
* 💯 full: record is listed and fully fetched
* ☑️ sparse: record is listed but **not** full
* 📥 staged: uncommitted whole record, returned and not kept anywhere
* 📌 present: record is known, as full or sparse (not tombstoned or void)
* 🔐 locked: no lease attached to the bank, and its cached row forgotten. Impossible to unseal until next proof
* 🎬 unlocked: the bank was unlocked for critical period (see individual doc)
* ❌ vacated: the device disabled, its bank locked, every lease of the bank scope cached on the device forgotten, and its device row; all calls refused
* 🧹 swept: the device disabled and its bank locked; all calls refused; like vacated but the cached lease and device row not deleted
* 🪦 gone: tombstoned in BankDB; the instance leaves its listing's list, and is kept at its scope for a new life; a bank is disabled too, a vault or a box stays enabled
* 🔲 void: absent (no row)
* 🛡️ no-throw: all errors caught;  `tryVerbNoun`  returns an Outcome<Noun>
* 🌀 misaligned: something is wrong enough that a useful error report is hard to give
* 💣 err=refused: oops
* 🫱 ok=got
* ⚠️ ok=expected:
* 👌 ok=success (general)
* 📗 ok=read
* 👍 ok=modified: modification was successfully transactionally applied, no changes since last update
* 💸 decrypted: whisper is authenticated against the bankKey of the bank's lease, the boxKey, the bank, vault and box stamps, and its non-sidecar metadata
* 🫆 encrypted: whisper cannot be decrypted without the device, and is prohibitively difficult to decrypt even with it.
* 🛵 sidecar:
* ☢️ hot stuff: secret key, cleartext or preimage
* 🥶 frozen: object's public properties cannot be modified, or new ones added, for its lifetime
* 📣 subscribed: full records are pushed when they change.
* 1️⃣ one-at-a-time: overlapping callers wait on single request and share its result
* 💾  saved
* ❎ deleted
* 🟰 no change
* 📸 snapshot: frozen pojo with public props for subscribers
* 🔑 wayin
* *️⃣ any
* 🗳️ box: has whisper, scoped by user_id/banklabel/vaultlabel/boxlabel and for most things bank_tok and vault_tok
* 🏦 vault, scoped by user_id/banklabel/vaultlabel and for most things bank_tok and vault_tok
* 🏛️ bank, scoped by user_id/banklabel and for most things bank_tok; has the one gate, lease and lock
* 🫥 disabled:  every guard throws. A bank is disabled once its device is, or once its row is a tombstone or of another life; a vault and its box listing with their bank, and a box listing also once its vault has moved to another life
* 📱 deviceDB: this device's database of cached leases, by bank scope and bank_tok, and the device row

Given the seam and the row, everything to its right is guaranteed.

* The columns left of the seam give what must be true when the call starts, and are filled in on the method's first row only: 🎬 unlocked; 🏛️, 🏦 or 🗳️, the object acted on, with ⏳ where it must be timely; 1️⃣ one at a time. A blank means not required. A ? marks a sigil that was inferred, where neither the old cell nor the gating file says so.
* The columns right of the seam: act, the outcome's name where it has one; ok; post, for the listing and then the object, whether each changed and to what; ret, the return value, or its properties where they matter; note.
* Rows and columns with nothing to say are left out. Proving and Gate write nothing, so they have no post column.

## Vault listing

VaultListing's factory and commit seams. An instance stands for its scope through its lives: a newer record of another life is received by the vault listed, not by a new instance. A deleted vault stays listed, as a tombstone under a new `vault_tok`, so that a late answer is ignored in favour of it. Its box listing, made under the old `vault_tok`, no longer answers: its watch stops and its boxes drop their exposed whispers, because the bankDB deletes the box rows in a cascade and no client deletes them one by one.

## Lease

## Areas of Concern

Eyebrow raisers from the Coach's replies in the sprint's Closer Look (`whiteboard/20261103-unrace/unrace-closerlook.md`).

* **Reflective installs:** `tattoo`, `adorn`, `decorate`. "only part of that is purposeful".
* **Fire-and-forget:** one `void` async IIFE and ten `void` calls, on error and notification paths. "invesrigate after sprint not good".
* **Callbacks and null sentinels:** `Device.#disable(cause)` takes the cause its snapshot's `err` shows, and `#endCause` is null while the device is enabled. "don't use null as a sentinel".
* **`try*` and throw forms:** round trips run both ways: the gate listing's verbs are `valOf` over their `try` form, while `device.tryEnsureBank` and `bank.tryEnsureUnlocked` are `outcomeOf` over the throw form; which form has the body.
* **Guards:** 32 `demandEnabled()` sites, where it should be called only from the `demandReadyToFoo` guards.
* **Rebirth:** the box's rebirth machinery, a new `box_tok` minted in `#editAndSeal` over a tombstone, is a little sus.
* **`bank.ensureUnlocked()` and its callback:** it takes no argument, but reads the device's `requestCleartext`, and without one its gating opens and waits for whoever holds `device.openGating`. "why a public method is getting that callback".
* **`box.whisper`'s verbs:** `expose`, `unexpose`, `veil`, `unveil` and `patchWhisper` keep or forget the whisper, and seal nothing. Only `expose` demands unlocked; `unexpose` and `veil` have no guard on a disabled box, and `patchWhisper` merges into a whisper the bank's lock hides.
