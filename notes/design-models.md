# Interface

The seams of Box, Vault, Lease, Proving, Gate, Gating and Bank as they are meant to be: the design of `notes/design-proof.md`, laid out in the conventions of `notes/design-vaultdb.md` (atoms first; entrypoints under Public). `lock`, `sweep` and `vacate` exist on every object down the chain, and are a no-op where there is nothing to do. The device caches only leases and the device record, not vault or box records, and no refresh timer runs. useWhisper is postponed, not removed. The goal: when lock is called, nothing crosses it.

Removed from the interface, and so absent from every table: every `cleartexts` parameter (`ensureVault(bag, cleartexts)`, `ensureUnlocked(cleartexts)`, the gate verbs'), `takeUpLease`, `takeUpCachedLease`, `vaultListing.close`, `makeStaged` and the staged record, `UnlockAtBirth`, `BirthLockSeq`, `closeSeqs`, the lease mutex, the unlock join and the ensuring map, `hydrateAll`, `attemptPasskey` (an `attempt({ passkeyClear })`), `ensureFullAndTimely` and `buildCachedLease`.

* 💪 sound (always true): all records are valid and mutually consistent: vault, box, listing, lease, gate, wayins, gatings
* ⏳  timely: refresh age was within limit at some point between call and first seam; parents are also 😎 timely
* 🎫 received: listing has memoized instance, sparse, full, or tombstoned
* 🗂️ listing: factory and index of storables; has their subscriptions, delivers vaultDB responses; to delist is to remove from its list
* ❗️ whole: record is either full or a tombstone
* 😎 whole and timely return value: the full record as stored in VaultDB at end of transaction
* 💯 full: record is listed and fully fetched
* ☑️ sparse: record is listed but **not** full
* 📥 staged: uncommitted whole record, returned and not kept anywhere
* 📌 present: record is known, as full or sparse (not tombstoned or void)
* 🔐 locked: lease deleted from vault and cache. Impossible to unseal until next proof
* 🎬 unlocked: vault was unlocked for critical period (see individual doc)
* ❌ vacated: locked, whisper cleared, all calls refused, delisted, frozen
* 🧹 swept: all calls refused, delisted; like vacated but cached lease not deleted
* 🪦 gone: locked, vacated, removed from VaultDB
* 🔲 void: absent (no row)
* 🛡️ no-throw: all errors caught;  `tryVerbNoun`  returns an Outcome<Noun>
* 🌀 misaligned: something is wrong enough that a useful error report is hard to give
* 💣 err=refused: oops
* 🫱 ok=got
* ⚠️ ok=expected:
* 👌 ok=success (general)
* 📗 ok=read
* 👍 ok=modified: modification was successfully transactionally applied, no changes since last update
* 💸 decrypted: whisper is authenticated against cached vault key, box key, vault, and its non-sidecar metadata
* 🫆 encrypted: whisper cannot be decrypted without the device, and is prohibitively difficult to decrypt even with it.
* 🛵 sidecar:
* ☢️ hot stuff: secret key, cleartext or preimage
* 🥶 frozen: object's public properties cannot be modified, or new ones added, for its lifetime
* 📣 subscribed: full records are pushed when they change.
* 1️⃣ one-at-a-time: overlapping callers wait on single request and share its result
* 💾  saved
* ❎ deleted
* 🟰 no change
* 📸 snapshot: frozen pojo with public props for subscribers
* 🔑 wayin
* *️⃣ any
* 🗳️ box: has whisper, scoped by applabel/user_id/vaultlabel/boxlabel and for most things vault_tok
* 🏦 vault, scoped by applabel/user_id/vaultlabel and for most things vault_tok, often gate_epoch
* 🫥 disabled:  every guard throws
* 📱 deviceDB: this device's database of cached leases and the device record

Given the seam and the row, everything to its right is guaranteed.

* The columns left of the seam give what must be true when the call starts, and are filled in on the method's first row only: 🎬 unlocked; 🏦 or 🗳️, the object acted on, with ⏳ where it must be timely; 1️⃣ one at a time. A blank means not required. A ? marks a sigil that was inferred, where neither the old cell nor the gating file says so.
* The columns right of the seam: act, the outcome's name where it has one; ok; post, for the listing and then the object, whether each changed and to what; ret, the return value, or its properties where they matter; note.
* Rows and columns with nothing to say are left out. Proving and Gate write nothing, so they have no post column.

## Vault listing

VaultListing's factory, lifecycle and commit seams. An instance is replaced only when vacated, swept, or created over a gone or void row. A deleted vault alone stays in its slot, as a tombstone, so that a late answer is ignored in favour of it. Its box listing is vacated, with its boxes disabled and delisted, because the vaultDB deletes the box rows in a cascade and no client deletes them one by one.

## Lease

## Areas of Concern

Eyebrow raisers from the Coach's replies in the sprint's Closer Look (`whiteboard/20261103-unrace/unrace-closerlook.md`).

* **Doc blocks:** "listing every refusal possible is foolish, wastes tokens and metastasizes". Stripped to `SLOPDOC` lines; the methods that would not go in a line are in `whiteboard/20261102-newdesign/sus-paranoiacs.md`, the stripped text in `whiteboard/20261102-newdesign/docblocks-slop.md`.
* **Fate:** one record judged in three layers, `slotStepOf`, `reconcileCentral` and `receive`. "the fate code is going to get out of control". Done: the vault listing's `slotStepOf` is the one override of the listing's judgment (`receive`, `keep` or `remake`), and `Listing.deliver` makes a slot plan first and carries it out. Left: the receive table still judges inside `Storable.reconcileCentral`, after `slotStepOf` has judged the same record.
* **Reflective installs:** `tattoo`, `adorn`, `decorate`. "only part of that is purposeful".
* **Fire-and-forget:** two `void` async IIFEs and four `void` calls, on error paths. "invesrigate after sprint not good".
* **Callbacks and null sentinels:** `#disable`, a parameter that mostly selects a branch. "don't use null as a sentinel".
* **`try*` and throw forms:** `valOf` inside an `outcomeOf`, round trips; which form has the body.
* **Guards:** 25 `demandEnabled()` sites, where it should be called only from the `demandReadyToFoo` guards.
* **Sparse vault and rebirth:** `Vault.receiveWorld` leaves a vault sparse the draft says cannot exist; the box's rebirth machinery is a little sus.
* **Weak tests:** stub worlds stand in for a real `vault.lock()` and a real `receiveWorld`.
* **`vault.ensureUnlocked()` and its callback:** it takes no argument, but reads the bank's `requestCleartext` off its world, and without one refuses `vault_locked`. "why a public method is getting that callback".
* **`box.whisper`'s setter:** it exposes, or forgets by null or `LOCKED`, and seals nothing. Only `= { val }` demands unlocked, and `= null` has no guard on a disabled box.
