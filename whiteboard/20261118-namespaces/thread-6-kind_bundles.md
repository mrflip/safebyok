* **Decisions made**:
  - **Module name `shapes-thingkinds.ts`, bundle `ThingkindChecks`**: STYLE.md smushes `kind` into one word, and `Thingkind`/`thingkindVals` are the code's names for the kinds of Thing; a bare "kinds" could be refusal kinds or wayinkinds.
  - **`byModelkind` and `ofLabelpath` move to `ThingkindChecks`, not stay on `ThingChecks`.** They must give the full bundles, which shapes-thing cannot import. Moving `ThingChecks` whole up would cycle (shapes-stamps, shapes-asks and shapes-thing's own Shell read its generic checks), and two bindings both named `ThingChecks` (a lower one and an extended upper one) would leave go-to-definition guessing. A fourth bundle in the new module is the plainest.
  - **The lower modules read `PerKindChecks` directly**, the handoff's second option, rather than a second set of lower per-kind bundles: one set of per-kind bundles, and each row in shapes-thingkinds says where its check is made. The cost is in shapes-asks, which reads `PerKindChecks.bankThing.parse(record)` where it read `BankChecks.record.parse(record)`.
  - **`policiesByModelkind` lives in shapes-thing** because `isPolicyOwn` in shapes-asks needs it and `Validator.Checkers` takes only Zod types, so it cannot ride in `PerKindChecks`. It is exported beside `PerKindChecks`, like `ThingFormat` and `thingkindVals`, and is on no entry point.
  - **Bundle keys**: `stamp` on all three, `stampedGate` on the gate, `birthAsk` on the bank (the pattern `stamp` sets: the kind is the bundle's).
  - **No kind-pinned asks.** Nothing in src parses a get, list or write ask of a fixed kind (`AskChecks.getAsk`/`listAsk` are read only by adapter-vault-db.ts, for any kind), so none was added; the generic asks stay on `AskChecks` alone. `birthBankAsk` is the one ask that belongs to a kind.
  - **Left on `StampChecks` only**: `bankScope` (the scope everything under a bank shares, not the bank Thing's own check) and the lease's checks (a lease is not a kind of Thing). Left on `AskChecks` only: `thingOptions`, `trackShape`, `thingWatchScope`, `watchArgs`, `loggableAsk`, all kind-generic.
* **Deviations** (each a contract question):
  - **`ThingChecks.byModelkind` and `ThingChecks.ofLabelpath` are gone from `ThingChecks`**; they are `ThingkindChecks.byModelkind` and `ThingkindChecks.ofLabelpath`, and give the fuller bundles. Same entry points; the package is private, and nothing outside packages/safebyok read them.
  - **The three bundles are no longer exported from shapes-thing.ts**, only from shapes-thingkinds.ts. Entry points unchanged.
  - Nothing else: no check accepts or refuses anything new; nothing stored changes.
* **Discoveries**:
  - STYLE.md's bundle text ("A kind's checks are read through its kind's bundle (`BoxChecks.sealable`)") names no module, so it stands, and now covers the stamps too. VOCABULARY.md's one row naming a bundle (`GateChecks.cited`, under **cited**) is still right.
  - `shapes-gateish.ts` now imports shapes-thingkinds for its two stamp reads; it is imported only as types by the models, so no cycle.
