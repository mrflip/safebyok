## 1. Eyebrows, ranked

**1. attemptUnlock answers the same wrong passphrase two ways, by timing.** `bank.ts:269` `if (this.isEnabled && (open?.bank === this) && (open.gatingkind !== 'unlock_bank'))`, then `:272` `return attemptWithoutGating(this, cleartexts, () => Promise.resolve(this))`. With a gate change open, attemptUnlock waits it out and, if the bank is then unlocked, resolves ok without looking at the cleartexts; on an unlocked bank with no gating it derives and checks. Probe: on an unlocked bank with no gating, a wrong passphrase came back `wrong_passphrase`; called while a replace gating was open and then landed, the same wrong passphrase came back `ok`. `bank-prompts.test.ts:600` pins the second case on purpose. *Origin:* new, from the ruling "waits for it to conclude, then checks unlocked again", applied to the re-entry point. *Cost:* two answers to one question on the same bank state; a form's answer depends on a crossing. *Flavour:* guidance gap (the ruling was written for prompts). *Settle:* decide once what attemptUnlock answers on an already unlocked bank, on both paths; if a form's answer never waits, the branch goes.

**2. A refused birth takes over the Device's lock, sweep and snapshot.** `bank.ts:113` `device.cheatHoldBank(this)` in the Bank constructor; `bank.ts:355` `const bank = this.make(record, bankOpts)` in `#birth`, before `tryBirthBank`. Every Bank.make rewires the Device, including a birth the vaultDB refuses. Probe: with born bank B locked, a refused birth (`gate_made_elsewhere`) left `device.getSnapshot().bank` no longer matching B; after B unlocked, the device snapshot still read `locked`; `device.lock()` left B unlocked. *Origin:* the fudge is ruled; holding on construction is new. *Cost:* the Device follows whichever Bank was built last; an earlier Bank's lease escapes `device.sweep()`. *Flavour:* solution off, inside a ruled fudge. *Settle:* whether the fudge should hold a bank only once known good (a birth that landed, a bank made for use); startup replaces it either way.

**3. gatingseq is drafted as a guard, used only as a remount key.** `device.ts:149,157` `this.#gatingseq += 1`; the only consumer is `knock-knock.test.tsx:70` `<UnlockForm key={gating.gatingseq} …>`. The draft (U) gave it a job: `device.unlock(gatingScope, { passphrase })` refusing a form shown for another request. No such entry point exists: forms answer through the Bank's re-entry verbs, where bank identity scopes the user and the verb scopes the kind, so a stale unlock form can only answer another unlock gating, which is harmless. *Origin:* the counter is drawn; the scope-checked entry point was replaced by Bank verbs this sprint. *Cost:* small; the draft's guarantee isn't delivered and no test notices. *Flavour:* guidance wrong given the code. *Settle:* agree a remount key is the whole job and strike the gatingScope paragraph, or want `device.unlock(gatingScope)` after all.

**4. V2 is met through the watch contract, not minder.tryWrite.** `bank.ts:167` `this.#vaultDB.tryWriteThing({ ...this.makePutAsk(ask), labelpath: 'bank', … })` and `:379` (`#putGate`) write past the minder; `vault.test.ts:390-409` pins why that works (every AdapterVaultDB notifies watches of bank, gate and root records before the write's answer returns). *Origin:* new, contradicting "Settled (V2)" on purpose, reasoning written beside the test. *Cost:* none in code; the guarantee rests on every adapter keeping the watch ordering and the minder having said hello. *Flavour:* guidance wrong given the code. *Settle:* accept the watch contract and strike V2, or insist the Bank reach a minder.

**5. Background refresh exists twice.** `thing-minder.ts:208` `refresh(scoped, opts)` sends refusals to tellBadNews unless silenceErrors; `thing-minder.ts:180` `tryAt(..., { ifStale: 'refresh' })` → `:325` `#backgroundRefresh` drops refusals into the cache as lastErr. refresh's non-silenced mode and its staleTime option have no production caller. *Origin:* refresh with silenceErrors is your withWhisper ruling; its other mode is new. *Cost:* two background-fetch paths with different error policies. *Flavour:* guidance gap (the ruling named refresh without noticing tryAt's path). *Settle:* could withWhisper call `tryAt(scope, { ifStale: 'refresh' })`? If so, refresh can go.

**6. useBankSnapshot(select) hand-rolls what the draft gave a library.** `react-giver.ts:108-110` `useSyncExternalStore(device.subscribe, () => pick(device.getSnapshot().bank ?? PendingBank), …)`, with a doc block warning select must never return a new object. dream-interface-2's Notes name `useSyncExternalStoreWithSelector` from `use-sync-external-store`, as Redux and Zustand use, so a selector can build an object; that package isn't a dependency. A select returning a new object makes plain useSyncExternalStore loop, guarded only by the doc. *Origin:* new. *Cost:* a sharp edge for every host; library-first not followed. *Flavour:* solution off. *Settle:* whether the one dependency is worth it now; the draft said yes.

**7. Tests whose subject is a crossing, and code that serves them.** `bank-prompts.test.ts:505`, `:660` (deleted in #779), `:674` "resolves 'disabled' at once on a swept bank, without waiting out the gate change still open on it", `:645` "answers the unlock gating opened by a prompt that waited out the same gate change", `:633` "waits out a dismissed gate change, then refuses a wrong passphrase directly", and `vault.test.ts` "on a locked bank, tells no bad news of a storage refusal met during the unlock" (which spies on the adapter's private doGetThing through `as unknown as`). For them: the `this.isEnabled &&` guard at `bank.ts:269` and the `demandEnabled()` after the await at `bank.ts:201`. *Origin:* new. *Cost:* about six tests and two branches whose subject is "X ended or locked meanwhile". *Flavour:* solution off, or a guidance gap (a ruling that adds waits invites tests of what happens during them). *Settle:* drop those tests and see which branches lose all coverage; those are candidates to go.

**8. withWhisper asks for the passphrase before it says there is no box.** `vault.ts:81-84`: refresh → `await this.#bank.ensureUnlocked()` → get → `throw Throwers.boxAbsent`; pinned by "on a locked bank, asks the user before it refuses 'absent\_box' for a box never stored". The background refresh usually knows the box is void before the dialog closes. *Origin:* drawn (Wh: "unlocks first"). *Cost:* UX only. *Flavour:* can't tell. *Settle:* whether absent should be checked from a timely car before the unlock.

**9. The giver-device fudge lives in three places.** `knock-knock-wor``ld.ts:77` `_cheat_giverDeviceOf` (new); `minder-giver-world.ts:91` `minderDeviceOf` (carried, reshaped, nearly the same body); `fake-giver-world.ts:118` (a whole fake Device). **10. **Evidence for an open finding (the bridge loop).** `react-bridge.ts:190-195` `deliverEvent` never reads `event.action.type`; a minder error event carries the unchanged bhnd car, so `dbHasNewer(car)` invalidates again: host refetch → minder.get refused → error event → invalidate. ## 2. Undergrowth

**Control primitives**

- **#gatingseq counter.** `device.ts:44` `#gatingseq = 0`, bumped in attachGating and detachGating. Drawn; see eyebrow 3.
- **Bank→Device subscription.** `device.ts:48` `#unsubscribeBank: () => void = () => undefined`, set in cheatHoldBank (`:125`, `bank.subscribe(this.#align)`). New, serving the fudge: every bank change runs two snapshot rebuilds and a deep isEqual. *Settle:* startup's Device-owns-bank design.
- **Joins on a stored promise.** `bank.ts:200,252,271` `await open.whenConcluded`. The promise lives in Gating (carried); the three waits are drawn by the gating ruling; the re-entry wait sits in a primitive's critical path (eyebrow 1).
- **Callback into the Device.** `gating.ts:120,140` `this.#device.alignOpenGating()`, a public Device method marked Internal, so the snapshot can show isAttempting (drawn need, plain solution).
- **Fire-and-forget refresh.** `thing-minder.ts:211` `void this.get(scope, { staleTime }).catch(...)`. Drawn by the ruling; see eyebrow 5.
- **Not present.** No new timers, abort controllers, retries, queues or generation tokens in src. The bridge's stopsListening Set and the minder's #isReady flag are carried.

**New mechanisms**

- **The re-entry nexus on the Bank.** The four attempt verbs → `#handIn` (`bank.ts:302`) → `attemptWithoutGating` (`gating.ts:249`). New; the draft had `device.unlock` and `device.prompt`. Four verbs, two dispatch paths each, plus the unlock's extra wait. *Flavour:* redesign? The Bank verbs dissolve the draft's gatingScope (eyebrow 3). *Settle:* agree the Bank verbs are the answering surface, and update U.
- **Four #…With helpers.** `#unlockBankWith`, `#replacePassphraseWith`, `#addPasskeyWith`, `#revokePasskeyWith` (`bank.ts:313-345`), three building identical friend objects. Debugging an unlock now goes launchUnlockBankPrompt → #launchOrJoin(kind, closure) → Gating → closure → #unlockBankWith → friend → unlockBank → friend.unlockByProving. *Flavour:* fails the over-DRY debug test, mildly.
- **The Vault class.** `vault.ts`, drawn (V). Several Vaults of one bank make several independent minders; tests use that to stand in for another tab. Who makes Vaults is a startup question.
- **Key builders, static and instance.** Static `ThingMinder.queryKeyOf`, `bankQueryKeyOf`, `isThingEvent` (`thing-minder.ts:424-436`) beside instance `queryKey(labelpathed)`, which ignores the scope's userid and banklabel: two builders that disagree for another bank's scope. *Settle:* whether the instance builders call the static one with this.scope.
- **shapes-giver.ts.** A split-out of the giver's types and checks; no new behaviour.

**Non-standard JavaScript**

- **Whole bodies in a functional wrapper.** tryGet and tryList put their entire body in `Outcome.outcomeOf(async () => { … })`, and get and list are `Outcome.valOf(await this.tryGet(...))`: a throw → catch → rethrow round trip. New; also the root of "misuse as a bad outcome".\
Use try/catch in the try method. The valOf is sound.
- **A do-nothing attempt passed to spend cleartexts.** `bank.ts:272` `() => Promise.resolve(this)` handed to attemptWithoutGating so the cleartexts are zeroed and a good outcome returns. Code-golf flavour; eyebrow 1 is the real issue.
- **Closures for later calling.** Each launch verb passes `(cleartexts) => this.#xWith(cleartexts)` into #launchOrJoin or #handIn. See the nexus.
- **Freezing arguments in place.** `thing-car.ts:44-47` `frozenCarOf` deep-freezes records a vaultDB answered with; drawn ("deep-frozen as they land"), documented at `react-giver.ts:73`.

Bottom line: little feared machinery. What exists is mostly drawn by the draft or by rulings; the exceptions are the re-entry nexus with its helper layer, and the subscription the fudge needs.
