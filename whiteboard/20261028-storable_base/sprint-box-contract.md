# Sprint: box fills its contract

Issued 2026-10-30 by the Coach, for an `opus` orchestrator running `/sprint`. Nothing here is about sealing or unsealing whispers.

## Goal

`packages/safebyok/src/models/` holds the storable redesign as a playground: `Storable`, `Listing`, `Box`, `BoxListing`, and `models-shapes.ts`, following `whiteboard/20261028-storable_base/storable-spec.md`. It typechecks and lints, and it has been exercised only by throwaway tests, since deleted. This sprint makes the box fill its contract, the verbs, guards, snapshot, listing and cache the spec and the core README promise, and finds out, by testing against that contract, which guarantees this simple model provides. The seal is not part of it: `Box.makeStaged` with a whisper and `Box.#unseal` refuse `unexpected_error` today, and they still do when the sprint ends.

## Ground rules

1. **Discipline about surface.** No new public or protected method, getter or option on `Storable`, `Listing`, `Box` or `BoxListing` unless a thread below names it. A worker that believes one is needed writes the case in its thread file under *Proposed surface* and works around it in the test instead. The same for complexity: no retry, no scheduling, no abort controllers, no policy knobs. The library offers simple brittle primitives (spec, *Please Remember!!*).
2. **Deviations from the old guarantees are findings, not work.** The model will not provide everything `notes/guarantees.md` and the old `src/box.ts` provided. A thread that meets one records it in its thread file under *Guarantees touched*, with the line of the guarantee and the behaviour observed, and moves on. Nothing is changed to restore a guarantee for its own sake; thread 6 gathers them for the Coach.
3. **Nothing about sealing or unsealing.** Tests of `edit` and `send` use sidecar-only patches over a full record, where `makeStaged` carries the sealed parts forward. A test may assert that a whisper patch refuses `unexpected_error`, and no more. `unsealWhisper`, `expose`, `reseal`, `seal` and the `whisper` accessor are out of scope except where thread 4 says.
4. **Spec first, then code.** A fix to the code is made only where the code plainly deviates from the spec or from its own doc table, and the fix is mechanical. A disagreement that needs judgment goes to the thread file and *For the Coach*; the test for that row is written to the spec and marked `it.todo` with the question.
5. **Tests.** They live in `packages/safebyok/tests/models/`, one file per thread's subject, named for the file under test (`storable.test.ts`, `listing.test.ts`, `box.test.ts`, `box-listing.test.ts`, `models-shapes.test.ts`), each thread adding to a file rather than making a parallel one. Shared fakes live in `packages/safebyok/tests/test-helpers/models-world.ts`, built by thread 1. STYLE.md *Tests* governs shape and naming. No test imports the old `src/box.ts` or `src/vault.ts`.
6. **The split personality is left alone.** The models' `BoxFormat` is the number 5001 in `fmt`; the stored box of today carries the string `safebyok-box-v8` in `format`, in `src/shapes/box.ts`. The two coexist until a later match-up thread. No conversion, no shim, no shared constant.
7. **Scratch patterns that worked**, for thread 1 to lift: a fake `BoxStore` as a closure over one `stored` record, answering `{ scope, ok: true, val, world: null }` from `get`, recording `put` and `del` calls with their `expected` revision, and answering `del` with a tombstone at the next revision; a fake `BoxCache` recording `save` calls and answering `load` from an array; a `BoxWorld` with a settable clock starting at `T0 = 1_700_000_000_000` (the epoch check refuses small numbers), `lockState` settable, `ensureTimely` and `takeWorld` recording calls; a full record whose sealed parts are the field kit's ciphered bags, `{ cipher_iv_b64, ciphertext_b64 }`; labels of two characters or more.
8. Every thread runs `pnpm --filter safebyok test`, typecheck and lint on its files as it goes, and `pnpm justify` before filing. Report each `@ts-expect-error` and `eslint-disable` added; the `eslint-disable-line sonarjs/cognitive-complexity` on `reconcileCentral` is the Coach's and approved.

## For the Coach

* The seal's place in `edit` (the `fill` and `adopt` merge re-mints a revision without resealing) is out of this sprint and will surface in thread 2's findings; thread 6 lists the rows that wait on it.
* `ifAbsent` on `fetch` and `ifPresent` on `send` are documented as not yet acted on. Thread 3 tests the documented behaviour (a thing not stored resolves null) and no policy.
* A staged edit surpassed by a received update follows the simple rule the Coach set on 2026-10-30: a healthy record drops it, a damaged one carries it to the next revision. The spec's *Receive* principles ("don't throw away customer data") say otherwise; thread 2 tests the rule, not the principle, and thread 6 lists the principle as changed.
* Threads 2 through 5 are independent of each other and of thread 7; the orchestrator may run three at once after thread 1 files.
