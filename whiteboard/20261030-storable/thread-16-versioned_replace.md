# Thread 16: the store's versioned replace (2026-10-31)

Branch `20261031-versioned_replace`, PR #PR, stacked on #387. Justify: green (safebyok 4322 passed, 37 skipped; every package green, size budgets unmoved).

* **Built**:
  * **The contract** (`src/models/models-shapes.ts`, the `Store` type and its doc block): `put(record, priorTok)` and `del(scope, priorTok)`, `priorTok` the `revision_tok` of the record the device holds, null for none; no prior seq is sent. The doc block states the cases in the order storage judges them: (1) the pairing, judged from the request alone: `priorTok` null with a seq other than 0, or a `priorTok` with a seq below 1, refuses **`bad_record`**; (2) no row: `priorTok` null lands, a `priorTok` refuses `stale_write` carrying nothing; (3) a row under another tok (any row, for `priorTok` null; a tombstone is a row): `stale_write` carrying it; (4) the row under `priorTok`: lands only at its seq + 1, which storage computes or queries; any other seq refuses `bad_record`. `del`: same tok rule, the tombstone at the row's seq + 1 under a fresh tok.
  * **The device** (`src/models/listing.ts`, `storable.ts`): `Storable.expectedRevision()` is now `priorTok()` (central's tok, null for a thing never stored); `Listing.expectedRevisionOf(scope)` is now `priorTokOf(scope)`. `listing.put` checks the pairing with a private helper `fitsPriorTok(revision_seq, priorTok)` in the listing's *Helpers* banner before it calls the store, refusing `bad_record` (`Phrases.PutUnfitForPrior`, new in `src/phrases.ts`, shared with the fake). `listing.del` has no record, so no pairing check. The `send` doc says what a put lands over.
  * **The fake store** (`tests/test-helpers/models-world.ts`): `put` and `del` enforce every case above in place; a `casRefusalOf(scope, held, priorTok)` closure holds the tok rule they share. The call log and `willPut`/`willDel` carry `priorTok` in place of `expected`.
  * **Tests**: appended to `tests/models/listing.test.ts`, under the banner *The versioned replace*: `PutCells` (13 cells, every cell of the `'replace'` row, the inconsistent column split into its three causes, over no row and over a row), `DelLandsCells`/`DelRefusedCells` (7), each checking the store kept or left its row; and `listing.put and listing.del: the prior tok they send` (6): the device's pairing refusals before any call, a record past seq + 1 sent and refused by storage, the send that lands, `stale_write` carrying nothing from a put and a del, and the stored revision lower than the one held.
  * **The spec** (`whiteboard/20261028-storable_base/storable-spec.md`, the `ifPresent` entry): restated in these terms, with the Coach's table; the `'replace'` row's last cell filled with `bad_record`, the other ❓ and 🤷 left.
* **Decisions taken**:
  * **The inconsistent kind is `bad_record`**, "a record or expectation the host handed in that is not one this package builds". The others surveyed: `corrupt_record` blames the adapter's answer, not the request; `unexpected_error` is a bug in safebyok, but `listing.put(record)` takes a host-handed record, so the host can make the request; `invalid_box` belongs to the old VaultDB's `putBox`; `record_conflict` and `stale_write` say storage changed, which it did not. For a Coach row.
  * **The pairing is judged before the tok**, so `priorTok` null with seq 2 over a row is `bad_record`, not `stale_write`: the request is wrong whatever is stored. The seq + 1 rule can only be judged over the row under `priorTok`, so a mis-numbered put over another tok's row is `stale_write`.
  * **A `priorTok` with a negative seq** is unpaired too (the device rule "a tok with seq > 0").
  * **`del` with `priorTok` null over no row**, kept as it was: it lands a tombstone at revision 0 (`NeverStoredSeq` + 1) under a fresh tok. Tested ("lands a tombstone at revision 0 over no row, with no prior tok, as it always has").
  * **The device checks the pairing only**, as the plan says, not seq = central's + 1. So `listing.put(box)` of a box with nothing staged sends central's own seq over central's tok and storage refuses `bad_record`; tested as "sends a record past central's seq + 1". The check is a line away if the Coach wants it on the device too.
* **For the Coach**:
  * **A revision lower than expected** (storage holds an older row, under another tok, than the device's central) meets the refusal of today, `stale_write` carrying it. The device delivers it, judges it **older**, keeps its own central, and is stuck: every later send meets the same `stale_write` (tested: "meets a stored revision lower than the one held...", two sends, central at 5 throughout).
  * The kind for the inconsistent cell (`bad_record`), above.
* **Discoveries**:
  * **Seed fixes, no assertion's subject changed.** 25 tests put or deleted over a record they had built but never stored, or put a record at seq 1 with nothing held. `listing.test.ts`: the `edited()` helper (three put tests) seeds the store; "puts a record by the same path" seeds; "expects no revision of a box not held" puts at revision 0; the four `listing.del(duck)` tests ("deletes a box at central's revision", "deletes at the revision of a staged tombstone's central", "deletes a sparse record's box the same way", "saves the tombstone to the cache once") seed; "resolves {a fetch, a put, a del, a build(record)} whose save is refused" takes a stored record per case, the put case putting a record at revision 2 over the stored 1 in place of `put(box)`; "makes each scope's instance once" seeds `built` and puts it at revision 2 in place of `put(box)`; "takes a box in when it comes back to life" seeds in place of a scripted `get`; "refuses a malformed answer to a put", "refuses it for a stale_write refusal's stored record" (vault_tok), "refuses it for a put and a del answered so" (scope), "refuses it for a put and a del answered for another scope", and the two "makes the fake store refuse 'stale_write' ... when none was expected" put at revision 0. `box-listing.test.ts`: "does not judge a put's or a del's answer" seeds; "delivers box_stored to a box not yet held" puts at revision 0. `box.test.ts`: the two rebirth tests ("stages a {deleted, damaged} box alive...") seed the tombstone; "stay nine through a receive, an edit and a del" seeds the record received. `loosened.test.ts`: the two "registered_at over a rebirth" tests (VOCABULARY.md:153, README.md:1282) seed. `storable.test.ts`: "notifies on an edit and on a del", "has no sidecar on the sparse form nor on a tombstone", "deletes a damaged thing at the ouchstone's revision" seed. Two `willDel` scripts there computed the tombstone's seq from `expected.revision_seq`; they now give it as 2.
  * **Tests could pass for the wrong reason**: those that expect `bad_record` from the scope guard or a malformed answer, and the device's new pairing refusal can give it first. Found by running the suite with the pairing kind briefly set to `crypto_failed`: every test that hit the check failed, and the ones expecting `bad_record` were fixed to put at revision 0. A trick worth reusing when a new refusal shares a kind with old ones.
  * The `stale_write` carrying nothing uses `Refusals.StaleBoxMessage`, which says "What is stored now is this error's meta.box_stored" though `meta` is absent; today's path for it (`staleWriteOf(scope, undefined)`) was already so.
