* **Decisions made**
  * **Why written_at.** The bank table admits few damages the bank's checks refuse: `label` is CHECKed to `'bank'`, `fmt` and the citation fields must match the ask for the write to go through, `born_at` must equal the proposed record's, and `lifetok` is the one field the title rules out. `written_at` is compared by nothing in a write.
  * **Store, as words.** At rest, a vaultDB or deviceDB "has" a row (VOCABULARY's vaultDB and deviceDB rows say "has"); a deviceDB test that calls `stowLease` "stows"; a test cheat "plants". Code names stay: `StoreLimits`, `storeLimitsOf`, `StoreLimitCap`, `StoreLimitFloor`, `StoreQuotaTooSmall`, `storeQuotaTooSmall`, and IndexedDB's `store` parameters. The section banners `[Storing]` and `[Storing Throwers]` are `[Storage]` and `[Storage Throwers]`.
  * **Two messages reworded**, words only: `BankRootsMissing` ends "the datastore needs mending by hand", and Convex's `SidecarKeyRefused` says "is one Convex refuses". Tests read both through their phrase constants.
