Proved: safebyok-graphql 305 tests (its battery over memory and Postgres, 228, among them), typecheck and lint green; `pnpm justify` green after the catch-up onto #946's tip: safebyok 2,446, safebyok-graphql 305, safebyok-postgres 177, e2e 576.

* **Built**
  * **`GraphQLVaultDB` is a pipe** (`packages/safebyok-graphql/src/graphql-vault-db.ts`). `implements DB.VaultDB`, no longer `extends DB.AdapterVaultDB`, as big's `GraphQLBankDB implements BX.BankDB`. Each `try*` verb: check the ask (`AskChecks.*`, `checkedWriteAsk`, `checkedBirthBankAsk`), send it, parse the answer by its form (`WireChecks.<verb>Answer`), check where its Things sit (`check*At`), answer it (`answerOf`). Every throw lands in `answerForThrown`, which answers as `AdapterVaultDB.answerForThrown` does: `storageRefusalOf`, meta `oplabel` and loggable ask, act the refusal's own meta act else `unclassified_storing` (the battery's `dubdel` needs that), dbhas null. `answerOf` restores the core's answer: the loggable ask, a refusal made a VaultError again (`refusedOverWire(err, { act, dbhas })`, its meta's `ask` set again), the null `dbhas` a good answer crosses with dropped, every record deeply frozen (`DB.frozenAnswerOf`). `watch` checks its args (`AskChecks.watchArgs`) as `AdapterVaultDB.watch` does; each notice is parsed, checked to be in the watch's bank, made an item event with `DB.noticeOf`, and handed to `onNotice` under a try that tells what it throws to `onBadNews`.
  * **Wire checks** (`src/shapes/shapes-wire.ts`). `WireChecks` reads each verb's answer on the core's answer shapes: per act, `val` and `dbhas` as that act has them and `null` where it has none (`gotlive` val, `gotgone`/`gotvoid` dbhas), or any refused one; every record by `SeenChecks.seenCited` (scope and citation, kept whole); tracked as `'same'` or a record per root, `'unread'` only when refused; kids by `CrossedChecks.kids`; incompatible rows by `CrossedChecks.incompatibleThing`; stats by `VaultStatsChecks.vaultStats`. All strict.
  * **Light consistency checks**, named steps in the same file, each refusing `storage_issue`: `checkGotAt` (a get's or write's `val ?? dbhas` at the ask's scope), `checkListedAt` (parent, or refused dbhas, at the ask's scope; each delta and incompatible row in the bank and one label under it), `checkRootsAt` (birth/world roots each at its own labelpath; a refusal's dbhas a root of the bank), `checkStatsAt`, `checkWatchedAt` (bank scope), all with `checkTrackedAt` (each non-`'same'` tracked root at its labelpath). Scope mismatches refuse through `thingScope`/`bankScope.parseConsistent`; a child outside its parent or a non-root refusal Thing through the new thrower `thingOutOfReach` (`refusals/throwers.ts`, phrase `ThingOutOfReach`).
  * **Wire rename**: `damaged_rows` → `incompatible_rows`, `SafebyokDamagedThing` → `SafebyokIncompatibleThing` (schema, operations, resolvers' refused answer). No other wire change. `vaultResolvers` is untouched beyond that: any `VaultDB`, `try*`, `getUserId`, `crossedErrOf`.
  * **Core** (`packages/safebyok`): exports `frozenAnswerOf` and `noticeOf` (from `adapter-vault-db.ts`; `noticeOf` now deep-freezes its record, and `AdapterVaultDB.watch` no longer does so itself), `SeenChecks` and `loggableAskOf`. `CrossedChecks` keeps `err` and `answered`, renames `damagedThing` → `incompatibleThing`, gains `kids` (was `listing.val`), and loses `tracked`, `roots` and `listing`, which only the classifier read. `crossedErrOf` and `refusedOverWire` stay. No change to the datastore contract.
  * **Tests** (`safebyok-graphql/tests`). `graphql-vault-db.test.ts`: the act-fit tests are gone; new: an answer as served (no null dbhas), a refusal with its act, Thing and meta ask, deep freezing, an ask refused unsent, and `MisanswerCases`, a table (root field, ask, alteration, blurb) of 24 misanswers of form or of place, each first checked to pass unaltered. Watch: a notice of another bank as bad news, a throwing `onNotice`, a frozen notice with its banktok and read_tc, a bad scope refused before subscribing. `vault-schema.test.ts` drops `TypeChecks`: the answer checks now read the typed parts themselves. Batteries unchanged, green over memory and Postgres.

* **Decisions made**
  * **Records checked by scope and citation only**, not by their kind's full check. `shapes-answers.ts` already says a reader checks each record where a Thing takes it, and the VaultMinder's Things do. The scope check is what keeps `VaultMinder.getOnce`'s `this.carAt(scoped)!` safe.
  * **"Of the ask's kind" falls out of the labelpath**: the ask's own check ties its modelkind to its labelpath, and a record at the ask's labelpath is of that kind. No separate kind step.
  * **A refused birth's dbhas is checked as "a root of the bank"**, not as the root its act names: naming which root would re-derive the act.
  * **The verbs cast their answer** (`answerOf(...) as DB.GetThingAnswer`): the form check let through only that verb's acts, and records are typed as the answer has them without their bodies checked. One `as unknown as AnyAnswer` inside `answerOf`, and two record casts (`refusedOverWire`'s dbhas, a notice's record), each commented. Set aside: a generic `answerOf<AnswerT>` (the linter's `no-unnecessary-type-parameters` flags it as a disguised cast), and `custom<RecordT>()` per field (typed lies in a dozen places, and Zod's messages lost).
  * **No `refusedAnswerOf` export**: tried for `answerForThrown`, but its act is always `unclassified_storing`, where an AdapterVaultDB answers the refusal's own act.
* **Deviations**
  * **"The throwing verb throws its refusal"** in the handoff: the new core's `VaultDB` has no throwing verbs (only `try*` and `watch`); the throwing ones are the VaultMinder's (`getOnce`, `putOnce`...), which throw `answer.err`. So there is nothing to match beyond `try*`.
  * **Behaviour a working server never shows, changed for a broken one**: an act out of step with its Things is answered as the server answered it (the classifier refused it `storage_issue`); a non-refusal thrown in a verb is `storage_unavailable`, as it was through `AdapterVaultDB`.
* **Discoveries**
  * `checkChildOfKind` cannot fire for a valid ask: `thingScope`'s labelpath check (in `WireChecks`) already refuses a labelpath too deep for its kind, and a child one label under the ask's labelpath has the ask's modelkind. Its `MisanswerCases` row (a child too deep) is refused by the form check, not by this step; the other three new rows were verified to fail with their step removed.
  * A birth over a born bank answers `retcon`, not `passed`, with the battery's fixtures.
  * `answerForThrown` must carry the refusal's meta act: `checkedWriteAsk` refuses a del citing a tombstone with act `dubdel` before anything is sent.
* **For the Coach**
  * The trust story moved: the browser checks a server's answer by form and place, not by act. `notes/security.md` has it.
* **Looked up**: nothing in the plan or progress documents; the handoff sufficed.
